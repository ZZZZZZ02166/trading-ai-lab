# Absorption

- **DOM Displays Resting Depth at Each Price**. The DOM shows current resting limit orders around the market. Aggressive trades can consume liquidity at the best bid or ask, but resting quotes can change or disappear. Forthmann uses executed flow and price response alongside displayed depth. [PDF pp. 64–66](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=64)
- **Absorption Appears as Execution Without Progress**. Strong buy-initiated executions can hit passive selling while price fails to establish a new high. The mirrored case has heavy selling with a defended low. Forthmann compares price and delta to infer this condition, with replenishing liquidity as one explanation. [PDF pp. 78–81](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=78)
- **Icebergs Are an Inference From Repeated Fills**. Hidden or replenishing liquidity can produce many executions at one level without sustained movement. Displayed DOM depth alone cannot reveal total hidden interest or the owner. The useful observation is persistent execution with a disproportionate or absent price response. [PDF pp. 80–84](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=80)
