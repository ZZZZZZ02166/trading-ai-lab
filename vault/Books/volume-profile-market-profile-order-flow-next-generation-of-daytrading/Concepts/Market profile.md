# Market profile

- **Use Profiles for Location and Flow for Timing**. Forthmann starts with recent auction context, uses profiles to narrow the relevant price area, then inspects executions for timing. Market profile and volume profile address time and volume acceptance respectively. The combined method asks what happens as the reference is reached. [PDF pp. 9–13](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=9)
- **TPO Measures Time at Price**. A market profile marks prices visited in successive time brackets, illustrated with half-hour letters. Wider rows show repeated visits across brackets. Its control price reflects the most TPO activity, which can differ from the price with the most executed volume. [PDF pp. 19–21](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=19)
- **Initial Balance Anchors Opening Range Extension**. The first opening brackets define an initial balance, often the first two half-hour brackets in the illustration. Trade beyond that range is an extension. Forthmann treats a relatively narrow balance as a reason to watch for expansion, and the active instrument and session matter. [PDF pp. 20–21](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=20)
- **Single Prints Record a Brief Visit**. A single-print price row belongs to just one TPO bracket. A single-column extension at a profile extreme is a buying or selling tail in the book's terminology. This locates brief acceptance or rejection to investigate on a revisit. [PDF pp. 20–21](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=20)
- **Double Distributions Have Two Acceptance Regions**. A double distribution occurs when the market spends time or trades heavily in one region, moves through a thinner transition, and builds a second concentration. It gives two potential reference zones and a transition area. The later open and subsequent activity help tell which zone matters. [PDF pp. 32–33](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=32)
- **Anchor a Flexible Profile to a Defined Auction**. Flexible profiles can cover a specific trend, rotation, or multi-day range. The start and end must correspond to a recognizable event or trade window. Using the same range for TPO and volume makes differences in acceptance easier to interpret. [PDF pp. 38–44](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=38)
- **Range Auction Can Show Two Control Prices**. Forthmann profiles a defined range using both market and volume views. A TPO POC shows where time concentrated and VPOC shows where executions concentrated. Divergence identifies two reference prices to carry into the subsequent session. [PDF pp. 40–43](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=40)
- **A Weak Extreme Can Advertise a Later Sweep**. A weak high or low may consist of closely repeated prices with little decisive rejection. Forthmann treats that structure as a possible target for a later sweep. This chart-based usage of unfinished auction is distinct from a zero-quantity test inside a single footprint. [PDF pp. 58–60](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=58)
- **An Opening Drive Depends on the Prior Auction**. Forthmann distinguishes directional opening initiative from a test or rotation. An early push following consolidation can carry a different meaning from a push after several already extended sessions. Earlier tails, value and the new auction provide reference points. [PDF pp. 108–112](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=108)
- **Use Profile Tails to Frame False Breaks**. The crude and E-mini examples establish a short opening range, show a break that fails, then use lower-timeframe footprints to assess the response. Existing single-print tails and value structure give the probe a defined location. A subsequent stack is interpreted in the context of that sequence. [PDF pp. 113–117](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=113)
