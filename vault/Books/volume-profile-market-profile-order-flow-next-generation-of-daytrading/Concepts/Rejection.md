# Rejection

- **Single Prints Record a Brief Visit**. A single-print price row belongs to just one TPO bracket. A single-column extension at a profile extreme is a buying or selling tail in the book's terminology. This locates brief acceptance or rejection to investigate on a revisit. [PDF pp. 20–21](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=20)
- **Low-Volume Nodes Mark Thin Price Regions**. Low-volume nodes are local indentations in a volume profile. They can appear between more accepted areas or along a fast directional move. Forthmann watches for either a reaction at a boundary or rapid passage through a thin region. [PDF p. 27](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=27)
- **A Profile Bounce Can Start Before the Line**. Forthmann treats strong volume and thin-profile transitions as zones. A retest may turn ahead of the original activity, react inside the band, or go through it. He uses the current response to distinguish these possibilities. [PDF pp. 52–54](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=52)
- **Broadening Swings Need a Confirmed Failure**. The book's broadening-top example marks expanding highs and lows and waits for a completed structural failure. The larger shape serves as warning context, with the subsequent failed move providing more concrete evidence. Flexible profiles map reference areas around it. [PDF pp. 123–127](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=123)
- **Evaluate a False Break by Its Return**. Forthmann describes popular breakouts and reversal formations that fail, leaving traders positioned in the wrong direction. Confirmation comes from rejection, reentry, and a subsequent attempt in the opposite direction. The recognizable formation helps define where a test occurred. [PDF pp. 166–177](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=166)
- **Track Prior and Overnight Extremes for Sweeps**. The book shows squeezes and prior-day or non-institutional highs and lows getting taken during the main session. The useful observation is how price responds after the extreme is reached, with profile and order flow supplying context. A sweep by itself only identifies the path through the level. [PDF pp. 178–193](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=178)
