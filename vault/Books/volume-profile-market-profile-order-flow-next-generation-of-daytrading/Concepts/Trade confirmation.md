# Trade confirmation

- **Use Profiles for Location and Flow for Timing**. Forthmann starts with recent auction context, uses profiles to narrow the relevant price area, then inspects executions for timing. Market profile and volume profile address time and volume acceptance respectively. The combined method asks what happens as the reference is reached. [PDF pp. 9–13](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=9)
- **Tape Speed Is an Alert to Read Context**. The book uses large-print and speed-of-tape displays to call attention to an acceleration near a swing, reference zone, or stop run. The display alerts the trader to inspect the next price reaction. A burst can mark exhaustion or the start of a directional move. [PDF pp. 67–70](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=67)
- **Footprints Time a Move at a Known Level**. A footprint decomposes each bar into executed volume per price level and side. Forthmann looks for the sequence of imbalances around a mapped reference. His examples allow the chain to contain a break and use an illustrative ratio distinct from Dale's software default. [PDF pp. 70–73](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=70)
- **Confirm a Footprint Impulse With Price Progress**. Forthmann checks whether price and successive local control prices advance after a first stack or large execution. If an opposing print cannot move price through an extreme, the next impulse can clarify which side is gaining initiative. A preidentified reference gives the sequence context. [PDF pp. 74–77](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=74)
- **Wait for Narrow Opening Balance to Resolve**. In the Hang Seng example, a narrow range forms near the active open while delta keeps falling and price holds a higher low. The later upward VPOC crossing helps confirm resolution. The example separates the initial stall from the later directional evidence. [PDF pp. 83–85](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=83)
- **Price Sometimes Moves Before Delta Catches Up**. Forthmann shows both the standard absorption divergence and another sequence in which price has already turned while delta still lags. Compare synchronized panels, observe the next footprint imbalance, and note when the disagreement resolves. The ordering matters for any replay study. [PDF pp. 86–90](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=86)
- **Work From Higher-Timeframe Location to Footprint**. The yen and E-mini studies first locate price around an established profile or recent extreme. A smaller price/delta view then shows whether aggression gains progress, and the footprint checks for a later initiative print or imbalance. The final entry assessment follows that order. [PDF pp. 128–136](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=128)
- **Weekly Profiles Can Supply Local Reference Levels**. Forthmann projects a weekly VPOC onto a faster crude chart to observe the next interaction. The gold and EUR/USD examples similarly use earlier structure before zooming into delta and footprints. The higher-timeframe reference and lower-timeframe trigger are separate inputs. [PDF pp. 149–156](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=149)
- **Treat a Trend as Swing Progression**. Forthmann discusses preceding swing highs and lows, closes beyond them, and follow-up behavior to read a trend. A local visual pattern can look different across chart intervals. Sustained progress and a defended retest give the structure more context. [PDF pp. 195–198](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=195)
