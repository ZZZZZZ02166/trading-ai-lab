# Volume profile

- **Use Profiles for Location and Flow for Timing**. Forthmann starts with recent auction context, uses profiles to narrow the relevant price area, then inspects executions for timing. Market profile and volume profile address time and volume acceptance respectively. The combined method asks what happens as the reference is reached. [PDF pp. 9–13](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=9)
- **Volume Point of Control Is a Price Mode**. The VPOC is the price with the highest executed volume in the selected profile. Ordinary time-based volume shows how much traded in each time bar. A VPOC and time-based POC can therefore represent different prices in the same auction. [PDF p. 22](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=22)
- **A Naked POC Is Still Untested**. Forthmann calls a prior control price that has not yet been revisited a naked POC. Price can later return to the level during a different auction. The actual reaction can be assessed with current profile and footprint context. [PDF pp. 23–26](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=23)
- **Low-Volume Nodes Mark Thin Price Regions**. Low-volume nodes are local indentations in a volume profile. They can appear between more accepted areas or along a fast directional move. Forthmann watches for either a reaction at a boundary or rapid passage through a thin region. [PDF p. 27](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=27)
- **Value Area Contains About Seventy Percent**. Forthmann uses the approximately 70 percent of selected activity bracketed by value area high and value area low. The reference depends on the instrument, interval, and whether the distribution is volume or time based. It provides a way to compare the current open with prior acceptance. [PDF pp. 28–30](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=28)
- **P and b Shapes Need Market Context**. A P profile can show higher acceptance after a rally or a short-covering pause in a broader decline. A b shape can likewise appear in a down move or near a developing base. Forthmann looks at what came before and how the next auction behaves. [PDF pp. 31–33](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=31)
- **Compare Like Sessions Before Comparing Profiles**. Fixed profiles provide consistent daily, weekly or monthly windows. Forthmann checks main-session and 24-hour profiles separately because they can place the highest-volume node at different prices. He emphasizes recent references and uses older levels selectively. [PDF pp. 34–38](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=34)
- **Anchor a Flexible Profile to a Defined Auction**. Flexible profiles can cover a specific trend, rotation, or multi-day range. The start and end must correspond to a recognizable event or trade window. Using the same range for TPO and volume makes differences in acceptance easier to interpret. [PDF pp. 38–44](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=38)
- **Range Auction Can Show Two Control Prices**. Forthmann profiles a defined range using both market and volume views. A TPO POC shows where time concentrated and VPOC shows where executions concentrated. Divergence identifies two reference prices to carry into the subsequent session. [PDF pp. 40–43](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=40)
- **Developing POC Migration Tracks Moving Acceptance**. When new trades build sufficient size at another price, the developing volume POC can migrate. Forthmann reads sustained migration in the context of an existing move and fresh initiative. He gives less weight to an isolated shift inside an established range. [PDF pp. 45–50](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=45)
- **A Profile Bounce Can Start Before the Line**. Forthmann treats strong volume and thin-profile transitions as zones. A retest may turn ahead of the original activity, react inside the band, or go through it. He uses the current response to distinguish these possibilities. [PDF pp. 52–54](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=52)
- **Separate Accumulation From Short Follow-Up Pauses**. Forthmann draws a flexible profile over an established rotation and watches order flow if a planned reference is nearby. He distinguishes this longer formation from a shorter pause after directional initiative has already appeared. The two pictures imply different stages of an auction. [PDF pp. 55–57](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=55)
- **A Hanging Ledge Can Be a Continuation Pause**. A ledge is a compact pause inside a larger move. Forthmann profiles the ledge, examines its developing control price, and uses follow-through to distinguish continuation from an attempted reversal. A P shape can form during temporary short covering in a downtrend. [PDF pp. 118–122](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=118)
- **Examples Combine Distinct Pieces of Auction Evidence**. FDAX uses a previous VPOC with local delta and footprint evidence, Bund uses a double distribution and later buying sequence, and crude profiles successive intraday legs. Each case applies the same sequence to different market speeds. The examples illustrate the author's process on selected trades. [PDF pp. 137–147](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=137)
- **Weekly Profiles Can Supply Local Reference Levels**. Forthmann projects a weekly VPOC onto a faster crude chart to observe the next interaction. The gold and EUR/USD examples similarly use earlier structure before zooming into delta and footprints. The higher-timeframe reference and lower-timeframe trigger are separate inputs. [PDF pp. 149–156](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=149)
