# Footprints

- **Footprints Time a Move at a Known Level**. A footprint decomposes each bar into executed volume per price level and side. Forthmann looks for the sequence of imbalances around a mapped reference. His examples allow the chain to contain a break and use an illustrative ratio distinct from Dale's software default. [PDF pp. 70–73](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=70)
- **Stack Settings Depend on Platform and Instrument**. The E-mini illustration compares adjacent price rows and illustrates a two-to-one labeling example. Forthmann also allows interpretation of an imperfect stack when neighboring activity supports it. A runnable test needs to declare the ratio, adjacency, minimum size, and treatment of gaps. [PDF pp. 71–73](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=71)
- **Confirm a Footprint Impulse With Price Progress**. Forthmann checks whether price and successive local control prices advance after a first stack or large execution. If an opposing print cannot move price through an extreme, the next impulse can clarify which side is gaining initiative. A preidentified reference gives the sequence context. [PDF pp. 74–77](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=74)
- **Work From Higher-Timeframe Location to Footprint**. The yen and E-mini studies first locate price around an established profile or recent extreme. A smaller price/delta view then shows whether aggression gains progress, and the footprint checks for a later initiative print or imbalance. The final entry assessment follows that order. [PDF pp. 128–136](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=128)
- **Examples Combine Distinct Pieces of Auction Evidence**. FDAX uses a previous VPOC with local delta and footprint evidence, Bund uses a double distribution and later buying sequence, and crude profiles successive intraday legs. Each case applies the same sequence to different market speeds. The examples illustrate the author's process on selected trades.
