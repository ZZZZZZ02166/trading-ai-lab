# Trend continuation

- **Developing POC Migration Tracks Moving Acceptance**. When new trades build sufficient size at another price, the developing volume POC can migrate. Forthmann reads sustained migration in the context of an existing move and fresh initiative. He gives less weight to an isolated shift inside an established range. [PDF pp. 45–50](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=45)
- **Separate Accumulation From Short Follow-Up Pauses**. Forthmann draws a flexible profile over an established rotation and watches order flow if a planned reference is nearby. He distinguishes this longer formation from a shorter pause after directional initiative has already appeared. The two pictures imply different stages of an auction. [PDF pp. 55–57](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=55)
- **A Hanging Ledge Can Be a Continuation Pause**. A ledge is a compact pause inside a larger move. Forthmann profiles the ledge, examines its developing control price, and uses follow-through to distinguish continuation from an attempted reversal. A P shape can form during temporary short covering in a downtrend. [PDF pp. 118–122](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=118)
- **Treat a Trend as Swing Progression**. Forthmann discusses preceding swing highs and lows, closes beyond them, and follow-up behavior to read a trend. A local visual pattern can look different across chart intervals. Sustained progress and a defended retest give the structure more context. [PDF pp. 195–198](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=195)
