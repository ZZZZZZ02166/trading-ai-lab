# Low volume node

- **Low-Volume Nodes Mark Thin Price Regions**. Low-volume nodes are local indentations in a volume profile. They can appear between more accepted areas or along a fast directional move. Forthmann watches for either a reaction at a boundary or rapid passage through a thin region. [PDF p. 27](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=27)
- **Double Distributions Have Two Acceptance Regions**. A double distribution occurs when the market spends time or trades heavily in one region, moves through a thinner transition, and builds a second concentration. It gives two potential reference zones and a transition area. The later open and subsequent activity help tell which zone matters. [PDF pp. 32–33](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=32)
- **Tie Invalidation to Failed Price Progress**. The book uses structure and thinner profile zones in location and risk decisions. Failure to make expected swing progress can call the trade premise into question. Forthmann emphasizes timely exits and prepares the trade plan before the decision point. [PDF pp. 199–203](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=199)
