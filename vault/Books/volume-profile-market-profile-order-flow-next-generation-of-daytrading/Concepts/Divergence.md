# Divergence

- **Absorption Appears as Execution Without Progress**. Strong buy-initiated executions can hit passive selling while price fails to establish a new high. The mirrored case has heavy selling with a defended low. Forthmann compares price and delta to infer this condition, with replenishing liquidity as one explanation. [PDF pp. 78–81](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=78)
- **Wait for Narrow Opening Balance to Resolve**. In the Hang Seng example, a narrow range forms near the active open while delta keeps falling and price holds a higher low. The later upward VPOC crossing helps confirm resolution. The example separates the initial stall from the later directional evidence. [PDF pp. 83–85](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=83)
- **Price Sometimes Moves Before Delta Catches Up**. Forthmann shows both the standard absorption divergence and another sequence in which price has already turned while delta still lags. Compare synchronized panels, observe the next footprint imbalance, and note when the disagreement resolves. The ordering matters for any replay study.
