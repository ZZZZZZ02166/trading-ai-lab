# Liquidity sweep

- **A Weak Extreme Can Advertise a Later Sweep**. A weak high or low may consist of closely repeated prices with little decisive rejection. Forthmann treats that structure as a possible target for a later sweep. This chart-based usage of unfinished auction is distinct from a zero-quantity test inside a single footprint. [PDF pp. 58–60](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=58)
- **Prepare Active-Session References Before the Opening**. Forthmann reviews the prior main-session high and low, overnight or early-session extrema, recent value and POCs, and the scheduled release calendar. Preparation creates scenarios for the open. The source's timing examples should be interpreted for their specific instrument and session. [PDF pp. 95–100](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=95)
- **Use Profile Tails to Frame False Breaks**. The crude and E-mini examples establish a short opening range, show a break that fails, then use lower-timeframe footprints to assess the response. Existing single-print tails and value structure give the probe a defined location. A subsequent stack is interpreted in the context of that sequence. [PDF pp. 113–117](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=113)
- **Broadening Swings Need a Confirmed Failure**. The book's broadening-top example marks expanding highs and lows and waits for a completed structural failure. The larger shape serves as warning context, with the subsequent failed move providing more concrete evidence. Flexible profiles map reference areas around it. [PDF pp. 123–127](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=123)
- **Evaluate a False Break by Its Return**. Forthmann describes popular breakouts and reversal formations that fail, leaving traders positioned in the wrong direction. Confirmation comes from rejection, reentry, and a subsequent attempt in the opposite direction. The recognizable formation helps define where a test occurred. [PDF pp. 166–177](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=166)
- **Track Prior and Overnight Extremes for Sweeps**. The book shows squeezes and prior-day or non-institutional highs and lows getting taken during the main session. The useful observation is how price responds after the extreme is reached, with profile and order flow supplying context. A sweep by itself only identifies the path through the level. [PDF pp. 178–193](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=178)
- **Separate Initial Setup From Multi-Session Holding**. The final EUR/USD example begins with a local backfire setup and then shows the wider hourly path over subsequent sessions. The original evidence and the later holding decision have different horizons. It is an example of how the method scales across views. [PDF pp. 204–207](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=204)
