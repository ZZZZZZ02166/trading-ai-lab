# Initial balance

- **Initial Balance Anchors Opening Range Extension**. The first opening brackets define an initial balance, often the first two half-hour brackets in the illustration. Trade beyond that range is an extension. Forthmann treats a relatively narrow balance as a reason to watch for expansion, and the active instrument and session matter. [PDF pp. 20–21](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=20)
- **Wait for Narrow Opening Balance to Resolve**. In the Hang Seng example, a narrow range forms near the active open while delta keeps falling and price holds a higher low. The later upward VPOC crossing helps confirm resolution. The example separates the initial stall from the later directional evidence. [PDF pp. 83–85](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=83)
- **An Opening Drive Depends on the Prior Auction**. Forthmann distinguishes directional opening initiative from a test or rotation. An early push following consolidation can carry a different meaning from a push after several already extended sessions. Earlier tails, value and the new auction provide reference points. [PDF pp. 108–112](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=108)
