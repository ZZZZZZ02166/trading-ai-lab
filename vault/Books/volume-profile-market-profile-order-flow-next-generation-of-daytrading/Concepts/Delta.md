# Delta

- **Absorption Appears as Execution Without Progress**. Strong buy-initiated executions can hit passive selling while price fails to establish a new high. The mirrored case has heavy selling with a defended low. Forthmann compares price and delta to infer this condition, with replenishing liquidity as one explanation. [PDF pp. 78–81](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=78)
- **Price Sometimes Moves Before Delta Catches Up**. Forthmann shows both the standard absorption divergence and another sequence in which price has already turned while delta still lags. Compare synchronized panels, observe the next footprint imbalance, and note when the disagreement resolves. The ordering matters for any replay study. [PDF pp. 86–90](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=86)
- **Delta Has Time and Volume-at-Price Views**. The book shows signed delta by bar, CVD and delta distributed across price levels. The views can locate stretches of related aggression or a large apparent buying or selling extreme. Comparing those prints with actual price progress is part of interpreting them. [PDF pp. 91–94](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=91)
