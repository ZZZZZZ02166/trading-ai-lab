# Profile shapes

- **P and b Shapes Need Market Context**. A P profile can show higher acceptance after a rally or a short-covering pause in a broader decline. A b shape can likewise appear in a down move or near a developing base. Forthmann looks at what came before and how the next auction behaves. [PDF pp. 31–33](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=31)
- **Double Distributions Have Two Acceptance Regions**. A double distribution occurs when the market spends time or trades heavily in one region, moves through a thinner transition, and builds a second concentration. It gives two potential reference zones and a transition area. The later open and subsequent activity help tell which zone matters. [PDF pp. 32–33](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=32)
- **A Hanging Ledge Can Be a Continuation Pause**. A ledge is a compact pause inside a larger move. Forthmann profiles the ledge, examines its developing control price, and uses follow-through to distinguish continuation from an attempted reversal. A P shape can form during temporary short covering in a downtrend. [PDF pp. 118–122](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=118)
