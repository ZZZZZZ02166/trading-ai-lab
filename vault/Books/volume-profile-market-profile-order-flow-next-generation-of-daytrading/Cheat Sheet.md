# Cheat Sheet

*Volume Profile, Market Profile, Order Flow: Next Generation of Daytrading by Johannes Forthmann*

## Mark the auction

- **TPO / market profile** counts visits by time bracket. Its widest row is the TPO POC.
- **Volume profile** totals executions by price. Its largest row is VPOC.
- **VAH / VAL** bound roughly 70 percent of selected activity in the examples.
- **Initial balance** covers declared opening brackets. A move out of that range is a range extension.
- **Single prints / tails** show prices visited in one bracket. **Thin volume** describes sparse executions and uses a different measure.

## Review a session

1. Mark the prior main-session high, low, value area and control prices. Note overnight extrema and event timing.
2. Check whether the open is in value, stays outside, or reenters. Use the session definition consistently.
3. Anchor a flexible profile only when the formation is identifiable. Track POC migration as the auction develops.
4. At the planned area compare price progress, delta, footprint rows, and any large prints. Look for both a stall and later initiative where relevant.
5. Record a reclaim or a failed continuation using completed bars and log the later price response.

## Common traps

A P or b shape can mean different things in different trend and liquidation contexts. Resting DOM size can disappear. A footprint stack threshold varies by platform and instrument. Time-based and volume-based profiles describe different distributions, even when their shapes look similar.
