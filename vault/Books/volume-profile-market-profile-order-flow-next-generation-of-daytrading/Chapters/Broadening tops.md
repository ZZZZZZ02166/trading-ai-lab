# Broadening tops

[PDF pp. 123–127](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=123)

Successive wider highs and lows give the broadening shape in the book. The author treats it as a warning and pays particular attention to a later failed break of the range after profiling the ongoing auction. The study distinction is between a formation that is identifiable in real time and a completed historical drawing that already reveals its outcome.
