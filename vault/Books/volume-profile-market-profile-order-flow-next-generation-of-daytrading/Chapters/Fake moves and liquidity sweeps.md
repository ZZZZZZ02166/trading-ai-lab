# Fake moves and liquidity sweeps

[PDF pp. 166–194](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=166)

This section groups failed breakouts, head-and-shoulders failures, backfire patterns, and squeezes. Across the examples, a recognizable level attracts activity, price reaches or exceeds it, and the expected continuation fails. The author watches early-session and prior-day extremes as common references in the illustrations. Profile context, a return through the level, and a later change in aggression provide a basis for replaying the sequence without assigning intent to every move.
