# Filtering the ledge

[PDF pp. 118–122](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=118)

A ledge is a compressed pause inside a more pronounced move. Forthmann places a flexible volume profile over the pause and uses visible shifts in control or initiative to judge continuation. His Nasdaq example shows a P shape caused by temporary covering within a downward move, followed by another downswing. A context-sensitive profile preserves that distinction better than assigning a direction to the letter.
