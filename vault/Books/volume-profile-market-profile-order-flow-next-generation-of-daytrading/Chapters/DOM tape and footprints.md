# DOM tape and footprints

[PDF pp. 64–77](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=64)

The DOM is a view of orders still resting at each price, and it can change before anything trades. Tape tools flag faster execution or larger prints. A footprint preserves executions by price and side, including diagonal comparisons and clusters of same-side imbalances. The author uses imbalances as part of a sequence, sometimes with a break in the stack, and looks for actual follow-through in prices and bar control levels. A conspicuous print after a failed extreme has a different context from the same print in the middle of a range.
