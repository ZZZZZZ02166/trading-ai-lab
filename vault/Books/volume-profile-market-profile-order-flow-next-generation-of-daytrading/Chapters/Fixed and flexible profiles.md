# Fixed and flexible profiles

[PDF pp. 34–51](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=34)

Daily and weekly profiles provide repeatable anchors. Main-session and 24-hour windows can put their references at different levels. A flexible profile isolates a selected rotation, impulse, or multi-day range and can be shared between the time and volume views. Forthmann studies the two control prices together or follows a developing POC as volume builds at another price. The FDAX example shows a shift after a smaller auction is established. A POC move inside sideways trade has a weaker interpretation without fresh initiative.
