# Preparation and liquidity conditions

[PDF pp. 95–107](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=95)

The author prepares the prior main-session reference levels, value and POC, overnight or early-session extremes, and scheduled releases before evaluating the open. The liquidity/volatility material warns about erratic movement with wide wicks and thin conditions. Historical thresholds and preferred market hours are author examples. The practical distinction is between price travel that can be planned around and unstable moves with little usable follow-through.
