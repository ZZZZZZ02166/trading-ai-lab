# Trend as swing progression

[PDF pp. 195–198](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=195)

The discussion focuses on price closing through meaningful prior swing points and its ability to maintain progress thereafter. The EUR/USD chart shows acceleration when preceding tops are crossed. Pullbacks and follow-up signals have meaning relative to the sequence of swings. That framing is more stable across bar sizes than a shape named from one snapshot.
