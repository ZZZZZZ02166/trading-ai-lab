# Trade and risk management

[PDF pp. 199–203](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=199)

Before entry, identify meaningful invalidation and consider the difference between a fast low-volume region and a busy area where price can pause. The author discusses how failure to build an expected new swing changes the trade premise and emphasizes limiting losses on an intraday timescale. The examples are about the relationship of structure, available liquidity, and time in the trade.

- [[cards/volume-profile-market-profile-order-flow-next-generation-of-daytrading/tie-invalidation-to-failed-price-progress|Tie Invalidation to Failed Price Progress]]
