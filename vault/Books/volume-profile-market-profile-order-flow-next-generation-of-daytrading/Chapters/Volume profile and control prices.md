# Volume profile and control prices

[PDF pp. 22–27](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=22)

Volume profile bins executed quantity by price, while normal volume bars aggregate by time. The modal price is the VPOC. A prior VPOC that remains untested is a naked POC in this book. Thin areas describe sparse business and can give a transition or boundary around a richer auction. The EUR/USD and FDAX illustrations show prior reference levels carried forward, then price behavior observed at the new encounter.
