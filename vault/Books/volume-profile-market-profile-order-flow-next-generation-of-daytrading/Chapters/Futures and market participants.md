# Futures and market participants

[PDF pp. 14–18](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=14)

The book situates the analysis in futures, where participation, liquidity and news timing differ by market. It distinguishes hedgers and longer-horizon participants from short-term traders. Forthmann uses other-time-frame participants as a way to interpret sustained initiative or defensive activity. An actual identity, net position or motive cannot be assigned from a footprint alone. Timing and visible capacity differ across the main and out-of-hours sessions.
