# Summary and practice questions

[PDF pp. 157–165](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=157)

Forthmann reviews the value of learning an instrument's pace and developing a repeatable preparation process. He distinguishes theoretical chart rules from observations about where transactions and rotations took place. The bonus questions cover setup, preparation, journaling and the limits of following other traders' opinions. For study, keep the planned setup conditions separate from what later happened.

## Replay prompt

How would you record and review a sound process when the resulting trade picture failed?
