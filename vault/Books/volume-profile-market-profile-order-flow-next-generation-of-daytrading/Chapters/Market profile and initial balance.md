# Market profile and initial balance

[PDF pp. 19–21](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=19)

Time price opportunity counts the brackets in which a price is visited. The illustrated half-hour letters build a distribution whose widest row is a time-based point of control. The first two brackets depict the initial balance. Range extension goes beyond it, while a one-bracket row can form part of a tail. The size and direction of any extension can be assessed relative to earlier opening balances and the prevailing auction.
