# Introduction and method

[PDF pp. 1–13](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=1)

Forthmann places profiles and executed order flow in one workflow. A price chart supplies a broad picture, market profile shows repeated time at different prices, and volume profile shows where executed size concentrated. A footprint or delta chart helps judge the immediate encounter with a level. The opening critique of chart patterns explains why the author puts more weight on observable activity and location, though his attributions about which participants acted still need to be treated as interpretations.
