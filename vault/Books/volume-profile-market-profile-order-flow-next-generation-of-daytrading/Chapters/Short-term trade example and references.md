# Short-term trade example and references

[PDF pp. 204–208](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=204)

The final EUR/USD example begins with a backfire setup on a smaller chart and then presents the multi-session hourly path. The chart views make clear that locating the entry and remaining in a position over several sessions are different decisions. The end matter references other work on profiles. This example is best studied by replaying the first decision and then independently recording the later holding conditions.
