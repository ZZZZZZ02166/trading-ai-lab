# Delta absorption and initiative

[PDF pp. 78–94](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=78)

Divergence appears when price makes less progress than signed executions imply. At a high, repeated buying may meet passive sellers while price stops advancing. At a low, selling may meet replenishing buy liquidity. The Hang Seng example combines a narrow opening balance, a defended price low, falling delta, then an upward POC crossing. The book also shows price leading delta. These cases suggest retaining the chronological order of the stall, the directional response and the eventual resolution.
