# Accumulation follow-up and weak extremes

[PDF pp. 55–60](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=55)

The author distinguishes an established rotation from a shorter follow-up pause after directional price movement begins. Both can be profiled where they occur near an existing reference. Reversals and weak highs or lows add a separate question: did price decisively reject a region or leave nearby equal extremes that could be revisited? The opening examples show sequential runs through early-session and prior-day levels. The weak-extreme usage is chart-based and should not be confused with Dale's footprint-extreme print rule.
