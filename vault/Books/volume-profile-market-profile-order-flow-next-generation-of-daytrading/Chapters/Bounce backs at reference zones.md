# Bounce backs at reference zones

[PDF pp. 52–54](../../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=52)

A retest can respond ahead of a prior volume band, penetrate part of it or cross it entirely. The earlier cluster or imbalance defines a zone, while the next auction supplies the evidence for whether it holds. The examples move from prior activity into a new encounter, which allows a study to record distance, penetration and follow-through separately instead of grading a reaction by exact-tick touch.
