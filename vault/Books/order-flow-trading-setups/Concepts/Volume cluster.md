# Volume cluster

- **Relative Volume Defines a Cluster**. Dale's footprint shades rows based on executed size so unusually heavy areas stand out from nearby prices. A cluster is a band of relatively heavy trading. Interpretation depends on where the cluster forms and how price subsequently leaves it. [PDF pp. 34–35](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=34)
- **Retest a Footprint Cluster Inside a Trend**. Find relatively heavy trading inside a decisive directional leg, then observe whether price separates from the band. Dale waits for a pullback to its initial boundary or heavier portion. He aligns the setup with the direction of the leg. [PDF pp. 46–50](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=46)
- **Defending Inventory Can Explain a Cluster Retest**. Dale offers two explanations for a reaction at a prior cluster: earlier participants may defend their positions, and opposing traders may close as price approaches. Both are explanations for the observed reaction. The chart directly shows concentration, departure, return, and response. [PDF pp. 47–48](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=47)
- **Profile the Cluster Inside a Sharp Rejection**. Look for a fast move that reverses decisively, then locate relatively heavy trading inside the rejection. Track the band after price clearly leaves it and test the first return in the direction of rejection. The move can span more than one candle. [PDF pp. 51–54](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=51)
- **Profile the Rotation Before a Directional Departure**. Dale profiles the pre-move range and highlights its heaviest traded band once price breaks out. A later return to that band becomes a support or resistance candidate. The book pairs the return with a separate order-flow confirmation. [PDF pp. 130–136](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=130)
- **Profile a Pause Within an Established Trend**. A strong directional move can leave a thin distribution with a relatively large bump where it paused. Dale focuses on that local concentration for a potential continuation retest. The underlying trend and the actual response at the band are both part of his setup. [PDF pp. 137–140](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=137)
- **Profile the Whole Rejection to Find the Fight**. Apply a flexible profile over a strong rejected probe and its reversal to find the heaviest traded band inside the event. This can expose levels hidden by different candle time frames. Dale describes the method as harder to apply when several concentrations compete. [PDF pp. 141–145](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=141)
