# Delta

- **Bar Delta Is Executed Ask Minus Bid**. Bar delta sums ask-side volume and subtracts bid-side volume across the footprint. A positive value indicates more buy-initiated execution, though the candle can still close lower. Rotations can make the relationship between delta and candle direction less reliable. [PDF pp. 27–30](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=27)
- **Delta Can Diverge From Candle Direction**. An up bar with negative delta or a down bar with positive delta shows price and executed aggression are telling different stories. Dale uses this discrepancy to investigate a reaction near a planned level. The example pairs an advancing price with weaker delta at resistance. [PDF pp. 28–30](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=28)
- **Cumulative Delta Adds Signed Execution Over Time**. Cumulative delta runs a sum of bar delta from the session start. Price and the running flow total can be shown on matching time axes to compare their direction. A different session reset changes the displayed series. [PDF pp. 41–43](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=41)
- **Look for Aggression Away From the Reference**. Dale watches for dominant bid-side execution and negative delta at resistance, or dominant ask-side execution and positive delta at support. He reads the response within five-minute footprints in these examples. The proposed aggression is in the direction of the reaction away from the level. [PDF pp. 92–95](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=92)
- **Absorption and Initiative Form Different Stages**. Dale describes a sequence in which passive interest first stalls a move and later aggressive traders push price away. The second stage supplies directional follow-through beyond the first stall. The footprint examples mark both stages separately. [PDF pp. 95–98](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=95)
- **Reassess a Trail When Order Flow Turns**. Dale trails a favorable position while aggressive execution still supports the move. Opposing imbalance, renewed absorption, or a delta reversal near a significant price can change that read. The decision is conditioned on price location and progress. [PDF pp. 110–114](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=110)
