# Volume at price

- **The Footprint Shows Executions at Each Price**. A footprint keeps the candle's price range and layers executed bid and ask quantity onto each price row. This reveals where within the bar activity was concentrated. A normal candle only shows the aggregate price movement. [PDF pp. 25–26](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=25)
- **A Footprint Node Is Its Busiest Price Row**. Dale defines a bar's high-volume node by the largest combined bid and ask volume at a single price. This is a local, per-bar reference. A different time window can produce a different node. [PDF pp. 26–27](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=26)
- **Daily Profile Adds Context to a Footprint**. Dale displays the day's volume distribution beside individual footprints to see where repeated trading took place. The daily histogram summarizes the session by price while the footprint shows how that activity was distributed within particular bars. [PDF pp. 31–32](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=31)
- **Relative Volume Defines a Cluster**. Dale's footprint shades rows based on executed size so unusually heavy areas stand out from nearby prices. A cluster is a band of relatively heavy trading. Interpretation depends on where the cluster forms and how price subsequently leaves it. [PDF pp. 34–35](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=34)
- **Volume Profile Plots Executed Size by Price**. A horizontal histogram bins executions by price across a selected interval. The thick portions show where more traded, whereas ordinary volume bars total activity by time interval. The profile identifies candidate price zones for further assessment. [PDF pp. 121–124](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=121)
