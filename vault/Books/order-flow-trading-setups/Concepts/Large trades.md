# Large trades

- **A Large-Trade Filter Misses Split Execution**. The filter highlights single executions above a configurable size. Dale's 25-lot illustration is calibrated to his currency-futures example, and activity varies by instrument and session. Small clips and iceberg replenishment can be missed by a large-print cutoff. [PDF pp. 60–61](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=60)
- **Track Retests of Filtered Large Execution Areas**. Identify a large execution with the trades filter, then wait for one or two entire footprints to build outside the marked area. Measure the first pullback to the zone after that separation. Dale uses the approach direction to distinguish support from resistance. [PDF pp. 60–64](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=60)
- **Infer Passive Interest From a Stalled Extreme**. At resistance a large ask print can accompany aggressive buying into a passive seller while price fails to extend. At support the mirrored example shows large bid volume during a stalled sell push. Unusually large is relative to the instrument and the session. [PDF pp. 81–86](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=81)
