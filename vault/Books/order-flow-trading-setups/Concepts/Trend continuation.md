# Trend continuation

- **Retest a Footprint Cluster Inside a Trend**. Find relatively heavy trading inside a decisive directional leg, then observe whether price separates from the band. Dale waits for a pullback to its initial boundary or heavier portion. He aligns the setup with the direction of the leg. [PDF pp. 46–50](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=46)
- **Trade Stacked Imbalance Retests With Directional Context**. A stack during a one-way move defines a candidate band. Dale waits for price to move away before it comes back and uses only the first test in this setup. A buying stack approached from above is considered possible support, and the mirrored case possible resistance. [PDF pp. 65–70](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=65)
- **Profile Shape Describes Where Business Concentrated**. Dale associates D with a broad middle distribution, P with a higher bulge, lowercase b with a lower bulge, and thin profiles with directional travel. Each shape summarizes where volume accumulated. The time window affects which shape is visible. [PDF pp. 125–128](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=125)
- **Profile a Pause Within an Established Trend**. A strong directional move can leave a thin distribution with a relatively large bump where it paused. Dale focuses on that local concentration for a potential continuation retest. The underlying trend and the actual response at the band are both part of his setup. [PDF pp. 137–140](../../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=137)
