## Summary

Historical volatility estimators that exploit OHLC data to achieve far higher efficiency than close-to-close returns. Covers Parkinson's range-based estimator (4.9× efficiency), Garman-Klass (7.4× efficiency), and Rogers-Satchell's drift-adjustment. Knowing which estimator to use for a given data regime is critical to accurate vol measurement.
