## Summary

The idealising assumptions of Black-Scholes-Merton and how each fails in real markets. Covers log-normal returns vs. observed fat tails, continuous hedging impossibility and transaction costs, constant volatility vs. the smile, and how these violations explain observed skew, kurtosis risk premia, and the limits of BSM as a pricing tool vs. a quoting convention.
