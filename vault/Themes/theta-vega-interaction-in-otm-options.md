## Summary

How time decay and vega interact specifically in out-of-the-money options. Covers the non-linear theta accumulation profile for short OTM positions, vega sensitivity at different strikes relative to ATM, and the gamma-theta tradeoff at the wings where both are lower but so is the hedge cost — the structural basis for strangle and condor strategies.
