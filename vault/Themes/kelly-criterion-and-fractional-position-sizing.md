## Summary

Optimal position sizing using the Kelly criterion and its fractional variants for vol strategies. Covers full-Kelly growth maximisation and its brutal drawdown profile, the practical advantages of half-Kelly (75% growth rate at 25% variance cost), and how to calibrate bet size to measured edge and outcome variance in options strategies.
