## Summary

Conversion and reversal structures as operational put-call parity arbitrage used by market makers. Covers how conversions (long put + short call + long stock) and reversals (short put + long call + short stock) lock in risk-free profits when parity is violated, their financing mechanics, and why they are rare but important in practice.
