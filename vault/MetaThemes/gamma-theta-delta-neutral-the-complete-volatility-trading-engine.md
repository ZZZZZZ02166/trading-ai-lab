## Summary

The synthesis of gamma, theta, and delta-neutral management as an integrated vol-trading framework. Gamma and theta are opposite faces of the same option — the seller collects theta in exchange for gamma risk, while the delta-neutral trader captures realised volatility through systematic rebalancing. This meta-theme spans 44 cards from 4 books and represents the core operational engine of any systematic volatility strategy: understanding the tradeoff, managing the hedge, and surviving the path. The central testable question it raises is whether the theta collected exceeds the gamma loss over a full realised-volatility cycle.

## Why These Themes Were Merged

Both parent themes centre on the relationship between gamma and theta and the practice of delta hedging. Gamma-Theta covers the structural tradeoff; Delta-Neutral Trading and Gamma Scalping covers its operational implementation. High centroid cosine (0.760) and 25 cross-theme card edges confirm they are two faces of one concept: you can't manage gamma without delta hedging, and you can't delta-hedge without understanding the theta you're collecting.
