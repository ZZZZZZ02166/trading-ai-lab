# Volatility Regime Dynamics: Term Structure, Mean Reversion, and the Variance Premium

🔗 **Meta-Theme** | **Cards:** 19 | **Books:** 6 | **Confidence:** 0.643

## Summary

The synthesis of volatility term structure dynamics and implied-volatility mean reversion as a unified framework for understanding when and why selling volatility has positive expected value. VIX contango encodes the market's tendency to over-price near-term risk; mean reversion captures how IV reliably falls from elevated levels; and the variance premium is the systematic gap between implied and realised volatility that persists across regimes. Spanning 19 cards from 6 books, this meta-theme is the most cross-book concept in the knowledge base — its predictions are consistent across Sinclair, Natenberg, and other authors. The central testable question: does the variance premium narrow or reverse in backwardation regimes, and if so, does that predict better or worse performance for short-vol strategies?

## Why These Themes Were Merged

Volatility Term Structure and IV Mean Reversion share VIX as their empirical anchor and the variance premium as their economic explanation. High centroid cosine (0.762) and 9 cross-theme edges confirm they describe the same phenomenon from two angles: term structure describes the shape of the IV surface across time, mean reversion describes how that shape evolves dynamically. Together they explain both when to enter and what to expect from the entry.
