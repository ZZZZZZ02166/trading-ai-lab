# Risk Measurement: The Fundamental Tradeoff Between Reward and Risk

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 773-783

## Summary

Every market participant must balance reward and risk. A trader who focuses only on reward ignores the possibility of error. A trader who focuses only on risk cannot profit. The Greeks are the primary tools for measuring option risk: delta (directional risk), gamma (rate of delta change — curvature risk), theta (time risk), vega (volatility risk), rho (interest-rate risk). No position can have positive theoretical edge without accepting some form of Greek risk. Risk management means understanding which Greeks are large and ensuring no single Greek can cause an unacceptable loss.
