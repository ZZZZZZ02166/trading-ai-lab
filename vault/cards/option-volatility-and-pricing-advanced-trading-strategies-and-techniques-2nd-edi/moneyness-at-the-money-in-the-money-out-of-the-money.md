# Moneyness: At-the-Money, In-the-Money, Out-of-the-Money

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 327-336

## Summary

Precise definition: ATM requires exercise price exactly equal to current underlying price. In practice (exchange-traded options): ATM = the call and put whose exercise price is closest to current price. ITM call: exercise price < current price. OTM call: exercise price > current price. ITM put: exercise price > current price. OTM put: exercise price < current price. ATM options have: delta ≈ ±0.50, peak gamma, peak vega among same-expiry options, fastest absolute theta decay. ATM options are most actively traded because their Greeks are most sensitive to vol and time changes — making them the primary instruments for vol trading.
