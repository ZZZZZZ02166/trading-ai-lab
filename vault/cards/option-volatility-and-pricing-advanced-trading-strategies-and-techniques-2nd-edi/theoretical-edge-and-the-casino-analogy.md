# Theoretical Edge and the Casino Analogy

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 895-904

## Summary

A position with positive theoretical edge means, on average and over many repetitions, the trader should profit by the amount of the edge. Like a casino: the house edge is small per game but reliable across millions of games. Options trading works the same way: selling options at above theoretical value, or buying at below theoretical value, creates positive edge. But edge only manifests in the long run — any single trade can lose. A trader needs: (1) a positive edge per trade; (2) sufficient size to make edge statistically meaningful; (3) capital to weather variance from expected value.
