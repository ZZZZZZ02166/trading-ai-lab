# Time Box: Diagonal Spread Intermonth Arbitrage

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2072-2084

## Summary

A time box = two diagonal spreads: +1 near-month lower call / −1 far-month upper call / −1 near-month lower put / +1 far-month upper put. The time box exploits mispricings in the volatility term structure — if August IV is too high relative to June IV, the time box generates a credit exceeding fair value. Unlike a regular box spread (which arbitrages put-call parity at one expiration), a time box arbitrages the volatility relationship between two expirations. Used primarily by market makers monitoring term-structure mispricings.
