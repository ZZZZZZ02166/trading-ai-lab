# Vertical Spread Mid-Life Value: Probability Driven

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1583-1593

## Summary

Before expiration, a vertical spread's value reflects the probability of the underlying finishing above or below the strikes. If underlying is at 80 and strikes are 100/105: call spread ≈ $0 (unlikely to reach strikes); put spread ≈ $10 (very likely to finish below 95). If underlying is at 120: call spread ≈ $5; put spread ≈ $0. Between strikes, value is intermediate. This probability-based valuation means: as the underlying approaches the spread range, the credit spread's risk increases rapidly. Managing credit spreads requires monitoring the underlying's proximity to the short strike.
