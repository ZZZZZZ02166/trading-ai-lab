# Collar: Combining Put Protection with Call Premium

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2370-2383

## Summary

A collar on a long stock position: buy OTM put (protection) + sell OTM call (premium offset). Example: stock at 100, buy 95 put, sell 105 call. Protected below 95; profit capped above 105. A zero-cost collar sets strikes so premium received = premium paid. Collar = long stock + long put + short call = long stock + synthetic short stock risk reversal. Equivalent to a vertical spread on the underlying. Used by institutional investors to cheaply hedge concentrated equity positions. The tradeoff: upside is capped in exchange for downside protection.
