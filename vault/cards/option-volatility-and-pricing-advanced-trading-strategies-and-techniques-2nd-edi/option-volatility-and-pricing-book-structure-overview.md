# Option Volatility and Pricing: Book Structure Overview

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 34-49

## Summary

Natenberg's comprehensive practitioner text covers: (Ch.1-3) financial contracts, forward/futures pricing, contract specifications; (Ch.4) expiration P&L and parity graphs; (Ch.5) theoretical pricing models and the Black-Scholes model; (Ch.6) volatility — random walks, normal distributions, standard deviation, scaling; (Ch.7-9) dynamic hedging, Greeks introduction; (Ch.10-13) delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho in detail and across market conditions; (Ch.14-17) introduction to spreads, volatility spreads, calendar and diagonal spreads, synthetics; (Ch.18-19) Black-Scholes and binomial models in depth; (Ch.20-25) vol estimation, trading, and risk management. The defining characteristic: every topic is approached from a probability and risk-management perspective, not from a directional trading perspective.
