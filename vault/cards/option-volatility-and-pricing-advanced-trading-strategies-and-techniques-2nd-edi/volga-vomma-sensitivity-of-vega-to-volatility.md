# Volga (Vomma): Sensitivity of Vega to Volatility

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1120-1123

## Summary

Volga (also called vomma or dvega/dvol) = the rate of change of vega with respect to implied volatility. For ATM options, volga ≈ 0 (vega is nearly constant). For OTM and ITM options, volga is positive — vega increases as vol increases. Volga is maximum for options with deltas around 10 and 90 (deep OTM/ITM). As time increases, volga for OTM/ITM options becomes more sensitive to vol level. Practical implication: OTM options have significant volga — their vega increases as IV rises, creating convex vol exposure. Long volga positions profit if vol rises significantly (vol-of-vol bets).
