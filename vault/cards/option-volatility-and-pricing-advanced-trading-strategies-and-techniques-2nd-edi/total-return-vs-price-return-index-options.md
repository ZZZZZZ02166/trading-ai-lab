# Total-Return vs. Price-Return Index Options

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3143-3153

## Summary

Two main types of stock indexes: (1) Price-weighted (e.g., DJIA): divisor adjusts for dividends to prevent artificial index drops; dividends affect the forward price calculation for options; (2) Total-return: dividends reinvested — no price decline on ex-dividend date; forward price grows at risk-free rate alone. Options on a total-return index require no dividend adjustment in pricing models. Options on a price-weighted index require careful modeling of the dividend flow. Most equity index options (SPX, NDX) are on price-return indexes — dividends must be modeled.
