# Dividends and Short Stock: Effect on Option Pricing

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 785-794

## Summary

When a trader is short stock, the expected dividend reduces the forward price, lowering call values and raising put values. A short-stock holder therefore should: be willing to sell calls at a lower price (they're worth less) or buy puts at a higher price (they're worth more). This creates a natural hedge: selling calls or buying puts against short stock allows the trader to hedge the short back into a long position at the adjusted forward price. This is the mechanics behind conversion/reversal arbitrage in dividend-paying stocks.
