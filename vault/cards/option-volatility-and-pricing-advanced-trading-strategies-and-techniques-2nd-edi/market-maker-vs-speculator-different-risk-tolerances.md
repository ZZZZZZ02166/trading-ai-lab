# Market Maker vs. Speculator: Different Risk Tolerances

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3022-3031

## Summary

Market makers and speculators operate with fundamentally different time horizons and risk tolerances. Market makers: goal is to capture bid-ask spread; hold positions for minutes to hours; delta-hedge immediately; care most about gamma and vega risk accumulating in the book. Speculators: hold for days to weeks; accept directional and vol risk; profit from forecasting ability. A market maker who fails to hedge promptly becomes an unintentional speculator — exposed to large adverse moves without the edge to justify the risk. The discipline of immediate hedging is non-negotiable for market makers.
