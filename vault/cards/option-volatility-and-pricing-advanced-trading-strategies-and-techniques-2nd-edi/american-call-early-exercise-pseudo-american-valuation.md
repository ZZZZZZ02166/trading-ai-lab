# American Call Early Exercise: Pseudo-American Valuation

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2231-2235

## Summary

American calls on dividend-paying stocks may be worth exercising early — just before the ex-dividend date. The pseudo-American method: calculate the call value assuming: (1) expiration the day before ex-dividend (call worth its intrinsic + remaining time value), vs. (2) normal expiration with underlying price reduced by expected dividend. Take the higher value. If the dividend exceeds the remaining time value, early exercise is optimal. Traders holding short ITM calls on dividend-paying stocks must monitor this: assignment risk increases sharply when the dividend exceeds the option's time premium.
