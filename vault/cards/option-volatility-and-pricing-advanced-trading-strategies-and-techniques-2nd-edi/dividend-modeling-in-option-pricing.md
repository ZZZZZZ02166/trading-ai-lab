# Dividend Modeling in Option Pricing

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 225-234

## Summary

Equity option pricing requires accurate dividend forecasts. The ex-dividend date (when the stock drops by approximately the dividend amount) reduces the forward price, lowering call values and raising put values. Key inputs: ex-dividend date and dividend amount. Common approach: if a company has paid $0.25 quarterly for 10 quarters, assume $0.25 per quarter going forward. The present value of expected dividends is subtracted from the spot price to compute the forward price. Mispricing dividends systematically biases call values (too high) and put values (too low) for ITM options where dividend impact is significant.
