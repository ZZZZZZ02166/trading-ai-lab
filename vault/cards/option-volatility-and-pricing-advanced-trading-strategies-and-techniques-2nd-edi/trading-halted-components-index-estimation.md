# Trading Halted Components: Index Estimation

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3158-3162

## Summary

When a component stock is halted pending news, index traders estimate the stock's contribution using the midpoint of the price indication (e.g., midpoint of $67.50–$68.00 indication = $67.75) scaled by the stock's index weight. Index futures may trade based on this estimated index value. When the stock resumes trading, the index reprices, potentially creating a rapid adjustment in futures. Traders holding index positions when a component is halted carry the risk that the true reopening price differs from the estimate. This is a form of gap risk inherent in index products.
