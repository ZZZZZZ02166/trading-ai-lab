# Butterfly Spread: Structure and Payoff

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1318-1326

## Summary

A long call butterfly: +1 lower call / −2 middle call / +1 upper call. Maximum profit at expiration: when underlying is at the body (middle strike). Maximum value = spread width − premium paid. Example: 90/100/110 call butterfly. If underlying at 100: 90 call worth $10, 100 and 110 calls worth $0, position worth $10. Below 90 or above 110: worth $0. The butterfly pays maximum when the underlying is exactly at the body, and zero when it's outside the wings. This is the opposite of a straddle: profits from a quiet, range-bound market.
