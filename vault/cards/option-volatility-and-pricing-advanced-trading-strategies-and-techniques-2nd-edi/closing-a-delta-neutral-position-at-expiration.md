# Closing a Delta-Neutral Position at Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 957-968

## Summary

At expiration of a delta-neutral position, closure procedure: (1) Let OTM options expire worthless; (2) Sell ITM options at parity (intrinsic value) or exercise and offset in the underlying; (3) Liquidate any outstanding underlying contracts at market. This systematic closure locks in all P/L. The final realized P/L = sum of all gamma scalps throughout the holding period, minus theta paid, plus/minus vega P/L from IV changes, plus/minus interest on underlying position. P&L attribution to each component is essential for evaluating whether the strategy worked as intended.
