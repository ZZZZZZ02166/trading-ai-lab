# Volatility Derivatives: Pure Exposure to Volatility as an Asset

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3600-3610

## Summary

Before VIX futures and variance swaps, no instrument allowed pure vol exposure without delta risk. Volatility derivatives: (1) VIX futures — trade on the expected 30-day IV of the S&P 500 30 days forward; typically in contango (futures > current VIX) due to uncertainty premium; (2) Variance swaps — pay realized variance minus fixed variance strike; mathematically simpler (variance is additive); (3) Volatility swaps — pay realized vol minus fixed vol; harder to replicate. These instruments allow: hedging vega without changing delta; speculating on vol-of-vol; expressing term structure views without legging into calendar spreads.
