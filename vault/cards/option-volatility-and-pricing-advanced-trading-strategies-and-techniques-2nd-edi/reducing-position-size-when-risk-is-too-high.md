# Reducing Position Size When Risk Is Too High

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1803-1811

## Summary

A disciplined trader knows that sometimes, because of risk considerations, reducing position size — even at a cost (giving up theoretical edge) — is the right action. If a spread has moved against the trader and risk parameters are being breached, reducing size is more important than maintaining maximum theoretical edge. Risk control is always the primary concern. Traders who refuse to reduce size when risk parameters are exceeded typically suffer larger losses later. Rule: define maximum acceptable risk before entry; reduce size (not just adjust) when that risk is breached.
