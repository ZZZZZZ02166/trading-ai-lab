# Spread Adjustments: When to Adjust and How

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1786-1793

## Summary

Two types of adjustments in spread trading: (1) Delta adjustments — rebalancing when delta drifts too far from neutral; (2) Greek adjustments — changing the spread's Greek profile (gamma, vega, theta) when the position's risk characteristics become unacceptable. Methods for delta adjustment: sell underlying, sell calls, or buy puts. The best adjustment method: whichever improves the risk-reward characteristics of the overall position most efficiently. Avoid adjustments that increase gamma or vega risk beyond acceptable levels. Each adjustment should improve the position's theoretical edge or reduce risk.
