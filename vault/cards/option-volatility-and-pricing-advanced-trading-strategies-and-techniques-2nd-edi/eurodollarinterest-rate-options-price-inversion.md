# Eurodollar/Interest-Rate Options: Price Inversion

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 662-670

## Summary

Interest-rate options are quoted on indexed prices (Eurodollar: 100 − interest rate). A 93.00 call in listed terms = a 7.00% put in interest-rate terms — they represent the same position. A correct model makes this transformation automatically. Failure to understand this inversion causes directional errors: buying a Eurodollar call when you expect rates to fall is correct (rates fall → price rises from 93 toward 100), but mistaking the contract as equivalent to a commodity call can lead to the wrong conclusion. Always verify the quoting convention before trading interest-rate derivatives.
