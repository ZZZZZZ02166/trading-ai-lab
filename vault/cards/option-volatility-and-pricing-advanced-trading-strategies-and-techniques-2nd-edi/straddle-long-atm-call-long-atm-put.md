# Straddle: Long ATM Call + Long ATM Put

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1292-1306

## Summary

A long straddle consists of a long ATM call and a long ATM put with the same strike and expiration. Greek characteristics: +gamma (profits from large moves in either direction), −theta (pays daily), +vega (profits from rising IV). The straddle profits when: realized vol > implied vol (large moves from gamma scalps), or IV rises (vega gain). The straddle loses when: underlying is quiet and IV falls. At expiration, the straddle breaks even when the underlying is at the strike ± (call premium + put premium). A short straddle has the inverse profile: collects premium, profits from quiet/declining vol.
