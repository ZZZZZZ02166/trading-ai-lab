# Ratio Spreads: Asymmetric Payoff with Credit Potential

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1353-1366

## Summary

A call ratio spread: long fewer lower-strike calls, short more upper-strike calls (e.g., +1 call / −2 higher calls). Credit received upfront. P/L: profits if the underlying stays below the short strike; limited profit if underlying moves above the long strike; large losses if underlying moves far above the short strikes (naked short call exposure). Put ratio spreads are equivalent with puts. Ratio spreads are typically initiated for a credit — they profit from quiet to moderately bullish/bearish markets but have uncapped risk on one side. Requires strict stop-loss management.
