# Volatility-Aware Hedging Beyond Simple Puts

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2383-2385

## Summary

Simple hedges (buying a put, selling a covered call) are most common but not optimal in all situations. More sophisticated alternatives: (1) Ratio put spreads — buy more lower puts, sell fewer higher puts, for net debit or credit; cheaper than outright puts but limited protection; (2) Calendar spreads — short near-term option to finance far-term protection; (3) Delta-neutral overlays — systematically hedge directional risk dynamically. Each approach involves a different tradeoff between protection cost, upside participation, and complexity.
