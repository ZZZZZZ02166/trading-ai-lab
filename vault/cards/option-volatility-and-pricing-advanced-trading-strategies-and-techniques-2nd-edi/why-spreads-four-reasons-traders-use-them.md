# Why Spreads: Four Reasons Traders Use Them

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1249-1252

## Summary

Option spreads serve four distinct purposes: (1) Relative value — when absolute IV is uncertain but one option appears mispriced vs. another, spread the two to capture relative mispricing; (2) Directional with a specific scenario — construct a position that profits specifically within a forecast range; (3) Reducing variance — spreading reduces the variance of outcomes compared to outright positions, making edge more reliable in the short run; (4) Capital efficiency — many spreads require less margin than outright positions for similar exposure. Most professional options trading involves spreads rather than outright positions.
