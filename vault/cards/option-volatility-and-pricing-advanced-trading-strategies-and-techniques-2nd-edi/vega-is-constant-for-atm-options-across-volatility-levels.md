# Vega Is Constant for ATM Options Across Volatility Levels

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1113-1120

## Summary

An ATM option's value vs. implied volatility graph is approximately linear. Because vega is the slope of this graph, the vega of an ATM option is relatively constant across different volatility levels (20%, 30%, 40%). This means: for ATM options, a 1% increase in IV always produces approximately the same dollar change in value regardless of where IV starts. For OTM and ITM options, vega is not constant — the value vs. IV graph is curved, meaning vega itself changes with volatility (volga).
