# Theta Components: Volatility Decay vs. Interest Effects

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2538-2550

## Summary

Black-Scholes theta has two components: (1) Volatility value decay — always negative for long options; equal in sign for calls and puts; dominates in most conditions; (2) Interest-rate components — can be positive or negative depending on settlement type and whether call or put. For deep ITM calls, the interest component (cost of carry benefit) partially offsets decay. For deep ITM puts, interest adds to decay (waiting costs you the interest you could earn). Total theta = sum of both components. In most practical situations, the decay component dominates and theta is simply the daily time value erosion.
