# American Put Premium: Largest in Low-Vol, High-Rate Environments

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2248-2262

## Summary

The American put's excess value over its European counterpart (early exercise premium) is largest when: (1) Volatility is low — the put is more likely to stay deep ITM; waiting adds little additional value from future movement; (2) Interest rates are high — the time value of collecting intrinsic value today exceeds the optionality of waiting. The premium shrinks at high volatility because large future moves make waiting more valuable. Practical implication: deep ITM puts should be analyzed for early exercise when vol is low and rates are high. The risk for short-put holders: unexpected early assignment.
