# Interest Rate Effects on Option Values: Calls vs. Puts

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 783-785

## Summary

For stock options: increasing interest rates raises call values and lowers put values. Two effects: (1) Rising rates increase the forward price (cost of carry); (2) Rising rates reduce the present value of the option. For calls, the forward price increase dominates — calls rise. For puts, the forward price increase hurts (puts benefit from lower forward price), so puts fall. Deep ITM calls have the highest rho. For options on futures with futures-type settlement, interest rates have less effect because there is no cost of carry on the futures contract itself.
