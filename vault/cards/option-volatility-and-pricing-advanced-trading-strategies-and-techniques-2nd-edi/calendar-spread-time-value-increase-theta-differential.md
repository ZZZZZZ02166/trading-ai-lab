# Calendar Spread Time Value Increase: Theta Differential

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1387-1397

## Summary

The calendar spread value increases as time passes because of ATM theta acceleration: the near-term option (which is short) decays faster per day than the far-term option (which is long). This theta differential is the calendar spread's 'rent.' As expiration of the near-term option approaches, its theta accelerates dramatically for ATM options, while the far-term option's theta is relatively stable. The maximum value of the calendar spread typically occurs near the expiration of the short-term option, when both options are still ATM.
