# Gamma Curvature: Why Long Gamma Always Profits from Large Moves

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 997-1009

## Summary

The curvature of an option's value curve (positive gamma for long options) creates a mathematical asymmetry: for a given price change ΔS, the long-gamma position gains more from favorable moves than it loses from unfavorable moves of equal magnitude. This is because delta itself shifts in the favorable direction as the underlying moves. The aggregate of all delta-neutral rebalancing trades (buy on dips, sell on rallies) is always positive for a long-gamma position, regardless of direction. The cost of this free asymmetry is theta — the daily decay of time value.
