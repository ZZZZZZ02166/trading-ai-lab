# Interest on Underlying Hedge Position

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 968-973

## Summary

A delta-neutral hedge shorting 50 underlying at 97.70 generates $4,885 in proceeds earning 6% × 70/365 = $56.21 interest over the holding period. Each rebalancing trade also generates a cash credit or debit that earns or costs interest until expiration. This interest component is part of the true P/L — often small at low interest rates but significant at high rates. The full theoretical value of the option accounts for this cost of carry. Traders who ignore interest when computing actual P/L will overstate or understate results.
