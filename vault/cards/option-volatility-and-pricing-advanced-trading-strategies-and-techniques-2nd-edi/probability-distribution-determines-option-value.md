# Probability Distribution Determines Option Value

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 487-497

## Summary

The theoretical value of an option depends entirely on the probability distribution assumed for the underlying at expiration. The higher the volatility input, the wider the distribution, and the higher the expected value of OTM options. Different distribution assumptions (normal, lognormal, fat-tailed) produce different theoretical values for the same option. This is why traders argue about volatility: it is a disagreement about the shape and width of the distribution. Implied volatility is the distribution width implied by market prices — it is the market's consensus about how wide the distribution will be.
