# Volatility's Effect on Delta: High Vol Pulls All Deltas Toward 50

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1056-1066

## Summary

In a high-volatility environment: OTM options have higher deltas (closer to 50) because there is more probability they expire ITM; ITM options have lower deltas (also closer to 50) because there is more probability they expire OTM. In a low-volatility environment: OTM options have lower deltas (closer to 0); ITM options have higher deltas (closer to 1 or -1). The practical implication: OTM options bought at low vol will see their deltas rise significantly if vol spikes. Conversely, ITM options become more ATM-like at high vol, making them useful for delta-hedging strategies.
