# Equity Skew: Two Forces Creating Persistent OTM Put Premium

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3441-3449

## Summary

Equity OTM puts have persistently higher IV than OTM calls (downward-sloping skew). Two simultaneous structural forces: (1) Insurance demand — investors continuously buy OTM puts for crash protection, creating buying pressure that inflates OTM put IV; (2) Covered call writing — investors owning stock systematically sell OTM calls to generate income, creating selling pressure that deflates OTM call IV. Both forces reinforce the skew: OTM puts bid up, OTM calls sold down. This structural premium makes selling OTM puts theoretically profitable on average — but with crash risk that materializes periodically.
