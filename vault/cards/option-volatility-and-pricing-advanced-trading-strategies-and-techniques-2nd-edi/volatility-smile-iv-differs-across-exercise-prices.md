# Volatility Smile: IV Differs Across Exercise Prices

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3433-3441

## Summary

In practice, implied volatility is not constant across strikes for the same expiration — it forms the 'volatility smile' or 'volatility skew.' Black-Scholes assumes constant IV, but market prices reveal that IV varies systematically with strike. The shape is observable by back-solving IV from market prices at each strike. For equity options, the smile is actually a skew (monotonically downward sloping), not symmetric. For FX options, the smile is often symmetric. For commodities, skew can be upward-sloping (upside skew) reflecting supply-shock risk.
