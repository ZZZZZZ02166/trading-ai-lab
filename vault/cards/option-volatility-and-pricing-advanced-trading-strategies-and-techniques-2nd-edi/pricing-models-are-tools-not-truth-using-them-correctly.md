# Pricing Models Are Tools, Not Truth: Using Them Correctly

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 464-476

## Summary

Option pricing models are probability-based tools that help traders make better decisions, but they are not infallible. A trader who ignores models is flying blind. A trader paralyzed by model uncertainty (analysis paralysis) cannot profit either. The correct approach: use the model as a framework for thinking about option value, while maintaining full awareness of its assumptions and limitations. The most important limitation: all models require a volatility input that cannot be observed directly. A model's output is only as good as the volatility estimate used as input.
