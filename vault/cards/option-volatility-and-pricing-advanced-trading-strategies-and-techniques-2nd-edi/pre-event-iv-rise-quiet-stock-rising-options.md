# Pre-Event IV Rise: Quiet Stock, Rising Options

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1397-1413

## Summary

Before a significant known event (earnings, FDA, central bank), the underlying stock may remain quiet while IV rises. Traders price in the anticipated large post-event move. Conditions: (1) low realized vol pre-event (gamma scalps negligible); (2) rising IV pre-event (positive vega). Long calendar spreads or long vega positions benefit pre-event because the far-term option has more vega exposure. After the event, vol crush typically occurs — IV collapses even if the stock makes a large move. Short calendar spreads (short vega) can benefit post-event.
