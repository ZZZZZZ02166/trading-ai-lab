# Automatic Exercise Thresholds and Do-Not-Exercise Notices

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 336-344

## Summary

At expiration, exchanges automatically exercise any ITM option above a threshold: $0.05 for retail, $0.02 for professionals. Lower threshold for professionals because their per-trade transaction costs are lower — exercise profitable at smaller ITM amounts. A holder who does not want exercise must file a do-not-exercise notice. This creates pin risk for short-option holders: when stock is near the strike at expiration, the holder may or may not exercise, and the short party doesn't know if assignment will occur until after close. Pin risk is largest when the underlying is within $0.05–$0.10 of the strike.
