# Theta Acceleration: ATM Options Decay Fastest Near Expiry

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1094-1099

## Summary

Theta of ATM options accelerates as expiration approaches — the daily decay rate approaches infinity at the final moment. For ITM and OTM options, theta actually slows near expiry (they have less time premium to decay). The practical implication: an ATM option with 1 week remaining decays faster per day than the same option with 1 month remaining. The theta-gamma tradeoff is most extreme near expiry for ATM options: maximum daily income (theta) but maximum gamma risk simultaneously. Calendar spread traders who are short near-term ATM options benefit most from this acceleration.
