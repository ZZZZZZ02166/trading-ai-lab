# Margin for Error in Spreads: Context-Dependent

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1747-1755

## Summary

What constitutes an adequate margin for error in a spread depends on the volatility characteristics of the specific market. A 5 percentage point vol margin may be extremely tight in a high-vol market where vol swings 10+ points routinely, but ample in a stable market. Traders must calibrate their required margin for error to the historical range of vol in their specific market. A margin for error that seems comfortable based on average conditions may be dangerously tight during a vol spike. Always stress-test your vol assumption: what happens if your forecast is wrong by 50%?
