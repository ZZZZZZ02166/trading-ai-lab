# Using Greeks to Characterize a Position's Risk Profile

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 904-913

## Summary

Delta and gamma together describe the directional characteristics of a position: delta tells you which direction helps; gamma tells you how fast you want the market to move. A position with +delta and +gamma: benefits from upward moves that accelerate. A position with +delta and −gamma: benefits from slow, steady upward moves — fast moves hurt. Theta and vega describe the time and volatility characteristics. A full risk profile requires knowing the signs and magnitudes of all four main Greeks simultaneously. Managing a position means keeping these Greeks within acceptable bounds.
