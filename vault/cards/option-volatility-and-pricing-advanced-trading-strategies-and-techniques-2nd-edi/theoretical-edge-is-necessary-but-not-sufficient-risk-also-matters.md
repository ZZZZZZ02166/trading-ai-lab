# Theoretical Edge Is Necessary But Not Sufficient: Risk Also Matters

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 455-464

## Summary

Determining theoretical value (expected value) is only half the problem. Because option values are probability-based, the laws of probability only guarantee outcomes in the long run. A single trade with positive edge can still lose. A trader must also consider: (1) the variance of outcomes — a wide distribution means a single trade may deviate far from expected value; (2) the required capital to weather adverse outcomes; (3) whether the edge is large enough relative to the variance to justify the risk. A small edge on a large, volatile position is not equivalent to a large edge on a small position.
