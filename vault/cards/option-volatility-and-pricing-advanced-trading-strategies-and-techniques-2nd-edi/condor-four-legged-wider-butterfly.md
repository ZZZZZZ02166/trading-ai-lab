# Condor: Four-Legged Wider Butterfly

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1338-1353

## Summary

A condor consists of four legs: +1 lowest call / −1 lower-middle call / −1 upper-middle call / +1 highest call (or puts equivalently). The condor's maximum profit range is wider than a butterfly (between the two inner strikes instead of only at one strike). The condor pays maximum when the underlying is between the two short strikes at expiration. Call condor and put condor of the same strikes have identical characteristics (put-call parity). Condors are preferred over butterflies when a wider profit zone is desired but maximum profit is lower per dollar of risk.
