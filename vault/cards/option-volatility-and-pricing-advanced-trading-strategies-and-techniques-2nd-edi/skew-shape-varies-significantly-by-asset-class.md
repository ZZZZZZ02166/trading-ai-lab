# Skew Shape Varies Significantly by Asset Class

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3754-3756

## Summary

Skew shape reflects asset-specific risk characteristics: (1) Equity indexes: steep downward skew — OTM puts >> OTM calls; crash risk is dominant fear; (2) Individual stocks: similar but less steep than indexes; idiosyncratic upside (M&A, earnings) reduces the extreme downward skew; (3) Commodities: often upward skew (OTM calls > OTM puts) for agricultural commodities — supply disruption risk is to the upside; mixed for energy; (4) Currencies: symmetric smile — crash risk is bidirectional; both OTM puts and calls elevated. Traders who apply equity skew intuitions to commodity or FX markets will systematically misprice options in those markets.
