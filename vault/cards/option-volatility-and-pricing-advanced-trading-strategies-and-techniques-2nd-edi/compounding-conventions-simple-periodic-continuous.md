# Compounding Conventions: Simple, Periodic, Continuous

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 670-682

## Summary

The future value of $1,000 at 12% annual rate after 1 year: (1) Simple interest: $1,120; (2) Monthly compounding: $1,126.83; (3) Continuous compounding: e^0.12 × $1,000 = $1,127.50. Black-Scholes uses continuous compounding. For option pricing, the compounding convention affects theoretical values and Greeks, especially rho and theta's interest-rate component. When comparing models or broker quotes, always confirm which convention is used. The difference is small for short-dated options but meaningful for LEAPS or multi-year positions.
