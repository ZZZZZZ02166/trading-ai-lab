# IV Term Structure: Identifying Overpriced and Underpriced Months

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2863-2873

## Summary

The implied volatility term structure plots IV against expiration date for a fixed strike. A smooth term structure (fitted curve) represents 'fair' calendar spread pricing. Months that plot above the smooth curve are relatively overpriced; months below are underpriced. Calendar spreads between an overpriced near month and a fairly-priced far month generate a premium vs. fair value. The term structure in backwardation (near > far) typically occurs during high-vol, high-fear environments; normal contango (near < far) reflects the baseline uncertainty premium for future time periods.
