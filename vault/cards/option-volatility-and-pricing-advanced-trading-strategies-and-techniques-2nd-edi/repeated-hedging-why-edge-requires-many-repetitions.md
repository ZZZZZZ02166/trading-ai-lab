# Repeated Hedging: Why Edge Requires Many Repetitions

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 989-997

## Summary

A single delta-neutral hedge with positive theoretical edge can still lose — the laws of probability only guarantee results in the long run. The adjustment process (gamma scalping) forces the trader to make many small bets at the same favorable odds, spreading variance across many trades. A trader who rebalances frequently will have a P/L closer to the theoretical expectation than one who rebalances rarely. The analogy: a casino game with 0.5% house edge on a single $1,000 bet is risky; the same edge spread across 10,000 $0.10 bets is nearly certain to profit.
