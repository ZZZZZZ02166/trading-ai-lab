# Synthetic Positions from Put-Call Parity: Full Table

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1842-1848

## Summary

Put-call parity: C − P = F − K × e^(−rT) (for European options on futures). From this, all six synthetic positions: (1) Synthetic long underlying = long call + short put (same strike); (2) Synthetic short underlying = short call + long put; (3) Synthetic long call = long underlying + long put; (4) Synthetic short call = short underlying + short put; (5) Synthetic long put = short underlying + long call; (6) Synthetic short put = long underlying + short call (= covered call). All have identical risk to the real position. Covered call = synthetic short put — same downside, same risk.
