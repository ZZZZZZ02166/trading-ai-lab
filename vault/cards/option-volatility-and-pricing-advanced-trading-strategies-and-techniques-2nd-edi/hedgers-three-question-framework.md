# Hedger's Three-Question Framework

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 2385-2394

## Summary

Before constructing a hedge, answer: (1) Is this disaster insurance (must protect at worst case)? → only buying options qualifies; choice is which strike. (2) How much directional risk to eliminate? → determines option strike (ATM = full hedge, OTM = partial hedge) and position size. (3) What additional risks are acceptable? → determines whether to sell an option to finance the hedge (collar, ratio spread) or accept the cost of a pure long option. Most hedge failures come from not answering question 3 explicitly — traders inadvertently accept risks they didn't intend to take (e.g., a short call in a collar capping a large recovery).
