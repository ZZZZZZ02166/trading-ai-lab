# The Six Black-Scholes Assumptions and Reality

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 3276-3285

## Summary

Black-Scholes assumes: (1) Frictionless markets — no transaction costs, taxes, or borrowing restrictions; (2) Continuous trading — positions adjustable at any time; (3) Constant interest rates; (4) Constant volatility; (5) Volatility independent of underlying price; (6) Small price changes are normally distributed → lognormal prices at expiration. All six are violated in real markets. The most critical violations: (4) volatility is not constant (vol smile/skew); (6) fat tails and jumps create non-lognormal distributions. Practitioners use the model while managing the residual risk from these violations.
