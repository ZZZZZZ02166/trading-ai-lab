# Spreads Increase Breakeven Volatility Range (Margin for Error)

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1252-1257

## Summary

A key advantage of spreading: it increases the range of outcomes over which the position is profitable. An outright short straddle at 25% IV breaks even only if realized vol stays near 25%. A spread against the straddle might allow profitability for realized vols between 20% and 35% — a much wider range. This wider breakeven range means more occurrences when the trader is right. Spreads trade potential maximum profit for reliability of profit. For traders with positive-edge strategies, reliability is more valuable than maximum profit.
