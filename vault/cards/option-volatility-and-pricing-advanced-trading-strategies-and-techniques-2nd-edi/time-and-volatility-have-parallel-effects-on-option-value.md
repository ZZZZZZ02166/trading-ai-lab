# Time and Volatility Have Parallel Effects on Option Value

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1066-1081

## Summary

More time to expiration acts similarly to higher volatility: both increase the likelihood of large price changes and raise option values. Conversely, less time is like lower volatility. This parallel means that strategies designed to profit from time decay (short theta) often also require tolerance for low volatility (short vega). Traders can sometimes substitute one dimension for the other when constructing positions — a calendar spread exploits this: the near-term option has less effective 'vol-time' than the far-term option.
