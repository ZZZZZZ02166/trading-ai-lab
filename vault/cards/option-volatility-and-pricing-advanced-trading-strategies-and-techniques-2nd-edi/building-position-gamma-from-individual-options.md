# Building Position Gamma from Individual Options

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 845-848

## Summary

For a multi-option portfolio: position gamma is additive. Total position gamma = Σ(individual gamma × contract quantity × 100). Long options add positive gamma; short options add negative gamma. A trader with large positive gamma wants the underlying to move — in either direction — because each move creates a scalp profit. A trader with large negative gamma dreads large moves — each move creates an accelerating loss. The net gamma of the entire book is the most important single risk number for a market maker to monitor.
