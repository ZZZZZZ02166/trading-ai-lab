# Adjustment Methods: Underlying vs. Options

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1793-1802

## Summary

When a spread needs a delta adjustment and the underlying is available, the choice between adjusting with underlying vs. options has significant implications: Underlying adjustments: (1) no change to gamma/vega; (2) direct, clean delta change; (3) adds interest cost on the underlying position. Option adjustments: (1) change both delta and gamma/vega; (2) may create theoretical edge on the adjustment trade itself; (3) can improve or worsen the spread's risk profile. Traders who can capture edge on adjustment trades (buying underpriced, selling overpriced options) prefer option adjustments. When no edge is available, underlying adjustments are simpler.
