# Selecting the Best Vertical Spread

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 1594-1600

## Summary

Given many available strikes and expiries, criteria for optimal vertical spread: (1) Strike selection — use the current underlying price as the body; choose width based on your price target range. (2) Expiry selection — match holding period to anticipated timeline for the move. (3) IV comparison — check that the short leg has higher IV than the long leg (sell high IV, buy low IV relative to skew). (4) Risk-reward — wider spreads collect more premium in credit spreads but require more space for the underlying to move. The ideal spread maximizes (edge per dollar risked) × (probability of profit).
