# The IV-HV Gap: Investigating Market vs. Model Discrepancy

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 710-720

## Summary

When a theoretical value is $2.94 but market trades at $3.40, IV exceeds the trader's forecast. This discrepancy has three possible explanations: (1) Structural demand: persistent hedging demand inflates option prices regardless of fair value; (2) Information asymmetry: the market knows something about upcoming events; (3) Wrong forecast: the trader's vol estimate is too low. The correct response: investigate before trading. Blindly selling IV because it seems high has been the source of many large losses when IV continues to rise. Always ask: why is IV where it is? before assuming mispricing.
