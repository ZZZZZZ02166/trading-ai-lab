# Implied Values: Back-Solving for the Unknown Variable

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Volatility and Pricing — pp. 214-225

## Summary

Given a pricing model and all inputs except one, the unknown can be back-solved from a market price. This applies to: implied spot price (from forward price), implied interest rate, implied dividend D = [S × (1 + r × t)] − F, and most importantly, implied volatility (from an observed option price). If a contract is fairly priced, its implied value represents the market's consensus estimate of the missing input. Implied volatility is the most critical application — given a market option price and all other known inputs, back-solve the Black-Scholes model to find IV. IV is not calculated directly; it is always inferred.
