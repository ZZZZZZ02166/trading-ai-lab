# The Reverse Conversion: Mirror of the Conversion

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 202-203

## Summary

Reverse conversion = short stock + short put + long call (same strike, same expiry). Mirror image of conversion. Used when calls are underpriced relative to puts (reverse parity violation). The short stock rebate (interest income from short sale proceeds) significantly affects reverse conversion profitability — professional traders with high rebate rates find more reverse conversions viable than retail traders.
