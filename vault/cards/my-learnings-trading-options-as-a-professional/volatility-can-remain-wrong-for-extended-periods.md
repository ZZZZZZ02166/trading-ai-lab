# Volatility Can Remain 'Wrong' for Extended Periods

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 248-251

## Summary

Just because IV appears elevated relative to historical vol doesn't mean it will normalize before expiration. IV can remain high for weeks or months if uncertainty persists (earnings cycle, litigation, macro uncertainty). Similarly, IV can stay depressed in calm markets longer than expected. Traders who sell 'expensive' IV face the risk of IV expanding further — especially in markets approaching shock events. Never assume mean reversion is imminent.
