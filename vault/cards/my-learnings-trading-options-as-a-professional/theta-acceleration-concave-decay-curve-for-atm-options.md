# Theta Acceleration: Concave Decay Curve for ATM Options

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 141-142

## Summary

An ATM 100 Call at 160 days has small theta (decays slowly). As expiration approaches, theta increases exponentially. The final 20 days may account for more time value erosion than the preceding 140 days. This concave decay curve means: (1) selling options in the last 3–6 weeks maximizes theta collection per unit of gamma risk; (2) buying ATM options far from expiry is expensive in theta but gives more time.
