# P/L Diagrams: Long and Short Puts

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 45-47

## Summary

Long put: profit rises as stock falls below break-even; loss limited to premium above break-even. Short put: premium received above break-even; increasing loss below. Both strategies show three time curves. Unlike calls, put time-value behavior is symmetric on the downside. Short puts are commonly used to generate income in range-bound or mildly bullish markets.
