# Position Vega: Dollar P/L from a 1% IV Change

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 149-149

## Summary

Position vega = option vega × contracts × 100. Example: long 4 XYZ 80 Calls, vega 0.13 → position vega = 0.52. A 1% rise in IV increases position value by $52. For short options, position vega is negative — a 1% IV rise causes a loss. Position vega quantifies the exact dollar risk of being wrong on an IV direction bet. Short vega positions want IV to fall; long vega want IV to rise.
