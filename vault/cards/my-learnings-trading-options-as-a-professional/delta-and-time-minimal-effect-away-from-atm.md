# Delta and Time: Minimal Effect Away from ATM

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 119-121

## Summary

Time to expiry has little effect on delta for deep ITM or OTM options. A deep ITM 90 Call stays near delta 1.0 regardless of time. An OTM 110 Call stays near 0 regardless of time. Only ATM options (~0.50 delta) are relatively stable across time horizons. This means time alone doesn't dramatically shift directional exposure unless the option is at the money.
