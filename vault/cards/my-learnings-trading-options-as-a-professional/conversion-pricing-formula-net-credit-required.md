# Conversion Pricing Formula: Net Credit Required

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 206-207

## Summary

Conversion target price for the call: Strike + (interest × days/365) + put price − stock price. This formula ensures the net credit received from the conversion exactly equals the interest that could be earned on the strike price capital over the holding period. If the actual call trades above this formula price, a conversion can be profitably executed. The formula is per-share and must be adjusted for actual trade size.
