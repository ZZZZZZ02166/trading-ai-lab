# Theory of the Bid-Ask Spread: Market Makers Earn the Edge

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 292-294

## Summary

Market makers earn profit by buying at the bid and selling at the ask. They delta-hedge each trade immediately to neutralize directional risk. Then they attempt to close the hedge at a profit before market conditions change. Example: sell 10 calls at bid 1.85, hedge with +460 shares at 54.03. Stock rises to 55.00. Buy back calls at ask 1.85 — but sell stock at 55.00 → profit from stock move. The bid-ask edge is captured by being the liquidity provider.
