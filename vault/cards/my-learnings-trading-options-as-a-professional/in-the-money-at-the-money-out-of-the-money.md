# In-the-Money, At-the-Money, Out-of-the-Money

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 26-28

## Summary

Call ITM when stock > strike; ATM when stock ≈ strike; OTM when stock < strike. Put ITM when stock < strike; ATM when stock ≈ strike; OTM when stock > strike. ITM options have intrinsic value (stock-price difference vs. strike). ATM and OTM options have only time value. Traders commonly call an option 'at the money' even when the stock is near — but not exactly at — the strike.
