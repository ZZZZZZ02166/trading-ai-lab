# IV Term Structure: How Implied Vol Varies by Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 242-243

## Summary

IV varies across expiration dates, creating a term structure (or volatility term structure). Normal (contango): longer-dated IV > shorter-dated (uncertainty increases with time). Inverted (backwardation): short-term IV > long-term (event-driven — e.g., earnings approaching). For calendar spreads: buy the back month when it's cheaper than front; sell when it's richer. Term structure shifts are a source of profits and losses for traders holding multi-expiry positions.
