# Multiple-Scenario Analysis for Option Strategies

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 98-99

## Summary

Option traders must evaluate positions across multiple market scenarios — not just the most likely outcome. Scenario analysis means asking: what happens to the position if (A) the forecast is right and IV stays constant, (B) forecast is right but IV falls, (C) forecast is wrong but IV rises? Joe's analysis of Jumpco April 75 Calls illustrates this: each scenario produces different P/L, and only by examining all three does a trader understand what they are really buying or selling.
