# Greeks Are Snapshots, Not Constants

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 336-337

## Summary

Position Greeks change as market conditions change. A position with delta = +500 today may have delta = +800 if the stock rises $5 (gamma effect). Vega may shrink as time passes. Theta may accelerate as expiration approaches. Traders who rely on static Greek calculations — without understanding how they will evolve — will be surprised. The graphs of position Greeks vs. stock price (Ch. 10 figures) illustrate how entire risk profiles shift dynamically.
