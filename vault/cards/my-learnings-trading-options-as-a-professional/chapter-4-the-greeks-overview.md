# Chapter 4 — The Greeks: Overview

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 113-114

## Summary

Five Greeks explain option price sensitivity: (1) Delta — stock price change. (2) Gamma — rate of delta change. (3) Vega — volatility change. (4) Theta — time decay. (5) Rho — interest rate change. Each Greek measures the impact of changing one input while all others remain constant. In the real world, multiple inputs change simultaneously, requiring traders to combine all Greek estimates.
