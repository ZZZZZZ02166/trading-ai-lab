# P/L Diagrams: Long and Short Calls

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 43-45

## Summary

Long call P/L diagram: hockey-stick at expiration — loss limited to premium paid below break-even; profit unlimited above. Three curves shown (expiration, 60 days, 30 days prior) reveal time premium. Short call: inverted hockey-stick — max profit is premium received; unlimited loss above break-even. Curved prior-to-expiry lines show why short options have positive theta but dangerous gamma.
