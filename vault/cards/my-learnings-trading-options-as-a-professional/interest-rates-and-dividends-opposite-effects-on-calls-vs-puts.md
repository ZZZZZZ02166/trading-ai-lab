# Interest Rates and Dividends: Opposite Effects on Calls vs. Puts

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 75-76

## Summary

Interest rates and dividends have opposite effects: higher interest rates → call prices rise, put prices fall (cost of carry). Higher dividends → call prices fall, put prices rise (dividend reduces stock on ex-date). These effects are significant for LEAPS and for positions held through ex-dividend dates. They are the mechanical basis of rho and the reason deep-ITM calls may be exercised early.
