# Expected vs. Realized vs. Implied Volatility: Three Distinct Concepts

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 253-254

## Summary

Three volatility terms are often confused: (1) Historical/Realized volatility: what the stock has done — backward-looking. (2) Implied volatility: what the market prices in via options — forward-looking, market consensus. (3) Expected volatility: a trader's personal forecast of future realized vol. The vol trade is always a comparison: does the trader's expected vol differ from implied vol? If yes, and if the trader is right, they profit.
