# Short Stock Rebate: Income for Professional Traders

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 41-43

## Summary

When professional traders short stock, they receive a rebate on the interest generated by the short sale proceeds. Market makers typically receive 80% of net interest from short stock positions. This rebate affects arbitrage pricing: it reduces the carrying cost of short-stock positions, making conversions and reverse conversions more profitable and tightening the pricing boundaries.
