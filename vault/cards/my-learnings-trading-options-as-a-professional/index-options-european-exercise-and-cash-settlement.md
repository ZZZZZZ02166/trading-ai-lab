# Index Options: European Exercise and Cash Settlement

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 102-104

## Summary

Index options (SPX, NDX, etc.) typically stop trading on Thursday before the third Friday, with final settlement based on Friday morning opening prices. They are European-style (no early exercise). A call exercised when SPX is 20 points above strike delivers $2,000 in cash (20 × $100 multiplier). Settlement risk: the opening prices used for settlement can gap significantly from Thursday's closing prices.
