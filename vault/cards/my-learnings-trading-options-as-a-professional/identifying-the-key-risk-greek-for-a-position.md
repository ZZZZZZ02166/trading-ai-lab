# Identifying the Key Risk Greek for a Position

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 350-352

## Summary

Different positions have different primary risks: long call spread → vega risk (IV change between strikes); short straddle → gamma risk (large moves) and vega risk (IV spike); covered call → delta risk with secondary theta benefit. Bittman advises identifying the one Greek that most threatens the position under adverse scenarios, then either hedging it or setting a dollar-denominated limit for it. This focuses risk management on what matters most.
