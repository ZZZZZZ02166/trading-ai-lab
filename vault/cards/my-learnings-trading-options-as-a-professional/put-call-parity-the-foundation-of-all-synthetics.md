# Put-Call Parity: The Foundation of All Synthetics

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 157-158

## Summary

Put-call parity: +Call − Put = +Stock. Rearranged: Long Stock = Long Call + Short Put (same strike, same expiry). All six basic positions (long/short stock, long/short call, long/short put) can be replicated by combining the other two instruments. This is not a theoretical curiosity — it is the structural relationship that: (1) underlies all arbitrage pricing, (2) determines which form of a position is cheapest, (3) enables market makers to hedge efficiently.
