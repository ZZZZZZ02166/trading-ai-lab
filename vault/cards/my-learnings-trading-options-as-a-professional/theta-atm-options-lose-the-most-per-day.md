# Theta: ATM Options Lose the Most per Day

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 136-138

## Summary

Theta (daily time decay) is most negative for ATM options. Deep ITM and OTM options have smaller absolute theta. The theta curve vs. stock price is an inverted bell shape peaking at the strike. As expiration approaches, ATM theta accelerates — the last few weeks of an ATM option's life see the most rapid dollar decay. This is the income source for short-option traders but the cost for long-option holders.
