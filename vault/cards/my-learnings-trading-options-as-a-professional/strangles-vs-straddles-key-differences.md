# Strangles vs. Straddles: Key Differences

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 50-52

## Summary

Strangle: different strike prices for call and put (e.g., 95 Put + 105 Call). Wider break-even points but cheaper than a straddle. Straddle has higher premium (both options ATM) and narrower break-even but greater probability of profit. Long strangle needs a bigger move to profit; short strangle has a wider profit zone. For given stock, straddle > strangle in premium but straddle expires worthless less often.
