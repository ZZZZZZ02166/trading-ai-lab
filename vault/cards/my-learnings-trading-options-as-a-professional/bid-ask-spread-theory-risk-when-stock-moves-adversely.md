# Bid-Ask Spread Theory: Risk When Stock Moves Adversely

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 294-296

## Summary

Adverse scenario: buy 10 calls at bid 1.85, hedge with −400 shares at 54.01. Stock falls to 53.00 (adverse). Calls fall to 1.46 (bid). Buy back stock at 53.01, sell calls at 1.46 — the stock gain partially offsets call loss. Net P/L depends on whether the delta hedge captured more than the bid-ask loss on the options. Market makers must close positions before the bid-ask edge is eroded by adverse moves.
