# Public Traders vs. Market Makers: Role and Priority

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 35-37

## Summary

Public traders are individuals and institutions without exchange membership. Market makers are exchange members who quote both bids and offers, providing liquidity. Market makers earn the bid-ask spread for their liquidity provision. Exchanges may accept public orders before market maker orders at the same price (customer priority), but market makers can trade ahead at better prices.
