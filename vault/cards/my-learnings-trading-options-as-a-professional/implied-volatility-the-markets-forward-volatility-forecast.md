# Implied Volatility: The Market's Forward Volatility Forecast

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 234-237

## Summary

Implied volatility (IV) is the volatility value that, when plugged into a pricing model with all other known inputs, produces the observed market option price. IV is the market's consensus forecast of future realized volatility over the option's life. Unlike HV (backward-looking), IV is forward-looking. Example from book: Gary calculates theoretical call value at 26% vol = $2.57, but market prices it at $3.40 — implying vol of 32.84%.
