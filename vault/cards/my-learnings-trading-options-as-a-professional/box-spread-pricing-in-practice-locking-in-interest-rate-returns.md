# Box Spread Pricing in Practice: Locking In Interest Rate Returns

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 313-314

## Summary

Box spread present value = (high strike − low strike) × [1 − (interest × days/365)]. A 90–100 box with 60 days and 4% interest: PV = 10 × [1 − (0.04 × 60/365)] = 10 × 0.9934 = 9.93. If the box can be purchased for $9.14, the locked-in profit is $0.79 per share — equivalent to lending at a rate higher than 4%. Institutional traders use box spreads as synthetic money market instruments when rates are favorable.
