# Options as Insurance: The Four Pricing Components

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 73-75

## Summary

Options are priced like insurance policies. Four pricing components: (1) stock price (the asset being insured); (2) strike price (the coverage level); (3) time to expiration (coverage period); (4) volatility (the risk factor — analogous to driving record). Additionally: interest rates and dividends affect pricing. Higher volatility → higher option prices, just as riskier drivers pay more insurance.
