# Bull Call Spread: Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta at Three Time Horizons

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 339-341

## Summary

20 long 70–75 call spreads at three time horizons (expiration, 35 days, 17 days): Delta: peaks ~1,800 between strikes; zero outside. Gamma: positive below $70, negative above $75 (zero crossing between strikes). Vega: bell-shaped positive (max between strikes). Theta: mirror of vega — most negative between strikes. All four Greeks are highest in magnitude when stock is between the strikes — the position's 'hot zone.'
