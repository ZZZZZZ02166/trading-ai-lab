# Seven-Part Option Trade Instruction

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 20-23

## Summary

Unlike a four-part stock order (action, quantity, name, price), an option order requires seven decisions: (1) action (buy-to-open, sell-to-close, etc.), (2) quantity (contracts), (3) underlying, (4) expiration month, (5) strike price, (6) option type (call or put), (7) price per share. Each additional dimension means more ways a position can gain or lose value.
