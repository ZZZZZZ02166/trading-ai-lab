# Synthetic Short Put: Covered Call

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 171-173

## Summary

Short put = long stock + short call (covered call). The covered call position has exactly the same P/L at expiration as a naked short put. Both profit when stock stays at or above the short strike; both suffer the same dollar loss if stock falls below the break-even. This equivalence means: if you sell covered calls for income, you are synthetically short puts — with identical risk.
