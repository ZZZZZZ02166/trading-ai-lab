# Call Values and Stock Price: Positive Correlation with Delta

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 78-79

## Summary

Call values are positively correlated with stock prices: as stock rises, call value rises. The relationship is not linear — it is curved (convex). Delta measures the slope: when stock is $86 and call is far OTM, delta ≈ 0.05 (barely moves). As stock reaches $100 (ATM), delta ≈ 0.55. At $114 (deep ITM), delta approaches 1.00. This convexity (gamma) means call values accelerate faster than delta alone predicts on sharp moves.
