# Vega: Highest for ATM Options and Long-Dated LEAPS

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 128-131

## Summary

Vega is highest for ATM options and increases with time to expiry. A 100 Call has highest vega; a 90 Call or 110 Call has lower vega. Longer-dated options (LEAPS) have much more vega exposure than near-term options. This is why LEAPS are the preferred vehicle for volatility bets: a 1% rise in IV produces a larger dollar change than the same rise for a short-dated option.
