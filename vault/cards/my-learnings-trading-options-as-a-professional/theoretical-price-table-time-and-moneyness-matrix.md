# Theoretical Price Table: Time and Moneyness Matrix

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 67-69

## Summary

A theoretical price table shows option values at a matrix of stock prices (rows) and days to expiry (columns). Example: 100 Call at 20% vol. Reading down a column: ITM options worth more than OTM. Reading across a row: option value decreases as time passes (time decay). At-the-money at 60 days ~$2; at 6 days ~$0.44. Table provides quick reference for strategy evaluation without recalculating.
