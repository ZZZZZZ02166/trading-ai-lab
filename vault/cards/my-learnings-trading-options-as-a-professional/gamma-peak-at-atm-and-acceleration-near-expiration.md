# Gamma Peak at ATM and Acceleration Near Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 125-127

## Summary

Gamma is highest for ATM options and lowest for deep ITM/OTM. The gamma of an ATM option rises exponentially as expiration approaches — in the final 20 days, gamma can spike dramatically. An ATM 100 Call gamma rises from ~0.01 at 160 days to ~0.25+ near expiration. For ITM and OTM options, gamma remains low throughout. This is why short-gamma traders near expiry on ATM positions face amplified risk.
