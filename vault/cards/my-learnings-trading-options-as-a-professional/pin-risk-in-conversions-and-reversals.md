# Pin Risk in Conversions and Reversals

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 210-213

## Summary

Pin risk arises when stock is exactly at the strike at expiration. The market maker holding a conversion doesn't know if the short call will be exercised. If they hedge assuming no assignment and then receive assignment (or vice versa), they end up with an unexpected large stock position overnight. This is not theoretical — it happens at every expiration when stocks close at or near popular strikes. Professional traders monitor short strikes approaching expiration carefully.
