# Speculators vs. Market Makers in Delta-Neutral Trading

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 288-289

## Summary

Two types of delta-neutral traders: (1) Speculators — use IV vs. realized vol forecasts to express a view; assume the risk that their forecast is wrong; hold positions for days to weeks. (2) Market makers — use delta-neutral hedging as a short-term technique to close positions at bid or ask before IV changes adversely; typically hold positions for minutes to hours. Both share the delta-neutral framework but have completely different objectives.
