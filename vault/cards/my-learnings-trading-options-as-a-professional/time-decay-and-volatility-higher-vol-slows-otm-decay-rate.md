# Time Decay and Volatility: Higher Vol Slows OTM Decay Rate

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 87-87

## Summary

Higher volatility slows the percentage decay of OTM options relative to ATM. A 110 Call at 20% vol declines 79% from 56 to 28 days, but only 21% in the final 28 days (most decay front-loaded). At 40% vol, the same 110 Call declines only 54% in the first half and 46% in the second — more even distribution. High-vol OTM options retain value longer. This affects roll timing for traders who sell OTM options.
