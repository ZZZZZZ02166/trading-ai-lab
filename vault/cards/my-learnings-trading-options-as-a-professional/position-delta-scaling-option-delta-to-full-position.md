# Position Delta: Scaling Option Delta to Full Position

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 147-147

## Summary

Position delta = option delta × number of contracts × 100 shares. Example: long 4 XYZ 80 Calls, delta 0.55 → position delta = 4 × 0.55 × 100 = 220. This means the position behaves like being long 220 shares. A $1 stock rise produces approximately $220 gain. Position delta is the primary tool for understanding directional exposure and for calculating hedge ratios.
