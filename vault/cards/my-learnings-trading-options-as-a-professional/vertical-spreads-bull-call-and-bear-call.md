# Vertical Spreads: Bull Call and Bear Call

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 54-55

## Summary

Long vertical call spread (bull call): buy lower-strike call, sell higher-strike call. Limited profit (spread width minus net debit); limited risk (net debit). Short vertical call spread (bear call): sell lower-strike call, buy higher-strike call. Net credit received; profit below lower strike, loss above higher strike. Both have defined risk and reward — the primary advantage over naked calls.
