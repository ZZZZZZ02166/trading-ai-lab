# Arbitrage: Riskless Profit from Mispricing

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 192-193

## Summary

Arbitrage is buying and selling the same economic exposure in different markets simultaneously to capture a risk-free profit. In options: if put-call parity is violated (actual prices differ from theoretical), traders can lock in profit by constructing the appropriate combination. True arbitrage requires no directional view — all Greeks (delta, gamma, vega, theta) approximately cancel. Used by market makers to maintain pricing efficiency.
