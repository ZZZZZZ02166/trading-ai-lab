# Short Box Spread: Borrowing Against the Spread Value

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 217-217

## Summary

Short box = sell bull call spread + sell bear put spread (same strikes). Receives net credit at entry. At expiration, short box always pays out exactly the spread width. Short box is equivalent to borrowing: you receive cash now, pay back the full spread value later. Cost of borrowing = spread value − net credit received. Market makers use short boxes when borrowing rates in the box market are cheaper than other financing.
