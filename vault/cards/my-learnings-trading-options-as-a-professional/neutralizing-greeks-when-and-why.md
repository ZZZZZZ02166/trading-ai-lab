# Neutralizing Greeks: When and Why

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 328-328

## Summary

Any Greek can be neutralized by taking an offsetting position. To neutralize delta: trade stock. To neutralize gamma and vega: trade options (only options change gamma and vega significantly). To neutralize theta: it follows from gamma/vega neutrality. Traders choose which Greek to emphasize: a speculative vol bet keeps vega exposure large; a market-neutral position minimizes all Greeks. The goal of neutralization is to eliminate unintended risk while retaining the desired exposure.
