# Eight Essentials for Professional Options Trading

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 18-19

## Summary

Bittman identifies eight topics every advanced trader must master: (1) option market fundamentals, (2) option price behavior including the Greeks, (3) synthetic relationships, (4) pricing arbitrage strategies, (5) volatility, (6) delta-neutral trading, (7) pricing arbitrage using bid-ask spreads, (8) managing position risk. Mastering all eight separates a professional trader from a retail speculator.
