# Delta and Volatility: High Vol Compresses Delta Toward 0.50

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 123-125

## Summary

Higher volatility compresses all deltas toward 0.50. A deep ITM 90 Call delta falls from ~0.90 at low vol toward 0.50 at high vol. An OTM 110 Call delta rises from near 0 toward 0.50 at high vol. ATM stays at 0.50 regardless. Intuition: in a high-vol environment, any option has a reasonable chance of being anywhere at expiration — so all options approach 50/50 probability of expiring ITM.
