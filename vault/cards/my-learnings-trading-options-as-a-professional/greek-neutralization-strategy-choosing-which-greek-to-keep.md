# Greek Neutralization Strategy: Choosing Which Greek to Keep

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 362-364

## Summary

A trader can selectively neutralize Greeks they don't want while keeping those they do. Example: want to bet on IV rising (keep long vega) but not on direction (neutralize delta with stock) and not on gamma (add offsetting options to reduce gamma). This selective Greek management is how sophisticated traders isolate specific risk factors. The cost of neutralizing unwanted Greeks is the transaction cost of the hedging trades.
