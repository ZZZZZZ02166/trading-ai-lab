# Synthetic Long Stock: Long Call + Short Put

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 161-163

## Summary

Synthetic long stock = long call + short put (same strike, same expiry). P/L at any stock price equals the P/L of owning the actual stock. If interest rates and dividends are zero, this relationship is exact. In practice, the effective stock price of the synthetic depends on the net premium paid/received and the interest component. Used when: synthetics are more liquid than the stock, or to avoid margin requirements.
