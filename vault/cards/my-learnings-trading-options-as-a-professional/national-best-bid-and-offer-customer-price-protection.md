# National Best Bid and Offer: Customer Price Protection

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 303-304

## Summary

NBBO (National Best Bid and Offer) ensures public traders receive the best available price across all exchanges. Market makers must fill public orders at the NBBO or better. If Exchange A quotes 3.40–3.60 and Exchange B quotes 3.45–3.55, a public order to buy will be filled at 3.55 (the lowest ask). This protects public traders but forces market makers to compete aggressively on pricing.
