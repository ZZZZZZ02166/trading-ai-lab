# Butterfly and Condor Spreads: Four-Leg Defined Risk

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 56-57

## Summary

Long butterfly (calls): buy lower strike, sell 2 middle strikes, buy upper strike. Max profit at middle strike; limited loss at extremes. Long condor (calls): buy lowest, sell next two, buy highest. Flat profit plateau between inner strikes; limited loss outside. Both profit from low realized volatility keeping stock near the center. Condor is wider but requires more premium. Both are 'short vol' at the center.
