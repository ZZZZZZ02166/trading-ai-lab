# Normal Distribution and Expected Stock Price Range

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 227-228

## Summary

Stock returns are approximately normally distributed. The 1σ range around the current price defines the expected range with 68% probability. The 2σ range: 95% probability; 3σ: 99%. Using IV as the volatility input: expected 1-day move = IV / √252 × stock price. Expected 1-year range = ±1σ = ±IV × stock price. This is why a $100 stock at 32% IV has an expected 1-year range of $68–$132 (±1σ).
