# Negative Position Gamma: Delta Moves Against You

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 149-150

## Summary

Short options have negative gamma. Example: short 10 QRS 40 Puts, gamma 0.11 → position gamma = −1.10. If stock rises $1: position delta changes from −3.40 to −2.40 (losing $100 delta exposure). On a stock drop, delta becomes more negative (position loses more rapidly). Negative gamma means the position's directional bias moves against the trader on any adverse move — losses compound.
