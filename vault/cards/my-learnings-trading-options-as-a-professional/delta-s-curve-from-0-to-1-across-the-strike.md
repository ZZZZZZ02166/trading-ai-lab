# Delta S-Curve: From 0 to 1 Across the Strike

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 114-114

## Summary

Delta forms an S-curve as stock price changes. Deep OTM call delta ≈ 0; ATM ≈ 0.50; deep ITM ≈ 1.00. The S-shape means delta changes fastest near the strike (where gamma is highest). For puts: S-curve from 0 to -1.00. This characteristic means options become more sensitive to stock moves as they go further in the money — accelerating gains and losses. Traders monitor delta to understand their directional exposure.
