# Position Greeks Table: Calculating Spread Greeks from Components

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 342-344

## Summary

70–75 call spread Greeks: 70 Call at delta 0.535, gamma 0.059, vega 0.087, theta −0.310. Minus 75 Call: delta 0.262, gamma 0.049, vega 0.066, theta −0.225. Net per spread: delta 0.273, gamma 0.010, vega 0.021, theta +0.085. Multiplied by 100 × 20 contracts: position delta = 546, gamma = 20, vega = 42, theta = 170 (daily income). Each Greek is additive across legs — the position's total Greek exposure equals the sum of all component legs.
