# Position Gamma: How Delta Will Change on a Move

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 147-149

## Summary

Position gamma = option gamma × contracts × 100. Example: long 4 XYZ 80 Calls, gamma 0.04 → position gamma = 0.16. If stock rises $1, position delta increases by 0.16 (from 2.20 to 2.36). For puts: long 10 QRS 40 Puts, gamma 0.11 → position gamma = 1.10. If stock rises $1, put delta moves from −3.40 to −2.40. Positive position gamma means you benefit from moves in both directions.
