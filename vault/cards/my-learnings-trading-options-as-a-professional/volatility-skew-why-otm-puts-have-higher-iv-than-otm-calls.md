# Volatility Skew: Why OTM Puts Have Higher IV than OTM Calls

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 247-248

## Summary

Equity option volatility skew: OTM puts have consistently higher implied vol than equidistant OTM calls (downward-sloping IV vs. strike curve). Causes: (1) demand for downside protection (investors buy puts as insurance); (2) asymmetric fear of crashes vs. rallies. Practical implication: a 90-strike put may trade 8+ vol points above a 110-strike call on the same stock. This creates opportunities in risk reversals: sell the rich OTM put, buy the cheap OTM call.
