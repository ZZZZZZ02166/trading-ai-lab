# Synthetic Short Stock: Short Call + Long Put

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 163-164

## Summary

Synthetic short stock = short call + long put (same strike, same expiry). Combined P/L matches short stock P/L. Used when: physically borrowing shares is expensive or unavailable, or to create short exposure in tax-advantaged accounts. Table 5-2 shows that at all stock prices, the combined position matches the short stock position's P/L exactly (before costs).
