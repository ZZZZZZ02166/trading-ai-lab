# Put Values and Stock Price: Inverse Correlation

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 111-111

## Summary

Put values are inversely correlated with stock prices. A 100 Put rises as stock falls below the strike and approaches zero as stock rises far above. At expiration: put value = max(0, strike - stock). Before expiration, put retains time value even when stock is above the strike (OTM). The curve is mirror of the call curve but with a negative slope. Delta for puts is negative (-0.50 at the money, approaches -1.00 deep ITM).
