# Three-Part Risk Analysis: Best Case, Worst Case, Most Likely

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 355-356

## Summary

Before entering any position, analyze three scenarios: (1) Best case — forecast correct, IV favorable, time passes: what is the maximum profit? (2) Worst case — forecast wrong, IV adverse, time passes: what is the maximum loss? (3) Most likely case — stock moves moderately, IV stays roughly constant: what is the expected P/L? This three-part analysis determines if the risk-reward is acceptable and where to place stop-losses.
