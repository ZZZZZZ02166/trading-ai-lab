# Position Theta: Daily Income or Cost in Dollars

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 152-152

## Summary

Position theta = option theta × contracts × 100. Example: short 4 XYZ 80 Calls, theta 0.04 → position theta = +0.16, meaning the position gains $16 per day from time decay. For long 4 calls with same theta: position theta = −0.16 (costs $16/day). Theta is the daily P/L driver for option sellers. Over 30 days, short 4 calls at this theta generate $480 from time decay alone — assuming other factors constant.
