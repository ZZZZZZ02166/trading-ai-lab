# Time Decay: Non-Linear Acceleration Near Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 85-87

## Summary

Time decay is not linear. A 100 Call declines from 6.53 at 90 days to 5.92 at 75 days to 5.25 at 60 days — relatively slowly at first. Near expiration, the same $6.53 option might lose $2+ in the final two weeks. The curve is concave: each day costs more in dollar terms as expiration approaches. This acceleration is most pronounced for ATM options and is the basis of theta strategies.
