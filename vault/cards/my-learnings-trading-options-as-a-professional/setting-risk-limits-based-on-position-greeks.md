# Setting Risk Limits Based on Position Greeks

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 325-326

## Summary

Risk limits should be set in dollar terms for each Greek: max delta exposure (directional risk), max gamma (move risk), max vega (IV risk), max theta (time cost). Example: if position delta is $5,268, a $1 adverse move costs $5,268. If maximum acceptable daily loss is $10,000, the position is at risk from a ~2-point adverse move. Greeks translate directly into dollar risk limits, enabling objective stop-loss decision making.
