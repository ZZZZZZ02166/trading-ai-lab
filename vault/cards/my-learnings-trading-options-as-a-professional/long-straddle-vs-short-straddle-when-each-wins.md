# Long Straddle vs. Short Straddle: When Each Wins

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 47-50

## Summary

Long straddle (long call + long put same strike/expiry): profits from large moves in either direction; loses the full premium if stock stays at the strike. Short straddle (short call + short put): max profit at the strike; unlimited losses in both directions. Long straddle requires a 'big' move either way; short straddle profits from calm, range-bound price action and high IV crushing to lower realized vol.
