# Long Delta-Neutral: Betting on High Realized Volatility

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 260-263

## Summary

Long delta-neutral = long options + short stock. Profits if realized vol exceeds IV (gamma scalping wins exceed theta paid). Best setup: IV is depressed below historical range; catalyst event approaching. P/L from gamma scalps accumulates as stock oscillates. If stock makes large moves repeatedly, gamma scalps compound into significant profit even while theta erodes the position daily.
