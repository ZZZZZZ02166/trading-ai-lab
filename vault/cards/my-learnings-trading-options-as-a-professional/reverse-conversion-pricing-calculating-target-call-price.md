# Reverse Conversion Pricing: Calculating Target Call Price

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 308-309

## Summary

Reverse conversion target call price = stock price − put price + (interest × days/365). If stock = 55, put = 1.50, interest = 4%, 30 days: target call = 55 − 1.50 + 0.18 = 53.68. If market call is below this, a profitable reverse conversion is possible. The formula tells the market maker exactly what price they need for the call to lock in the target profit. Deviations from this price = arbitrage opportunity.
