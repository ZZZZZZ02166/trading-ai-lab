# Position Greeks Change Direction as Stock Crosses Strike

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 358-359

## Summary

A bull call spread's gamma changes sign as the stock crosses the short-call strike. Below the lower strike: positive gamma (benefits from moves). Above the upper strike: negative gamma (hurts from moves). Traders who don't recognize this flip can be surprised when their 'long gamma' spread suddenly becomes 'short gamma' after a big move. Always re-evaluate Greeks whenever stock moves significantly relative to strikes.
