# Position Rho: Dollar P/L from a 1% Rate Change

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 154-154

## Summary

Position rho = option rho × contracts × 100. Example: long 4 XYZ 80 Calls, rho 0.07 → position rho = +0.28. A 1% interest rate rise increases position value by $28. For short 10 QRS 40 Puts, rho −0.02 → position rho = −0.20: a 1% rate rise decreases position value by $20 (puts fall in value with rising rates, hurting short-put holder — less credit than expected). Rho scales with position size like all Greeks.
