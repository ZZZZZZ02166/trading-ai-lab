# Gamma Scalping: Monetizing Realized Volatility

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 271-272

## Summary

Gamma scalping: a long gamma position (long options + short stock delta-neutral) profits by repeatedly rebalancing. As stock rises: delta increases → sell stock to re-neutralize → lock in a small gain. As stock falls: delta decreases → buy stock to re-neutralize → lock in another small gain. Each round-trip scalp generates a small profit. The total of all scalps is gamma P/L. If total scalp profit > theta paid, the long gamma position wins.
