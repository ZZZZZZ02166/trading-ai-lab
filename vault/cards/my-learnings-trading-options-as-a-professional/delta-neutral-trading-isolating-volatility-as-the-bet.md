# Delta-Neutral Trading: Isolating Volatility as the Bet

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 256-257

## Summary

Delta-neutral trading neutralizes directional (stock price) exposure, leaving the position primarily exposed to volatility. Established by: (1) taking an option position (creates delta); (2) trading stock in opposite direction to offset delta. Example: long 20 calls at 50 delta = +1,000 delta → short 1,000 shares → delta-neutral. Remaining exposures: gamma (movement benefit/cost), vega (IV benefit/cost), theta (time decay).
