# Chapter 10: Managing Position Risk — Overview

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 321-322

## Summary

Position risk management for a multi-option portfolio requires: (1) computing position Greeks (delta, gamma, vega, theta, rho) across all positions; (2) understanding how these Greeks change as market conditions change; (3) identifying which Greek creates the most risk; (4) setting risk limits for each Greek; (5) hedging Greeks that exceed limits. Traders must understand how position Greeks evolve — not just their current values.
