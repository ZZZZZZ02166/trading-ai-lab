# Delta: Introduction and Initial Definition

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 82-83

## Summary

Delta is introduced as the ratio of option price change to stock price change. If a 100 Call rises from 6.53 to 7.11 when the stock rises from $100 to $101, the delta is 0.58 (call changed $0.58 per $1 stock move). Delta is not constant — it changes with stock price (captured by gamma). Delta also approximates the probability that the option will expire in the money.
