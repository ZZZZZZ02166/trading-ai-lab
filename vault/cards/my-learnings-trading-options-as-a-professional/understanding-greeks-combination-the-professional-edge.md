# Understanding Greeks Combination — The Professional Edge

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 364-365

## Summary

Bittman's concluding principle: professionals who understand how all five Greeks interact — not just individually — have a decisive edge. A position can look safe on delta but dangerous on gamma; safe on theta but exposed on vega. Only by evaluating the full Greek profile across realistic market scenarios does a trader truly understand what they own. The Greeks are a language for describing risk; fluency in that language is the mark of a professional.
