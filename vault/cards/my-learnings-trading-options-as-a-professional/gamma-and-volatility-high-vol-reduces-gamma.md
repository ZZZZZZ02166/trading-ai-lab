# Gamma and Volatility: High Vol Reduces Gamma

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 127-128

## Summary

Higher volatility reduces gamma for all options. As vol rises, the probability distribution of stock prices widens, making delta change more gradually per dollar move. A deep ITM call at low vol has high gamma (concentrated S-curve); at high vol, the S-curve is more gradual (lower gamma). Counterintuitive: even though high-vol options have large absolute moves, their delta changes per unit are smaller.
