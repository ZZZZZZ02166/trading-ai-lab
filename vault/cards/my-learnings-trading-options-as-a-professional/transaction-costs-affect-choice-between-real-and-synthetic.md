# Transaction Costs Affect Choice Between Real and Synthetic

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 180-181

## Summary

Theoretically, real and synthetic positions are equivalent. In practice, transaction costs (bid-ask spreads, commissions) often favor one over the other. Professionals choose whichever form minimizes transaction costs in the current market. The key insight: knowing the synthetic equivalent helps traders evaluate whether they are paying fair value or whether one form is relatively cheaper at any given moment.
