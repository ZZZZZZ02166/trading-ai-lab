# The Conversion: Locking In Riskless Profit

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 193-193

## Summary

Conversion = long stock + long put + short call (same strike, same expiry). Net delta ≈ 0. P/L is the discrepancy between actual market prices and put-call parity. Example: long stock at $100, long 100 Put at $3.50, short 100 Call at $8.25 — if put-call parity says the call should be worth $8.00, the $0.25 mispricing is a riskless $25 profit per contract. Market makers execute conversions to arbitrage away such discrepancies.
