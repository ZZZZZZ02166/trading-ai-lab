# Arbitrage Is the Enforcer of Put-Call Parity

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 221-222

## Summary

Put-call parity holds not because of theory but because market makers actively exploit any violation through conversions, reversals, and box spreads. The moment a mispricing appears, professional traders with low transaction costs attack it, driving prices back into alignment. For retail traders: the parity relationship constrains option prices in observable ways, ensuring calls and puts at the same strike reflect the same forward price.
