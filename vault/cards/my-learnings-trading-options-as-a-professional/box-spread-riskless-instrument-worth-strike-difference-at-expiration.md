# Box Spread: Riskless Instrument Worth (Strike Difference) at Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 209-210

## Summary

Box spread = bull call spread (buy lower call, sell higher call) + bear put spread (buy higher put, sell lower put), same strikes. At expiration, a 90–100 box is always worth exactly $10 regardless of stock price. Present value = $10 − interest. If the box trades below present value, buying it locks in a risk-free profit equal to the difference. Boxes are pure interest-rate instruments: no directional, gamma, or vega exposure.
