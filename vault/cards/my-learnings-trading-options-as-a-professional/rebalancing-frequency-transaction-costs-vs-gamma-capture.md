# Rebalancing Frequency: Transaction Costs vs. Gamma Capture

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 279-281

## Summary

Delta-neutral positions require rebalancing as stock moves (gamma causes delta to drift). Rebalancing too frequently: transaction costs (bid-ask × shares) eat all gamma scalp profits. Rebalancing too rarely: large directional exposure builds up, defeating the purpose of delta neutrality. Professional rule: rebalance when delta drifts by roughly 1 standard deviation of expected stock movement. This balances cost vs. risk.
