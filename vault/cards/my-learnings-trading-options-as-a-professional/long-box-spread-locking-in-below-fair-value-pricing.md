# Long Box Spread: Locking In Below-Fair-Value Pricing

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 214-214

## Summary

A 90–100 long box (long 90C, short 100C, short 90P, long 100P) costs net debit at entry. If the box is fairly priced: debit = PV($10) = $10 − interest. If market prices make the box cost less than PV, buying the box locks in a risk-free profit. Example from book: box cost $9.14 against PV of $9.93 → $0.79 profit. Boxes are used by large institutions to lend or borrow at effective interest rates determined by market conditions.
