# Position Theta and the Rho-Theta Link with Interest Rates

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 330-331

## Summary

When interest rates are zero: neutralizing gamma also neutralizes theta and vega (they move together). When rates are non-zero: theta also includes an interest-rate component (cost of carry), so neutralizing gamma does not fully neutralize theta. The interest component of theta is linked to rho. For high-rate environments or long-dated positions, carry costs become a meaningful part of daily P/L and must be tracked separately.
