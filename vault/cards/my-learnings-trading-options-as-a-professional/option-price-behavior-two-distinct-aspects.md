# Option Price Behavior: Two Distinct Aspects

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 72-73

## Summary

Option price behavior requires mastering two distinct aspects: (1) how prices change as market conditions change (stock price, time, volatility, interest rates, dividends) — covered in Chapter 3; (2) how the Greeks measure these sensitivities and how they change as conditions change — covered in Chapter 4. A trader who understands only one aspect will be surprised by positions.
