# Theta and Volatility: Higher IV Increases Daily Decay

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 142-144

## Summary

Higher implied volatility increases theta — options with more time premium decay faster in dollar terms. An ATM call at 50% IV has roughly 2.5x the theta of the same call at 20% IV. This confirms the gamma-theta linkage: the same conditions (high vol, ATM, near expiry) that maximize gamma also maximize theta. You cannot have one without the other.
