# Rho: Interest Rate Sensitivity — Calls Benefit, Puts Hurt

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 144-144

## Summary

Rho measures sensitivity to interest rate changes. Call rho is positive (rising rates increase call value); put rho is negative (rising rates decrease put value). Rho is largest for long-dated, deep-ITM options (LEAPS). For short-dated OTM options, rho is nearly zero. Rho matters most for: LEAPS positions, arbitrage pricing of conversions and box spreads, and portfolios held for months to years.
