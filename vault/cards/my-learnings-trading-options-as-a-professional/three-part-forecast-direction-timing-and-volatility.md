# Three-Part Forecast: Direction, Timing, and Volatility

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 96-98

## Summary

Stock trading requires one forecast (direction). Option trading requires three simultaneous forecasts: (1) direction — will stock go up, down, or sideways? (2) timing — will it happen before expiration? (3) volatility — will IV rise or fall? Getting direction right but being wrong on timing or IV can result in a loss even on a correctly directional position. This three-dimensional decision framework is what distinguishes option traders from stock traders.
