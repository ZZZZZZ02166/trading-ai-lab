# IV vs. Realized Vol: The Core Risk of Delta-Neutral Trades

**Type:** caution
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 282-282

## Summary

The primary risk of delta-neutral trading is that the vol forecast is wrong. Long gamma: if IV collapses and realized vol is low → theta destroys the position faster than gamma scalps recover. Short gamma: if realized vol spikes → gamma losses exceed theta collected. Both sides face the core problem: you are not trading the stock; you are trading the relationship between IV and realized vol. Being wrong on that relationship loses money even if the directional hedge works perfectly.
