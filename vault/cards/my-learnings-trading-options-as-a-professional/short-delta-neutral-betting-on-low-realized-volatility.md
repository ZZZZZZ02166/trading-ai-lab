# Short Delta-Neutral: Betting on Low Realized Volatility

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 267-269

## Summary

Short delta-neutral = short options + long stock. Profits if realized vol is less than IV (theta collected exceeds gamma losses from stock moves). Best setup: IV is elevated above historical range; stock is in a calm, range-bound phase. Theta income accumulates daily. Risk: a sudden large move creates gamma losses that may overwhelm the theta collected. Requires active delta hedging to prevent large directional losses.
