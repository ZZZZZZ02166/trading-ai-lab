# Call vs. Put: Rights and Obligations

**Type:** definition
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 26-26

## Summary

Call: gives the owner the right to buy the underlying at the strike price; gives the writer the obligation to sell. Put: gives the owner the right to sell the underlying at the strike price; gives the writer the obligation to buy. All four parties — call buyer, call seller, put buyer, put seller — have distinct P/L profiles and risk characteristics.
