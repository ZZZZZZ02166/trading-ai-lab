# Breakeven Condition: When Gamma Scalps Equal Theta

**Type:** principle
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 275-276

## Summary

Long gamma position breaks even when total gamma scalp profits equal total theta paid. Rule of thumb: the daily realized move must equal approximately the 1-day expected move implied by IV (IV/√252 × stock price). If actual daily moves consistently exceed this threshold → long gamma wins. If actual moves consistently fall short → short gamma (theta collection) wins. This is the fundamental test: will realized vol exceed implied vol?
