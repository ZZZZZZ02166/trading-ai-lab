# Vega and Volatility: Concave Relationship

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 134-136

## Summary

Vega is not constant across volatility levels. It rises from zero, peaks at moderate vol (~30–50%), then declines at extreme vol. Intuition: at very high vol, options are already nearly fully priced for uncertainty; additional vol increases add less and less marginal value. This means traders cannot assume a fixed dollar change per vol point — vega itself changes as IV moves.
