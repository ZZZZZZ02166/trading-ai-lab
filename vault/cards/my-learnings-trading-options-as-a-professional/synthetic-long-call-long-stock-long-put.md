# Synthetic Long Call: Long Stock + Long Put

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 164-166

## Summary

Synthetic long call = long stock + long put (same strike). The long put acts as downside protection; above the strike, position profits like a call. Cost = put premium paid. Example: long stock at $100 + long 100 Put at $3 = synthetic long call with effective premium of $3. Used when calls are unavailable or overpriced relative to puts + stock combination.
