# Historical Volatility: Annualized Standard Deviation of Returns

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 224-225

## Summary

Historical volatility (HV) is the annualized standard deviation of daily logarithmic returns over a specified period. Common windows: 30-day, 90-day, 180-day, 300-day HV. Calculation: (1) compute daily returns as ln(today/yesterday); (2) compute standard deviation; (3) annualize by multiplying by √252 (or √256). HV is backward-looking and describes what the stock has done — not what it will do.
