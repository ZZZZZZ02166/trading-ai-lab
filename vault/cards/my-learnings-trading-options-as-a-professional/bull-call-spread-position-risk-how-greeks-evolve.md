# Bull Call Spread Position Risk: How Greeks Evolve

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 324-324

## Summary

For 20 long 70–75 call spreads: (1) Position value peaks between strikes. (2) Delta peaks when stock is between strikes (both options ATM-ish). (3) Gamma is positive below lower strike (long call dominates), negative above upper strike (short call dominates), zero between. (4) Vega positive between strikes, near zero outside. (5) Theta mirrors vega — most negative between strikes. Understanding these dynamics prevents surprises when the stock moves into or out of the spread range.
