# Setting Bid and Ask Prices Using Theoretical Value

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 299-300

## Summary

Market makers set bids and asks around their theoretical value (TV). A call with TV = 3.50 might be quoted 3.40–3.60. The market maker will buy at 3.40 (TV − edge) and sell at 3.60 (TV + edge). Edge = bid-ask spread / 2. If the market maker can consistently trade at both bid and ask, profit = 2 × edge per round-trip. Competitors narrow spreads over time — profitable market making requires low transaction costs and efficient hedging.
