# Interest Rate Effect on Synthetic Positions: Effective Stock Price

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 178-180

## Summary

With non-zero interest rates, synthetics diverge slightly from real positions. A synthetic long call (long stock + long put) has an effective premium equal to the put premium plus interest on the stock price. If interest rates are 5% and the position is held for 30 days, the effective cost includes ~1/12 of 5% × stock price. This explains why calls trade above put value by the interest carry amount.
