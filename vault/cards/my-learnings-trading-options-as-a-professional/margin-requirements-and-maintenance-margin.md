# Margin Requirements and Maintenance Margin

**Type:** concept
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 40-41

## Summary

Initial margin: minimum equity required to open a marginable position (e.g., 50% for stock purchases). Maintenance margin: minimum equity required to keep the position open (lower than initial, e.g., 25%). A margin call occurs when account equity falls below maintenance margin — broker requires deposit or liquidation. Option sellers face margin requirements because of potential assignment obligations.
