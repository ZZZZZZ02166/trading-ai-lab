# Two-Trade Arbitrage: Creating Conversions Across Exchanges

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 314-314

## Summary

Conversions can be executed in two separate trades to capture edge. Trade 1: buy stock + sell call (or buy put). Trade 2: complete the other leg. By splitting the trade, the market maker can capture the bid-ask spread on each leg separately. Total profit = sum of edges on each component minus transaction costs. This requires speed and execution precision — the opportunity closes as prices adjust.
