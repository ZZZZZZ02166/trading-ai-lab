# Rho and Volatility: Higher Vol Reduces Rho

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** My Learnings   Trading Options As A Professional — pp. 144-144

## Summary

Rho decreases as volatility rises. At high vol, the intrinsic/carry component of option value is a smaller fraction of total value (time premium dominates). This makes rho less meaningful in high-IV environments. For practical trading: rho matters when rates change significantly or when pricing long-term positions. In short-dated, high-vol environments, rho is the least important Greek.
