# Profile the Whole Rejection to Find the Fight

**Source** [PDF pp. 141–145](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=141)

## Summary

Apply a flexible profile over a strong rejected probe and its reversal to find the heaviest traded band inside the event. This can expose levels hidden by different candle time frames. Dale describes the method as harder to apply when several concentrations compete.
