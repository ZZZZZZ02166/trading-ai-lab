# Absorption and Initiative Form Different Stages

**Source** [PDF pp. 95–98](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=95)

## Summary

Dale describes a sequence in which passive interest first stalls a move and later aggressive traders push price away. The second stage supplies directional follow-through beyond the first stall. The footprint examples mark both stages separately.
