# The Footprint Shows Executions at Each Price

**Source** [PDF pp. 25–26](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=25)

## Summary

A footprint keeps the candle's price range and layers executed bid and ask quantity onto each price row. This reveals where within the bar activity was concentrated. A normal candle only shows the aggregate price movement.
