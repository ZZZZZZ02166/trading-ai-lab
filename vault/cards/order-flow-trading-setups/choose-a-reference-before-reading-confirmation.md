**Source** [PDF pp. 80–81](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=80)

## Summary

Dale first identifies support or resistance with a primary method, then looks at order flow when price arrives. The four confirmation families are large passive orders, absorption, aggressive orders with delta, and cumulative-delta divergence. His presented confirmation setups depend on the location.
