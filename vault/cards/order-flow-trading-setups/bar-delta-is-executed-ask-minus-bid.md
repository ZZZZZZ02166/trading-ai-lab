# Bar Delta Is Executed Ask Minus Bid

**Source** [PDF pp. 27–30](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=27)

## Summary

Bar delta sums ask-side volume and subtracts bid-side volume across the footprint. A positive value indicates more buy-initiated execution, though the candle can still close lower. Rotations can make the relationship between delta and candle direction less reliable.
