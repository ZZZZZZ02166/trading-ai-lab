**Source** [PDF pp. 121–124](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=121)

## Summary

A horizontal histogram bins executions by price across a selected interval. The thick portions show where more traded, whereas ordinary volume bars total activity by time interval. The profile identifies candidate price zones for further assessment.
