# Watch Heavy Two-Sided Volume Where Price Stalls

**Source** [PDF pp. 87–91](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=87)

## Summary

Dale's absorption setup looks for unusually heavy traded volume on both bid and ask around preselected support or resistance, while the previous push stalls. The evidence may take several minutes to appear. It is strongest as a response to a level already identified.
