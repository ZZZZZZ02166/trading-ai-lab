# Profile the Rotation Before a Directional Departure

**Source** [PDF pp. 130–136](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=130)

## Summary

Dale profiles the pre-move range and highlights its heaviest traded band once price breaks out. A later return to that band becomes a support or resistance candidate. The book pairs the return with a separate order-flow confirmation.
