# Aggressive and Passive Orders Share Each Print

**Source** [PDF pp. 20–24](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=20)

## Summary

A market buy executes against a resting sell limit at the ask. A market sell executes against a resting buy limit at the bid. The print locates the transaction and its aggressive side, while the price response helps infer whether the passive side mattered.
