# Treat Unfinished Business as a Nearby Reference

**Source** [PDF pp. 71–74](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=71)

## Summary

Dale uses an unfinished extreme in target planning, stop placement, and trade screening. Its presence can support a revisit hypothesis when price is already near it, but the market may first move far away. The reference alone gives no dependable entry timing.
