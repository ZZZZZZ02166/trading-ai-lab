# Multiple Nodes Share One Price Across Bars

**Source** [PDF pp. 35–36](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=35)

## Summary

A double or triple node forms when the maximum-volume row repeats at the same price in successive footprints. A coincident level in separated bars is a different measurement. Dale treats this repetition as an area to watch when price returns.
