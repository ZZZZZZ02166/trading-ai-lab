# Require Separation Before a Multiple-Node Retest

**Source** [PDF pp. 55–59](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=55)

## Summary

Dale looks for at least two consecutive bar nodes at the same price in a trend, pre-trend region, or strong rejection. His setup then waits for two entire footprints to form away from the node and considers its first later revisit. The approach determines the proposed side.
