# Check Unfinished Extremes Before Placing Stops

**Source** [PDF pp. 73–79](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=73)

## Summary

Dale checks whether a nearby unfinished extreme lies just beyond a proposed protective stop. The point is to understand whether a test of that extreme would also invalidate the chosen positioning. It remains secondary to the main setup and risk distance.
