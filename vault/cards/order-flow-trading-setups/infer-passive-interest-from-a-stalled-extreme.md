# Infer Passive Interest From a Stalled Extreme

**Source** [PDF pp. 81–86](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=81)

## Summary

At resistance a large ask print can accompany aggressive buying into a passive seller while price fails to extend. At support the mirrored example shows large bid volume during a stalled sell push. Unusually large is relative to the instrument and the session.
