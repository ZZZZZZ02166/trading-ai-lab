# Look for Aggression Away From the Reference

**Source** [PDF pp. 92–95](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=92)

## Summary

Dale watches for dominant bid-side execution and negative delta at resistance, or dominant ask-side execution and positive delta at support. He reads the response within five-minute footprints in these examples. The proposed aggression is in the direction of the reaction away from the level.
