# Profile a Pause Within an Established Trend

**Source** [PDF pp. 137–140](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=137)

## Summary

A strong directional move can leave a thin distribution with a relatively large bump where it paused. Dale focuses on that local concentration for a potential continuation retest. The underlying trend and the actual response at the band are both part of his setup.
