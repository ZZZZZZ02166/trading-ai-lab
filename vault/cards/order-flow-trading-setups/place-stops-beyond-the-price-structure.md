# Place Stops Beyond the Price Structure

**Source** [PDF pp. 115–120](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=115)

## Summary

The book compares a fixed stop, a stop beyond the support or resistance extreme, and a stop beyond a heavy-volume band. The structural approaches tie the stop to the trade premise. Dale checks whether the resulting distance is sensible for the instrument and market conditions.
