# Unfinished Business Is a Footprint-Extreme Test

**Source** [PDF pp. 38–41](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=38)

## Summary

Dale expects zero executed bid volume at a finished footprint high and zero executed ask volume at a finished low. He marks an extreme with prints on both sides as unfinished business. The mark is a level to track, and revisits can take time or fail to occur.
