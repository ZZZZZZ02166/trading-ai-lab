# A Stack Groups Neighboring Same-Side Imbalances

**Source** [PDF pp. 37–38](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=37)

## Summary

Adjacent imbalance levels on the same side form a stack. Dale describes the usual display as three or more, while the trading instructions allow the configured trigger to vary. The stack highlights a band of unusually one-sided execution.
