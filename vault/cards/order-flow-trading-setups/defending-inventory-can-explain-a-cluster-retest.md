# Defending Inventory Can Explain a Cluster Retest

**Source** [PDF pp. 47–48](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=47)

## Summary

Dale offers two explanations for a reaction at a prior cluster: earlier participants may defend their positions, and opposing traders may close as price approaches. Both are explanations for the observed reaction. The chart directly shows concentration, departure, return, and response.
