# Aim at the Near Edge of Prior Volume

**Source** [PDF pp. 105–109](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=105)

## Summary

When a trade approaches a zone with heavy historical activity, Dale considers taking profit before or at its leading edge. The zone may interrupt the move. A target selected from that distribution inherits the profile's window and price bins.
