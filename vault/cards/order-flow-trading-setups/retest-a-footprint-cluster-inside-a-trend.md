# Retest a Footprint Cluster Inside a Trend

**Source** [PDF pp. 46–50](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=46)

## Summary

Find relatively heavy trading inside a decisive directional leg, then observe whether price separates from the band. Dale waits for a pullback to its initial boundary or heavier portion. He aligns the setup with the direction of the leg.
