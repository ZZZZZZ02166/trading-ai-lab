# Track Retests of Filtered Large Execution Areas

**Source** [PDF pp. 60–64](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=60)

## Summary

Identify a large execution with the trades filter, then wait for one or two entire footprints to build outside the marked area. Measure the first pullback to the zone after that separation. Dale uses the approach direction to distinguish support from resistance.
