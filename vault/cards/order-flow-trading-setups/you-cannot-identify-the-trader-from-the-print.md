# You Cannot Identify the Trader From the Print

**Source** [PDF pp. 24–25](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=24)

## Summary

A conspicuous ask-side execution could reflect buying initiative or the ability of a passive seller to absorb it. The number identifies the traded size, and the price response supplies separate information. Dale treats participant identity and intent as inferences.
