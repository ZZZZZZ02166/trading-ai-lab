# Profile Shape Describes Where Business Concentrated

**Source** [PDF pp. 125–128](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=125)

## Summary

Dale associates D with a broad middle distribution, P with a higher bulge, lowercase b with a lower bulge, and thin profiles with directional travel. Each shape summarizes where volume accumulated. The time window affects which shape is visible.
