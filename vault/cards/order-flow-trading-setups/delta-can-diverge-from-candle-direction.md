# Delta Can Diverge From Candle Direction

**Source** [PDF pp. 28–30](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=28)

## Summary

An up bar with negative delta or a down bar with positive delta shows price and executed aggression are telling different stories. Dale uses this discrepancy to investigate a reaction near a planned level. The example pairs an advancing price with weaker delta at resistance.
