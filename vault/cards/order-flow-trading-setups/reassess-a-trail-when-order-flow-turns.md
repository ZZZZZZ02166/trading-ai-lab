# Reassess a Trail When Order Flow Turns

**Source** [PDF pp. 110–114](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=110)

## Summary

Dale trails a favorable position while aggressive execution still supports the move. Opposing imbalance, renewed absorption, or a delta reversal near a significant price can change that read. The decision is conditioned on price location and progress.
