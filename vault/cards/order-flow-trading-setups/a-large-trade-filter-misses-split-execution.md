# A Large-Trade Filter Misses Split Execution

**Source** [PDF pp. 60–61](../../../books/Order-Flow-Trading-Setups-1.pdf#page=60)

## Summary

The filter highlights single executions above a configurable size. Dale's 25-lot illustration is calibrated to his currency-futures example, and activity varies by instrument and session. Small clips and iceberg replenishment can be missed by a large-print cutoff.
