# Gamma Scalping: Trading Realized Volatility

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 260-261

## Summary

Long gamma trader sells stock when price rises (locks in positive delta gain), buys stock when price falls (re-neutralizes). Each rebalance captures a realized-move profit. Net P/L = sum of all stock scalps − total theta paid. Profitable if realized vol > implied vol.
