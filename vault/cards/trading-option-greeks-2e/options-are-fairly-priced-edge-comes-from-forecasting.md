# Options Are Fairly Priced: Edge Comes From Forecasting

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 108-109

## Summary

Options are not systematically overpriced or underpriced in aggregate. Neither always-buy nor always-sell strategies have inherent edge. Edge comes from correctly forecasting vol, direction, or timing relative to what the market has priced in.
