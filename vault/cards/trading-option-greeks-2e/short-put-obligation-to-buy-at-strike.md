# Short Put: Obligation to Buy at Strike

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 35-37

## Summary

Selling a put creates an obligation to buy shares at the strike price if assigned. Break-even = strike − premium. Max profit = premium collected; max loss = strike − premium (effectively owning stock above market). Short puts are bullish and equivalent to covered calls via put-call parity.
