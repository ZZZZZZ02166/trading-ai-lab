# More Theta Means More Gamma Near Expiration

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 116-118

## Summary

As expiration approaches with stock near the strike: theta rises (more daily reward) AND gamma rises (more directional risk). They are inextricably linked. The best theta-collecting period is also the most gamma-dangerous period.
