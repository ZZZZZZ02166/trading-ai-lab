# Diagonal Spread: Calendar With Strike Offset

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 238-239

## Summary

Buy far-month option at one strike + sell near-month at a different strike. Combines calendar characteristics (time spread, long vega) with vertical spread characteristics (directional delta). Bullish diagonal: buy lower-strike back-month, sell higher-strike front-month for income.
