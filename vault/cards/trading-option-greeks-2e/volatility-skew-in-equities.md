# Volatility Skew in Equities

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 86-87

## Summary

Equity skew: OTM puts trade at higher IV than ATM; ATM higher than OTM calls. Reflects demand for crash protection and fear. OTM puts can trade 10+ vol points above equidistant OTM calls. Known as a 'smirk' rather than a symmetric smile. Skew creates exploitable pricing discrepancies.
