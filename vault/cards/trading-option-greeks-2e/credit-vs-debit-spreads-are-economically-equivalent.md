# Credit vs. Debit Spreads Are Economically Equivalent

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 190-191

## Summary

Bull call spread (debit) and bull put spread (credit) with the same strikes have identical at-expiration payoff profiles. The difference is premium flow direction and margin treatment. Choose based on bid-ask spreads, margin, and liquidity—not based on credit vs. debit preference.
