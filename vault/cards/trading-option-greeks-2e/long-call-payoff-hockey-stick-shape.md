# Long Call Payoff: Hockey-Stick Shape

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 27-29

## Summary

Every long call at expiration has a hockey-stick payoff: flat at −premium (max loss) below the strike, then a 45-degree rising line above the strike. Break-even = strike + premium paid. Maximum loss is the premium; upside is theoretically unlimited.
