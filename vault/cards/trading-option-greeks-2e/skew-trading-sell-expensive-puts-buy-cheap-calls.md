# Skew Trading: Sell Expensive Puts, Buy Cheap Calls

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 328-329

## Summary

Equity vol skew makes OTM puts trade 4+ vol points above equidistant OTM calls. Risk reversal (sell OTM put, buy OTM call) profits if skew normalizes, regardless of stock direction. Can be delta-hedged to isolate the pure skew trade.
