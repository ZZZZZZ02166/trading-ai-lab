# Delta-Neutral Trading: Isolate Volatility Exposure

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 244-245

## Summary

Neutralize directional exposure by hedging delta with the underlying. The position then profits or loses primarily from changes in IV (vega) and/or magnitude of realized moves (gamma/theta)—not from direction. Requires continuous rebalancing as delta drifts.
