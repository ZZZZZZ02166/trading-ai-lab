**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 336-339

## Summary

From finding trades, to planning execution, to managing and adjusting, to planning exits—greeks are the vocabulary and toolkit of professional option trading. They quantify risk, help identify edge, and allow customization of risk/reward to match any market forecast.
