# Delta: Four Equivalent Definitions

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 41-41

## Summary

(1) Rate of change of option value per $1 move in underlying; (2) slope of option price vs. underlying graph; (3) equivalent underlying shares per contract (delta × 100 for equity options); (4) approximate probability of expiring ITM (trader's heuristic, not exact). Call deltas: 0 to +1. Put deltas: −1 to 0.
