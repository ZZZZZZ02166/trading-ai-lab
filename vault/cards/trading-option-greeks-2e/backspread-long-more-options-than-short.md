# Backspread: Long More Options Than Short

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 314-315

## Summary

Sell fewer options at one strike, buy more at a further strike (e.g., 1×2: sell 1 call, buy 2 calls at higher strike). Benefits from large directional moves AND rising IV. Used as a hedge against big moves or as a pure volatility-buying structure.
