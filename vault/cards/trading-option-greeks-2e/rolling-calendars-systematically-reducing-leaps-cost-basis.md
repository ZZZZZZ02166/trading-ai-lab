# Rolling Calendars: Systematically Reducing LEAPS Cost Basis

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 225-226

## Summary

Sell front-month calls against a LEAPS call each month. After 3 months collecting 1.50/month against a 4.00 LEAPS, aggregate credit = 4.50 > original cost—the long option is essentially 'free.' Risk: stock must stay near the short strike each month.
