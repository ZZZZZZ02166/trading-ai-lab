# Would I Do It Now? Rule: Eliminate Sunk Cost Thinking

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 335-336

## Summary

At any point while holding a position, ask: 'If I didn't have this position, would I enter it now at current prices?' If the answer is no, close it. This cuts through sunk-cost rationalization, hope-based holding, and cognitive bias. It forces honest reassessment of whether the original thesis still holds.
