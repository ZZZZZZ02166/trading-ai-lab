# Vol Pattern 3: Realized Rises, IV Falls (Post-Earnings Crush)

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 280-282

## Summary

Stock becomes more volatile, yet options become cheaper. Classic post-earnings: direction surprises but vol collapses after the announcement. Long gamma position before earnings benefits from realized outpacing IV. Short vol here would be painful.
