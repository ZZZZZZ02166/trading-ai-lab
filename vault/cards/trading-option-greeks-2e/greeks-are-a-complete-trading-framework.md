# Greeks Are a Complete Trading Framework

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 330-331

## Summary

Greeks help at every stage: find trades (identify vol mispricing), plan execution (size positions), manage positions (monitor risk evolution), and plan exits (identify when the thesis is invalidated). Without greek literacy, option trading is guesswork.
