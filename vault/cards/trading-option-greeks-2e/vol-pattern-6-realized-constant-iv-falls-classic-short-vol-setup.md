# Vol Pattern 6: Realized Constant, IV Falls (Classic Short-Vol Setup)

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 284-285

## Summary

IV falls while realized vol stays stable = vol premium is compressing. IV has been elevated and begins reverting to match realized, creating a profitable short-vol window. Two IV-to-realized convergences on one chart signal multiple entry opportunities.
