# Vol Pattern 1: Both IV and Realized Rise

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 277-278

## Summary

Options are expensive in absolute terms but may reflect legitimate fear. If realized rises faster than IV: market underestimates actual moves—buy vol. If IV rises faster than realized: options are overpriced—vol-selling opportunity, but caution: realized may still catch up.
