# Delta as ITM Probability Approximation

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 41-43

## Summary

Delta ≈ probability of expiring ITM (simplified, not mathematically exact). 0.50 delta ATM call ≈ 50% chance ITM at expiry. 0.20 delta OTM call ≈ 20% chance. Useful heuristic for quick risk assessment, but ignores the distinction between risk-neutral and real-world probability.
