# Selling Calendars: Unique Risk When Front Is Cheaper

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 230-231

## Summary

If front-month IV is 17% and back-month IV is 25%, selling the calendar (buy front, sell back) seems attractive. But selling time spreads when back-month is elevated carries its own risk: if the back-month IV rises further, the short back-month hurts more than the long front-month helps.
