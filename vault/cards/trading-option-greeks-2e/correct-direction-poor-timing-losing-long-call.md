# Correct Direction + Poor Timing = Losing Long Call

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 179-180

## Summary

A trader can be right on direction and still lose money on a long call if theta erodes the premium before the move. Vertical spreads have much lower theta, making timing less critical. Trade-off: spreads also reduce sensitivity to large moves.
