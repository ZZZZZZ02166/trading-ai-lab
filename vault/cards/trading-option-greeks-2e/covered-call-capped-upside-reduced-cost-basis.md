# Covered Call: Capped Upside, Reduced Cost Basis

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 31-32

## Summary

Covered call = long stock + short call. Short call caps upside at the strike but reduces cost basis by the premium collected. If stock rises above the strike, shares get called away. Break-even = stock purchase price − premium received.
