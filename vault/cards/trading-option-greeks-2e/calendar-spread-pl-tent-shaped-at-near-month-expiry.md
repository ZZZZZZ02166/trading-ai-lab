# Calendar Spread P/L: Tent-Shaped at Near-Month Expiry

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 220-222

## Summary

At near-month expiration: profit is highest if stock is at the short strike; losses accumulate if stock moves far away in either direction. After near-month expiration, trader owns the back-month option at a reduced cost basis = (original spread debit − time value captured).
