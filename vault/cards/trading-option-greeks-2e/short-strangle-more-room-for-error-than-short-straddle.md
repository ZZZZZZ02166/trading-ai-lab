# Short Strangle: More Room for Error Than Short Straddle

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 309-310

## Summary

Sell OTM call + OTM put. More premium than vertical spreads but unlimited risk. Key advantage: wider strikes provide wiggle room—stock can move moderately without losing. Best in high-IV environments when the stock is expected to stay within the short strikes.
