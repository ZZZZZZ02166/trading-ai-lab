# Short Gamma Forces Buying High, Selling Low

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 267-268

## Summary

Short calls + long stock delta hedge: when stock rises, delta goes negative—must buy stock at rising prices. When stock falls, must sell at falling prices. Short gamma systematically forces buy-high/sell-low trades. Theta income compensates for this structural disadvantage.
