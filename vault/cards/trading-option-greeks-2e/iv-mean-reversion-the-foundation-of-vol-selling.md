# IV Mean Reversion: The Foundation of Vol Selling

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 80-81

## Summary

IV oscillates around a characteristic range for each underlying. When IV deviates significantly above the mean → vol-selling opportunity. When depressed below the mean → vol-buying opportunity. Mean reversion is the foundation of most short-vol strategies.
