# Backspread Greeks Flip With Stock Movement

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 317-317

## Summary

A call backspread starts with positive gamma and positive vega. If the stock falls far below the short strike, vega and gamma turn negative—the position transforms from 'long vol' to 'short vol.' Understand the full risk profile across all price scenarios before entering.
