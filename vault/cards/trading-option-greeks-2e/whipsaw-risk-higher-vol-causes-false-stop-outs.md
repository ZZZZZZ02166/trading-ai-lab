# Whipsaw Risk: Higher Vol Causes False Stop-Outs

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 120-121

## Summary

In volatile markets, stop-loss prices can be reached due to random noise rather than real directional moves. Trader exits for a loss; market reverses; trade would have recovered. Higher IV → wider effective stops needed to avoid whipsaw, but wider stops = larger potential losses.
