# When to Buy a Straddle: IV Below Historical Range

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 295-296

## Summary

Buy straddles when IV is depressed below its historical range AND below recent realized vol. Goal: profit from IV normalizing upward (vega) or from large realized moves via gamma scalping. Key: enter early in a low-IV trend, not after IV has already started rising.
