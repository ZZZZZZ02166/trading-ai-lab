# Gamma: Rate of Delta Change

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 51-54

## Summary

Gamma = rate of change of delta per $1 move in underlying. Highest for ATM options near expiration; lowest for deep ITM/OTM. Long options have positive gamma (helpful convexity). Short options have negative gamma. Gamma causes large moves to benefit option buyers more than initial delta suggests.
