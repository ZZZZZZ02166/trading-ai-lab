# Protective Put: Portfolio Insurance

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 37-38

## Summary

Long stock + long put = a floor on downside. The put limits losses to (stock purchase price − strike + premium paid). Cost = put premium, analogous to insurance. Useful when bullish long-term but concerned about near-term volatility.
