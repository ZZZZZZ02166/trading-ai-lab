# Rho Scales With Time to Expiration

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 65-66

## Summary

22-day call rho ≈ 0.015. 2-year LEAPS rho ≈ 0.858. Rho increases with time because interest accrues over a longer period. In rising-rate environments, LEAPS traders must account for rho as a meaningful P/L factor.
