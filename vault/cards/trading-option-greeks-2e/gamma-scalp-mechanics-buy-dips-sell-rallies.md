# Gamma Scalp Mechanics: Buy Dips, Sell Rallies

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 261-263

## Summary

Long 20 ATM calls + short 1,000 shares. Stock falls $2: delta goes negative via gamma. Trader buys shares to re-neutralize (buys the dip). Stock rallies $3: delta goes positive. Trader sells shares (sells the rally). Each buy-low/sell-high cycle funds the theta cost.
