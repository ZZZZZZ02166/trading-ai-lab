# Bull Call Spread: AAPL 395-405 Example

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 175-177

## Summary

Buy AAPL Feb 395 call @ 14.60, sell Feb 405 call @ 10.20. Net debit: $4.40. Max profit: $5.60 (width − debit). Max loss: $4.40. Break-even: $399.40. Saves $10.20 vs. buying the outright call; sacrifices unlimited profit above $405.
