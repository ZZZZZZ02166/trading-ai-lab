# Comparing IV Across Expiration Months for Short-Vol Entry

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 122-124

## Summary

When selling vol, compare IV across months. March at 19.2% IV vs. April/May at 23%: March is cheapest by IV, offering less premium but possibly better risk-adjusted value. Higher-IV months reflect more uncertainty—decide whether the extra premium compensates for the added risk.
