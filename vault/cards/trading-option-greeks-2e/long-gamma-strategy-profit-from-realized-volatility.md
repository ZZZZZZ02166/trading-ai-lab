# Long Gamma Strategy: Profit From Realized Volatility

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 245-246

## Summary

Long options + short delta hedge. Profits if realized vol > implied vol priced into the options. Each stock move enables a profitable hedge rebalance (gamma scalp). Risk: if stock moves less than expected, theta erodes the premium. Breakeven requires enough realized movement to cover total theta paid.
