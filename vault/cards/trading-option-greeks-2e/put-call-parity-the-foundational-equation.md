# Put-Call Parity: The Foundational Equation

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 62-63

## Summary

c + PV(strike) = p + S. Every call and put with the same strike and expiration are mathematically linked. Mispricing of one creates a riskless arbitrage. Rearranged: call = put + stock − strike + interest − dividends. This is the basis for all synthetic positions.
