# Long Strangle: OTM Call + OTM Put

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 305-307

## Summary

Lower cost than a straddle; requires a larger stock move to profit (break-evens farther from current price). Lower vega per dollar spent. Use when expecting a large move but wanting to reduce premium at risk. Trade-off: wider break-evens in exchange for less upfront cost.
