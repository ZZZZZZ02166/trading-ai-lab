# Option Contract: Right Without Obligation

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 18-20

## Summary

An option gives the holder the right—but not the obligation—to buy (call) or sell (put) a fixed quantity of an underlying asset at a specified strike price on or before expiration. Standard equity contracts represent 100 shares. For every long contract there is a corresponding short contract—they are equally common.
