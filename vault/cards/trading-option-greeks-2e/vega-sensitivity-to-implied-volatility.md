# Vega: Sensitivity to Implied Volatility

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 59-60

## Summary

Vega = change in option value per 1 percentage-point change in IV. Long options: positive vega (benefit from rising IV). Short options: negative vega. ATM options have the highest vega; deep ITM/OTM have less. LEAPS have much higher vega than short-dated options.
