# Iron Condor Strike Selection: 1 Standard Deviation Rule

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 208-210

## Summary

With QQQ at $55.95, 31 DTE, IV=23.2%, 35 trading days: 1σ ≈ $4.05. Short strikes at ±1σ: sell 55 put and 57 call; buy 54 put and 58 call for protection. Net credit: $0.63. Places short strikes at the boundary of the 'expected range' at expiration.
