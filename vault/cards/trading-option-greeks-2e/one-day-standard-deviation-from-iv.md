# One-Day Standard Deviation from IV

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 77-79

## Summary

Formula: IV / √(trading days/year) = 1-day expected σ. For $100 stock at 32% IV: 32 / 16 = 2%. ~68% chance of <$2 daily move. Useful for setting daily-move expectations and for gamma-scalping breakeven calculations.
