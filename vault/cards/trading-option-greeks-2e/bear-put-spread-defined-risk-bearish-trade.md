# Bear Put Spread: Defined-Risk Bearish Trade

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 183-185

## Summary

Buy higher-strike put, sell lower-strike put. Profits if stock falls to or below the short strike. Max profit at or below the short strike; max loss above the long strike. Lower cost and lower vega than outright puts. Best for modest bearish moves.
