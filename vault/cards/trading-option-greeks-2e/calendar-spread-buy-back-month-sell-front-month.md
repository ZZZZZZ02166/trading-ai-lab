# Calendar Spread: Buy Back Month, Sell Front Month

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 219-220

## Summary

Same strike, different expirations. Short front-month option decays faster than the long back-month (theta differential). Also a vega play: long calendar benefits from rising back-month IV relative to front-month. Maximum profit at expiration if stock is exactly at the short strike.
