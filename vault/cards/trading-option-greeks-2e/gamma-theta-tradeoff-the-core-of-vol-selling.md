# Gamma-Theta Tradeoff: The Core of Vol Selling

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 110-112

## Summary

Vol-selling strategies profit from theta (positive daily decay) but suffer from gamma (losses from stock movement). These forces are inseparable: more theta income = more gamma risk. This is the fundamental tension in every short-volatility position.
