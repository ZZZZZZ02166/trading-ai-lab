# Higher Volatility Means Higher Theta

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 57-59

## Summary

Higher vol = higher option prices = more time value = higher daily dollar decay. A $2 option decays faster in dollar terms than a $0.50 option. This is the flip side of gamma-theta: high vol = high gamma AND high theta. The leverage you pay for comes at a daily cost.
