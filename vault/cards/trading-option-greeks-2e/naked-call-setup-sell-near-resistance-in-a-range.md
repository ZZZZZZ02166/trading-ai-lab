# Naked Call Setup: Sell Near Resistance in a Range

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 112-113

## Summary

Sell a call when you believe the stock will stay below the strike at expiration. Best near the top of a trading channel/resistance. Greeks: negative delta (bearish-neutral), positive theta (daily income), negative gamma (risk from upside), negative vega (profits if IV falls). Max profit = premium collected.
