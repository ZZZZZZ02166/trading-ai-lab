# Risk Priority for Long Option Buyers

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 87-89

## Summary

Priority: (1) delta = directional exposure, primary concern; (2) theta = time decay working against you; (3) vega = IV risk; (4) gamma = helpful on strong moves. Being correct on direction is not enough—theta and vega can cause losses if timing is off.
