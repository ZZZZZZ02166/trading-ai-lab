# Vol-Buying Setup: Rising Intraday Vol, Flat Close-to-Close HV

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 331-333

## Summary

If intraday (high-low) vol increases significantly but close-to-close HV hasn't risen, the market may be underpricing actual activity. Buy delta-neutral options while maintaining slight directional bias. Divergences between different HV measures can signal mispriced IV.
