# Naked Call: Unlimited Risk, Limited Reward

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 29-31

## Summary

Short naked call = limited premium collected, theoretically unlimited loss if stock rises. Profits only if stock stays below the strike at expiration. A covered call (long stock + short call) eliminates the unlimited-risk component because stock gains offset short-call losses above the strike.
