# Vertical Spread Crushes Vega and Gamma

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 177-177

## Summary

AAPL 395 call alone: gamma=0.0097, vega=0.513. AAPL 395-405 spread: gamma=0.0001, vega=0.020. The spread reduces vega by 96% and gamma by 99%. Use spreads when directional conviction is high but you want minimal vol/time risk.
