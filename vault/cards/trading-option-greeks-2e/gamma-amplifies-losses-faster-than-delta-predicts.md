# Gamma Amplifies Losses Faster Than Delta Predicts

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 115-116

## Summary

Short call with delta=0.34, gamma=0.15: if stock rises $1, new delta = 0.49. A stop-loss at 1.10 (on a 0.66 credit) is reached at only a $1.01 stock rise—not the $1.32 a pure-delta calculation implies. Gamma acceleration means stop-losses are hit faster than expected.
