# Iron Butterfly: Sell ATM Straddle + Buy OTM Strangle

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 198-200

## Summary

Short ATM call + short ATM put (straddle) + long OTM put + long OTM call (strangle). Maximum profit at the ATM strike at expiration. Capital-efficient vs. standard butterfly because OTM options often have tighter bid-ask spreads than ITM options.
