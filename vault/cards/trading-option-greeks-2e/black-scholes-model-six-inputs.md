# Black-Scholes Model: Six Inputs

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 39-40

## Summary

BSM prices European calls using six inputs: stock price, strike price, time to expiration, risk-free interest rate, dividends, and volatility. Only volatility is not directly observable—it must be estimated (historical vol) or backed out from market prices (implied vol). This is the foundation of all option pricing.
