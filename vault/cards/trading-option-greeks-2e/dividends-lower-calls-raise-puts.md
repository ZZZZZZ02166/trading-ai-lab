# Dividends Lower Calls, Raise Puts

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 157-158

## Summary

From put-call parity: call = put + stock − strike + interest − dividends. Higher dividends reduce call prices and increase put prices. Stock price drops on ex-date by ~the dividend amount, hurting calls and helping puts. Must enter accurate dividend forecasts into pricing models.
