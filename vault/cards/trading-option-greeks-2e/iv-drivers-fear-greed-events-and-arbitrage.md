# IV Drivers: Fear, Greed, Events, and Arbitrage

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 75-75

## Summary

IV rises from fear (protection demand), anticipated events (earnings, Fed), or speculative demand. IV falls from complacency, post-event vol crush, or market-maker arb. 'Buy the rumor, sell the news' manifests in IV rising into events and collapsing after.
