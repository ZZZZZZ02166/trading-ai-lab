# Short Straddle: Sell ATM Call + ATM Put

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 299-301

## Summary

Maximum profit = total premium if stock is exactly at strike at expiration. Unlimited losses in both directions. Passarelli: 'one of the riskiest positions one can trade.' Only appropriate when IV is highly elevated and you're confident the stock will move less than the market expects.
