# Interest Rates: Higher Rates Lift Calls, Depress Puts

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 148-149

## Summary

From put-call parity: higher rates increase carrying cost → raise the forward price → raise call values → lower put values. Calls benefit when rates rise; puts are hurt. Rho makes this effect explicit in the pricing model.
