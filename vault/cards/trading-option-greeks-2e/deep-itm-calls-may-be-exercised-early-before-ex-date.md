# Deep ITM Calls May Be Exercised Early Before Ex-Date

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 161-162

## Summary

If the time value of a deep ITM call < dividend amount, it is economically rational to exercise early to capture the dividend. This reduces call prices just before ex-dates. Short deep-ITM calls heading into ex-dates face early assignment risk.
