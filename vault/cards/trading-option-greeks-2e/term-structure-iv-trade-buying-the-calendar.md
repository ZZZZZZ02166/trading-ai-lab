# Term Structure IV Trade: Buying the Calendar

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 228-229

## Summary

July IV at 45%, September IV at 38%. Buy the calendar (sell July, buy September) at 7-point IV differential. If July IV converges to September's 38%, profit ≈ $1,283 on a 210-lot equivalent. Risk: IVs diverge further, or stock moves away from strike causing the spread to collapse.
