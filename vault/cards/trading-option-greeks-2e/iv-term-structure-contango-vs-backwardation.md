# IV Term Structure: Contango vs. Backwardation

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 84-86

## Summary

Normal: longer-dated options have higher IV (contango). Pre-event: front-month IV spikes above back-months (backwardation). Understanding term structure identifies which expiration month is cheapest/richest on a volatility basis—critical for calendar spread strategy selection.
