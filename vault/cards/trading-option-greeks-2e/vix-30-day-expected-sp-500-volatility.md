# VIX: 30-Day Expected S&P 500 Volatility

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 81-83

## Summary

VIX = CBOE's implied vol of a hypothetical 30-day SPX option. Represents the market's expectation of annualized S&P 500 vol over the next 30 days. VIX of 20 implies ~1.25% expected daily moves. The 'fear index'—spikes during crises, fades during calm. Mean-reverts strongly.
