# P/L Attribution: Vega + Gamma + Theta Decomposition

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 254-255

## Summary

Susie's IV trade: Vega P/L (+$3,000 from 3-point IV rise) − theta cost ($75) − delta/gamma loss ($225) = net $800 profit. Always decompose P/L into components. A net gain can mask a vega win offsetting a gamma loss. Attribution guides future sizing and diagnosis.
