# Ratio Vertical Spread: Sell More Than Buy for Credit

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 320-321

## Summary

Buy 1 call (or put) at a closer strike, sell 2 at a further strike. Creates a net credit or low debit but unlimited risk above the short strikes. Requires good prediction of both direction AND a limited range. Generally best avoided unless there is a specific edge in both vol and direction.
