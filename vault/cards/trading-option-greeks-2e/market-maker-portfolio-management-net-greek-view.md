# Market Maker Portfolio Management: Net Greek View

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 322-325

## Summary

Market makers accumulate positions across many strikes/expirations by providing liquidity. They manage the combined net greek profile—not individual trades. Key skills: lower bids when long gamma/vega (don't want more); raise bids when short gamma/vega (need to hedge).
