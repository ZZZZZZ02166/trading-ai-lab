# Implied Volatility: Forward-Looking Market Consensus

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 74-75

## Summary

IV = the volatility input that makes a pricing model match the observed market option price. It is the market's collective forecast of future realized volatility. $100 stock at 32% IV implies ~68% probability the stock stays between $68–$132 over one year.
