# Options Leverage: Double-Edged Amplification

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 104-105

## Summary

Small underlying moves can produce triple-digit % returns—or complete premium loss. One equity contract = 100 shares of exposure for the premium cost. Leverage cuts both ways. Options can also cap losses when used as hedges.
