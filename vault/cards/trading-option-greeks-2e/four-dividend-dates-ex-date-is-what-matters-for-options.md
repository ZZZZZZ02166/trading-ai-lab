# Four Dividend Dates: Ex-Date Is What Matters for Options

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 158-159

## Summary

(1) Declaration date—amount and dates announced; (2) Ex-dividend date—stock trades without the dividend right; (3) Record date—registered shareholders receive dividend; (4) Payable date—dividend is paid. Options traders focus on the ex-date: the stock drops by ~the dividend amount here.
