# Synthetic Long Stock = Long Call + Short Put

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 136-138

## Summary

Long call + short put (same strike, same expiration). At expiration: call exercised or put assigned = buy at strike either way. Forward price = strike + interest − dividends. Used by professionals to replicate stock exposure without tying up full capital.
