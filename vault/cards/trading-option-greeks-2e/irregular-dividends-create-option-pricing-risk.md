# Irregular Dividends Create Option Pricing Risk

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 165-167

## Summary

Companies may cut, raise, or eliminate dividends unexpectedly. Using historical dividend estimates when the actual dividend has changed causes systematic mispricing. Always check declared (not estimated) dividend amounts before pricing options on high-dividend stocks.
