# Ratio Spread: Unequal Number of Options

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 312-314

## Summary

Buy fewer options, sell more at a different strike (e.g., 1×2 call ratio). Net credit or lower cost. Extra short creates potentially unlimited risk beyond the short strikes. Can start delta-neutral but carries significant gamma risk as the underlying moves.
