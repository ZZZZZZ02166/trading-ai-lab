# Rho: Interest Rate Sensitivity

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 63-65

## Summary

Rho = change in option value per 1 percentage-point change in the risk-free rate. Calls: positive rho. Puts: negative rho. Negligible for short-dated options; significant for LEAPS (2-year call rho ≈ 0.858). Usually the least important greek for short-term traders.
