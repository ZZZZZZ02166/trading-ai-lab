# Delta-Neutral Mindset: Trade Vol, Not Direction

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 249-249

## Summary

Ask: 'Is IV too high or too low vs. likely realized vol?' not 'Is the stock going up or down?' Buy when IV < expected realized. Sell when IV > expected realized. Hedge delta continuously to strip out directional noise and express a pure volatility view.
