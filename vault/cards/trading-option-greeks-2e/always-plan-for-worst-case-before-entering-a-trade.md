# Always Plan for Worst-Case Before Entering a Trade

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 334-335

## Summary

Passarelli: 'You can plan for [unexpected moves]. And as an option trader, you have to.' Define max acceptable loss, exit triggers, and worst-case scenarios before entry. Options with defined max loss enforce this discipline. Unplanned losses are inexcusable.
