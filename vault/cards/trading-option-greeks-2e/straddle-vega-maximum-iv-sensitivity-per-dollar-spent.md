# Straddle Vega: Maximum IV Sensitivity Per Dollar Spent

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 294-295

## Summary

ATM straddle has the highest vega per dollar. Both call and put vega ≈ 0.05: total ≈ 0.10 per lot. 10-point IV rise = $100/lot gain. Long straddles are primarily vega plays in calm markets—they profit from IV expansion regardless of direction.
