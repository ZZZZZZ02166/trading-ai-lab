# Iron Condor: Short OTM Put Spread + Short OTM Call Spread

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 196-198

## Summary

Net credit received up front. Maximum profit if stock stays between the two short strikes at expiration. Defined max loss at the outer strikes. Combines a short put spread (below market) and a short call spread (above market). Lower capital requirement than a naked straddle.
