# Vol Pattern 7: Realized Falls, IV Rises (Pre-Earnings IV Rush)

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 285-286

## Summary

The classic pre-earnings setup: stock is quiet but options become increasingly expensive as an event approaches. Options buyers profit if the eventual move exceeds IV expectations. Passarelli: 'the picture of the classic IV rush that is often seen in stocks around earnings time.'
