# ATM Options Have Peak Gamma

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 95-95

## Summary

ATM options near expiration have the highest gamma—delta changes fastest per $1 move. Maximum leverage in the final weeks. For buyers: ATM gives maximum gamma sensitivity. For sellers: short ATM near expiration is the most gamma-dangerous position.
