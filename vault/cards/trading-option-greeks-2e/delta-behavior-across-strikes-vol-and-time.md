# Delta Behavior Across Strikes, Vol, and Time

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 43-44

## Summary

ATM options: delta ≈ 0.50. Deep ITM → delta approaches 1.00. Deep OTM → approaches 0. Higher volatility pushes OTM deltas higher and ITM deltas lower. More time to expiration also raises OTM deltas. At expiration delta is binary: 0 or 1.
