# Conversion and Reversal: Near-Riskless Arbitrage

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 139-141

## Summary

Conversion = long stock + long put + short call (same strike/expiry). Net delta ≈ 0. Reversal = short stock + short put + long call. P/L = put-call parity discrepancy. Used by market makers to lock in mispricings between options and their synthetic equivalents.
