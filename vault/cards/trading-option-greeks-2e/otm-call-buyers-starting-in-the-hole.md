# OTM Call Buyers: Starting in the Hole

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 97-98

## Summary

OTM calls have a high probability (~75–85%) of expiring worthless. Expected value can still be positive if the stock makes a large move, but buyers must be compensated by large wins to overcome frequent small losses. Buy OTM options only with strong conviction and a clear edge.
