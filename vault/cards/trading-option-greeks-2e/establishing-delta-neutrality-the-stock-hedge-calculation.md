# Establishing Delta Neutrality: The Stock Hedge Calculation

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 251-252

## Summary

To neutralize a 20-lot long call position at 50 delta/contract (= 1,000 total long deltas): sell 1,000 shares. If resulting net delta is still slightly positive (+20), buy 20 more shares. Read greeks in a pricing model; adjust stock position to achieve near-zero net delta.
