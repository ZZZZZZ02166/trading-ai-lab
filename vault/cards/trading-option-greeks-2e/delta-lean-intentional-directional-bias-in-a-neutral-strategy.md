# Delta Lean: Intentional Directional Bias in a Neutral Strategy

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 329-329

## Summary

A delta lean means intentionally accepting some directional delta instead of staying perfectly neutral—usually when the trader has a mild directional view. Passarelli: a lean 'should be entered into a model for simulation before executing' to study the up/down risk. If it reduces overall position risk, it's justified.
