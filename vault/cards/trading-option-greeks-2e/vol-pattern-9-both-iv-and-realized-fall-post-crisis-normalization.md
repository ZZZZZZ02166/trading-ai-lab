# Vol Pattern 9: Both IV and Realized Fall (Post-Crisis Normalization)

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 288-289

## Summary

Both declining simultaneously = slow dissipation of a crisis period. Stocks stabilize, fear ebbs, options become cheaper. Short-vol strategies become attractive again after a period of elevated vol. Often coincides with market bottoms and post-event stabilization.
