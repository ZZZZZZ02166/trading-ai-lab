# LEAPS in Rising Rate Environments

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 152-153

## Summary

LEAPS calls have rho ≈ 0.8+. A 1% rate increase boosts their value by $0.80+. In rising-rate cycles, long LEAPS calls receive an additional tailwind. Long LEAPS puts face a headwind. LEAPS rho risk is significant enough to include in scenario analysis.
