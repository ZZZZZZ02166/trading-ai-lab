# Long Straddle: Buy ATM Call + ATM Put

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 292-294

## Summary

Profits from large moves in either direction. Break-even = strike ± total premium. Example: 70-strike straddle at $4.25/side = $8.50 total; break-evens at $65.75 and $74.25. Primary risk: IV collapse and/or realized moves too small to cover theta. The ultimate pure volatility long.
