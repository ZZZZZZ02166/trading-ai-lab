# Volatility Charts: Reading IV and Realized Vol Together

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 276-276

## Summary

Plot IV and 30-day realized vol on the same chart over time. Key questions: (1) Is IV above/below its historical range? (2) Is IV above/below current realized vol? (3) Are they converging or diverging? This is the primary tool for identifying vol-selling and vol-buying setups.
