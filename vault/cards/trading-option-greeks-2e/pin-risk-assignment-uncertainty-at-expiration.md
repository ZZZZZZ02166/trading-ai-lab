# Pin Risk: Assignment Uncertainty at Expiration

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 143-144

## Summary

When stock price is very close to a strike at expiration, market makers cannot know if the option will be assigned. They face ±100 delta uncertainty. If they hedge incorrectly, they end up with an unintended stock position. Short option positions near ATM at expiration must be managed carefully.
