# Historical Volatility: Annualized Standard Deviation of Returns

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 70-71

## Summary

HV = annualized standard deviation of close-to-close log returns. Convention: 252 trading days/year. Backward-looking—describes how volatile the stock has actually been. Does not directly predict future volatility. Used as a benchmark to compare against IV.
