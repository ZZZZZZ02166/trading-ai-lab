# Open Interest vs. Volume

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Trading Option Greeks 2e — pp. 20-21

## Summary

Volume = contracts traded in a day. Open interest = contracts currently open. New buyer + new seller → OI increases by 1. Existing holder closes with existing short → OI decreases by 1. Volume can exceed OI if contracts are opened and closed multiple times in one day.
