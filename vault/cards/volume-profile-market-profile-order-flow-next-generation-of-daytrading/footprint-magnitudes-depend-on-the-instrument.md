# Footprint Magnitudes Depend on the Instrument

**Source** [PDF pp. 105–107](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=105)

## Summary

In choosing markets, Forthmann compares pace, session timing, liquidity and usual contract sizes. A large print in a thin contract can look small in a more active contract. Use comparable session windows before interpreting relative activity.
