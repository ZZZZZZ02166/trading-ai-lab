# Icebergs Are an Inference From Repeated Fills

**Source** [PDF pp. 80–84](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=80)

## Summary

Hidden or replenishing liquidity can produce many executions at one level without sustained movement. Displayed DOM depth alone cannot reveal total hidden interest or the owner. The useful observation is persistent execution with a disproportionate or absent price response.
