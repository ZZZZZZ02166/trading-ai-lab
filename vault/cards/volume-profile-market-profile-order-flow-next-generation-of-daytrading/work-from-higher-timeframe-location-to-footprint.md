**Source** [PDF pp. 128–136](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=128)

## Summary

The yen and E-mini studies first locate price around an established profile or recent extreme. A smaller price/delta view then shows whether aggression gains progress, and the footprint checks for a later initiative print or imbalance. The final entry assessment follows that order.
