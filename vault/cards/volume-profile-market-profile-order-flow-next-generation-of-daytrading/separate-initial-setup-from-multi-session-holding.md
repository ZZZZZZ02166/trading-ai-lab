**Source** [PDF pp. 204–207](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=204)

## Summary

The final EUR/USD example begins with a local backfire setup and then shows the wider hourly path over subsequent sessions. The original evidence and the later holding decision have different horizons. It is an example of how the method scales across views.
