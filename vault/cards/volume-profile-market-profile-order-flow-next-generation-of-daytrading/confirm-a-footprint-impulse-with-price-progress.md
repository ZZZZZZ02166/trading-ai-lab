**Source** [PDF pp. 74–77](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=74)

## Summary

Forthmann checks whether price and successive local control prices advance after a first stack or large execution. If an opposing print cannot move price through an extreme, the next impulse can clarify which side is gaining initiative. A preidentified reference gives the sequence context.
