# Absorption Appears as Execution Without Progress

**Source** [PDF pp. 78–81](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=78)

## Summary

Strong buy-initiated executions can hit passive selling while price fails to establish a new high. The mirrored case has heavy selling with a defended low. Forthmann compares price and delta to infer this condition, with replenishing liquidity as one explanation.
