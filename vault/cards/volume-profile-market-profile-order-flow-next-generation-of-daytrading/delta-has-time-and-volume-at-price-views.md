# Delta Has Time and Volume-at-Price Views

**Source** [PDF pp. 91–94](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=91)

## Summary

The book shows signed delta by bar, CVD and delta distributed across price levels. The views can locate stretches of related aggression or a large apparent buying or selling extreme. Comparing those prints with actual price progress is part of interpreting them.
