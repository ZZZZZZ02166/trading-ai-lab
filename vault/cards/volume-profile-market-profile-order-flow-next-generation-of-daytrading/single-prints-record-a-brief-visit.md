# Single Prints Record a Brief Visit

**Source** [PDF pp. 20–21](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=20)

## Summary

A single-print price row belongs to just one TPO bracket. A single-column extension at a profile extreme is a buying or selling tail in the book's terminology. This locates brief acceptance or rejection to investigate on a revisit.
