# Treat a Trend as Swing Progression

**Source** [PDF pp. 195–198](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=195)

## Summary

Forthmann discusses preceding swing highs and lows, closes beyond them, and follow-up behavior to read a trend. A local visual pattern can look different across chart intervals. Sustained progress and a defended retest give the structure more context.
