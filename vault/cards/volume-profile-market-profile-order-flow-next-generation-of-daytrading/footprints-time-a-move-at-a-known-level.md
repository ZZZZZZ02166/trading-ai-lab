# Footprints Time a Move at a Known Level

**Source** [PDF pp. 70–73](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=70)

## Summary

A footprint decomposes each bar into executed volume per price level and side. Forthmann looks for the sequence of imbalances around a mapped reference. His examples allow the chain to contain a break and use an illustrative ratio distinct from Dale's software default.
