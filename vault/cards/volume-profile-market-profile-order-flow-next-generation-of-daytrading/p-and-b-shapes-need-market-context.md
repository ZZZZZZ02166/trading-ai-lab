# P and b Shapes Need Market Context

**Source** [PDF pp. 31–33](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=31)

## Summary

A P profile can show higher acceptance after a rally or a short-covering pause in a broader decline. A b shape can likewise appear in a down move or near a developing base. Forthmann looks at what came before and how the next auction behaves.
