**Source** [PDF pp. 9–13](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=9)

## Summary

Forthmann starts with recent auction context, uses profiles to narrow the relevant price area, then inspects executions for timing. Market profile and volume profile address time and volume acceptance respectively. The combined method asks what happens as the reference is reached.
