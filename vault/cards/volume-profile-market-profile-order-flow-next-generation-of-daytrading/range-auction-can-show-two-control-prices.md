# Range Auction Can Show Two Control Prices

**Source** [PDF pp. 40–43](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=40)

## Summary

Forthmann profiles a defined range using both market and volume views. A TPO POC shows where time concentrated and VPOC shows where executions concentrated. Divergence identifies two reference prices to carry into the subsequent session.
