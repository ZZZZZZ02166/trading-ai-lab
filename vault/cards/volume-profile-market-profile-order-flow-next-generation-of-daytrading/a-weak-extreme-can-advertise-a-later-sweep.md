# A Weak Extreme Can Advertise a Later Sweep

**Source** [PDF pp. 58–60](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=58)

## Summary

A weak high or low may consist of closely repeated prices with little decisive rejection. Forthmann treats that structure as a possible target for a later sweep. This chart-based usage of unfinished auction is distinct from a zero-quantity test inside a single footprint.
