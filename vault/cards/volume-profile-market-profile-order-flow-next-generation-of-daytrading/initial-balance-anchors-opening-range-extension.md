# Initial Balance Anchors Opening Range Extension

**Source** [PDF pp. 20–21](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=20)

## Summary

The first opening brackets define an initial balance, often the first two half-hour brackets in the illustration. Trade beyond that range is an extension. Forthmann treats a relatively narrow balance as a reason to watch for expansion, and the active instrument and session matter.
