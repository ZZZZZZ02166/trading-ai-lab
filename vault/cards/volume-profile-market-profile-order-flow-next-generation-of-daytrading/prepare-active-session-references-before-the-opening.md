**Source** [PDF pp. 95–100](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=95)

## Summary

Forthmann reviews the prior main-session high and low, overnight or early-session extrema, recent value and POCs, and the scheduled release calendar. Preparation creates scenarios for the open. The source's timing examples should be interpreted for their specific instrument and session.
