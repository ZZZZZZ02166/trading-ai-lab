# Wait for Narrow Opening Balance to Resolve

**Source** [PDF pp. 83–85](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=83)

## Summary

In the Hang Seng example, a narrow range forms near the active open while delta keeps falling and price holds a higher low. The later upward VPOC crossing helps confirm resolution. The example separates the initial stall from the later directional evidence.
