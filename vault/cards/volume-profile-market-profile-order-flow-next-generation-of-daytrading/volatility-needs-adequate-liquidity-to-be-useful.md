# Volatility Needs Adequate Liquidity to Be Useful

**Source** [PDF pp. 101–104](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=101)

## Summary

The book separates tradable expansion from volatile action with thin liquidity and poor follow-through. Its examples show long wicks, overlapping bars, and erratic swings in the latter case. Instrument and session selection matter alongside measured range.
