**Source** [PDF pp. 34–38](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=34)

## Summary

Fixed profiles provide consistent daily, weekly or monthly windows. Forthmann checks main-session and 24-hour profiles separately because they can place the highest-volume node at different prices. He emphasizes recent references and uses older levels selectively.
