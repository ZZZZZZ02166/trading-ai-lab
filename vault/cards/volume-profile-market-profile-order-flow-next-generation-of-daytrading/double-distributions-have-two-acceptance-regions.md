# Double Distributions Have Two Acceptance Regions

**Source** [PDF pp. 32–33](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=32)

## Summary

A double distribution occurs when the market spends time or trades heavily in one region, moves through a thinner transition, and builds a second concentration. It gives two potential reference zones and a transition area. The later open and subsequent activity help tell which zone matters.
