**Source** [PDF pp. 149–156](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=149)

## Summary

Forthmann projects a weekly VPOC onto a faster crude chart to observe the next interaction. The gold and EUR/USD examples similarly use earlier structure before zooming into delta and footprints. The higher-timeframe reference and lower-timeframe trigger are separate inputs.
