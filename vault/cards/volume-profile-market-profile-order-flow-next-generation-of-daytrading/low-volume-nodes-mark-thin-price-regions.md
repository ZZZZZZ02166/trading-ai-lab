# Low-Volume Nodes Mark Thin Price Regions

**Source** [PDF p. 27](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=27)

## Summary

Low-volume nodes are local indentations in a volume profile. They can appear between more accepted areas or along a fast directional move. Forthmann watches for either a reaction at a boundary or rapid passage through a thin region.
