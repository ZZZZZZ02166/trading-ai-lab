# TPO Measures Time at Price

**Source** [PDF pp. 19–21](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=19)

## Summary

A market profile marks prices visited in successive time brackets, illustrated with half-hour letters. Wider rows show repeated visits across brackets. Its control price reflects the most TPO activity, which can differ from the price with the most executed volume.
