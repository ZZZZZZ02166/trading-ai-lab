# A Hanging Ledge Can Be a Continuation Pause

**Source** [PDF pp. 118–122](../../../books/Volume%20Profile%20%26%20Market%20Profile%20Order%20Flow%20for%20Next%20Gen%20Daytrading.pdf#page=118)

## Summary

A ledge is a compact pause inside a larger move. Forthmann profiles the ledge, examines its developing control price, and uses follow-through to distinguish continuation from an attempted reversal. A P shape can form during temporary short covering in a downtrend.
