# Leveraged ETFs: Compounding Makes Them Complex Instruments

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 246-246

## Summary

Key risks for traders using leveraged ETFs: (1) Volatility drag: in any volatile market, 2× and 3× ETFs lose expected value over time from compounding — even if the underlying ends where it started; (2) Options on leveraged ETFs are compound options — standard BSM misprices them; (3) Correlation between spot moves and vol changes affects leveraged ETF options differently than standard options; (4) Market IV for leveraged ETF options (Figure 13.3) is systematically above theoretical IV from the reference product. Practical consequence: do not use 2× or 3× ETFs as simple leveraged substitutes for the underlying in option strategies — the vol drag changes the risk profile.
