# Four Principles for Managing Unhedgeable Risks

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 120-120

## Summary

Figure 6.8 (butterfly P/L diagram) illustrates the key challenge: dynamic hedging removes directional risk but cannot hedge jump risk or vol-of-vol. Sinclair's four principles for managing risks that cannot be perfectly hedged: (1) Buy wings to protect against jump risk — long OTM options inoculate against gaps; (2) Diversify across underlyings — idiosyncratic jumps are uncorrelated; (3) Size each position to limit total loss — no single trade should be ruinous; (4) Don't anchor to worst historical scenario — use a range of scenarios including ones worse than any observed. Fat tails mean the worst outcome is always ahead.
