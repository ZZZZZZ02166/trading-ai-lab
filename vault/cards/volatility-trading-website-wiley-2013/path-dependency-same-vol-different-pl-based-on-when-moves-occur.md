# Path Dependency: Same Vol, Different P/L Based on When Moves Occur

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 131-131

## Summary

Figures 7.2 and 7.3 demonstrate path dependency with two extreme cases: Path 1: large jump on Day 1 — the option is immediately deep ITM/OTM, gamma collapses, and delta hedging captures little thereafter. Path 2: same sized jump on the last day before expiry — gamma is maximum near expiry for ATM options, and the hedge is imperfect due to the discrete jump. Both paths have identical annualized realized volatility (23.44%) but produce dramatically different P/L outcomes. Implication: the timing of large moves within the option's life matters as much as the total amount of realized vol. This is irreducible path dependency risk.
