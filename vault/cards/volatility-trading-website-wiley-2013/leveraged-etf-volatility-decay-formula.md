# Leveraged ETF Volatility Decay Formula

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 243-243

## Summary

A leveraged ETF with leverage ratio λ on an underlying with daily return r and volatility σ has value evolution: dL/L = λ(dS/S) − ½λ(λ−1)σ²dt. The second term is the volatility drag: always negative for |λ| > 1. For λ=2 (2× leveraged): drag = ½×2×1×σ²dt = σ²dt per day. For λ=3 (3× leveraged): drag = ½×3×2×σ²dt = 3σ²dt per day. Example: 2× ETF on underlying with 20% annual vol loses 2% (= 0.20²) per year in expected value from vol decay alone, regardless of underlying direction. Implication: long-term holders of leveraged ETFs experience compounding vol decay that exceeds the leverage benefit for volatile underlyings.
