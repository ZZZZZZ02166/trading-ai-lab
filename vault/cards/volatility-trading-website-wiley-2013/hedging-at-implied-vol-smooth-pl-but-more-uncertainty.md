# Hedging at Implied Vol: Smooth P/L But More Uncertainty

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 138-138

## Summary

If the trader hedges using BSM delta computed at implied vol (σᵢ), the daily P/L is stochastic (path dependent) but on average equals Vega × (σᵢ − σr). Figure 7.10 shows that the optimal hedging volatility that minimizes P/L standard deviation is σᵢ (implied), not σr (realized) — a U-shaped curve. Trade-off: hedging at σᵢ smooths P/L on each step but introduces more final P/L uncertainty. Hedging at σr locks in profit deterministically but requires knowing σr. In practice: most traders hedge at σᵢ for operational simplicity.
