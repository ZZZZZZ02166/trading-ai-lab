# VIX Trading Strategies: Basis Trade and Calendar Spreads

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 238-238

## Summary

Dynamic VXX/VXZ strategy (Figure 12.2, Aug 2010 – Aug 2012): Holds short VXX / long VXZ when term structure is in contango; reverses when backwardated. Performance: annualized return 2.62%, Sharpe 1.5, largest drawdown 18% (when VIX spiked Oct 3, 2011 from 20 to 56). Calendar put spread (buying ATM put + selling same-strike next-month put): captures the term structure roll; average annualized return 15.2%, Sharpe 1.2, largest drawdown 18%. Direct variance premium trade: short VIX straddles/strangles — average premium of 3.26% (Barnea and Hogan 2012, Oct 2006 – Nov 2010). Caution: all VIX strategies have severe drawdown risk during crisis periods.
