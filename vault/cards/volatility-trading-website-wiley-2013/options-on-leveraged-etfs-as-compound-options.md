# Options on Leveraged ETFs as Compound Options

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 243-243

## Summary

Carr and Madan (2014) showed that options on leveraged ETFs L can be replicated by a portfolio of options on the reference ETF S. Specifically, a call on L at strike K can be expressed as: C_L(K) = g(K) × ∫ P_S(xK)dx + h(K) × ∫ C_S(xK)dx (Equations 13.11–13.19). This is a compound option — option on option. Figure 13.3 shows: market IV for SSO (2× S&P 500 ETF) is higher than the theoretical IV derived from S&P 500 options. The market charges an extra premium beyond what the leverage ratio alone implies. Most likely terminal price interpretation: compare options using the expected (most likely) terminal value of L, not simple λ×return.
