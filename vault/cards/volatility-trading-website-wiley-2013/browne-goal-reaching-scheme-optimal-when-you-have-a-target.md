# Browne Goal-Reaching Scheme: Optimal When You Have a Target

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 155-155

## Summary

Browne (1999) solves a different problem: maximize the probability of reaching a target wealth G before going broke. The optimal fraction: α* = μ × W / (σ² × (G − W)), where W = current wealth, G = goal, μ = drift, σ² = variance of the process. Figure 8.12 shows Browne ratio and success probability: Browne bets aggressively when close to the goal (W near G) and conservatively when far away. Table 8.1: Browne scheme beats Kelly in expected time to reach a goal by 10-40% depending on risk level. Practical use: a trader with a specific annual return target uses Browne; a trader maximizing long-term wealth uses Kelly.
