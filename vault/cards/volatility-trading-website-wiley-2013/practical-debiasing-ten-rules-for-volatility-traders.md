# Practical Debiasing: Ten Rules for Volatility Traders

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 199-199

## Summary

Sinclair's Ch.10 summary condensed to actionable rules: (1) Don't trade too often — edge is diluted by transaction costs and noise; (2) Don't trade too large — overconfidence kills; (3) You don't need to be right on direction to profit from volatility; (4) Admit when wrong quickly — don't average losers without pre-planned rules; (5) Consider trading with a partner to get alternative viewpoints; (6) Weight new information carefully — adjust by sample size and reliability; (7) Look for evidence that contradicts your view (anti-confirmation); (8) Evaluate each trade on its own merits — not on recent P/L; (9) Look at implied vol levels before initiating, not just direction; (10) Consider whether news source adds value or is noise.
