# Volume-Volatility Correlation and Log-Normal Vol Distribution

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 60-60

## Summary

Figures 3.8 and 3.9 (SPY): correlation between daily range and volume = 0.81; correlation between absolute returns and volume = 0.68. High-volume days tend to be high-volatility days — useful for intraday vol prediction. Figure 3.10 shows S&P 500 30-day vol distribution is right-skewed (log-normal shape). Bull market (above 200-day MA): median vol = 12.1%, bear market: median vol = 21.6%. This ~1.8× difference is substantial — vol-selling strategies must be sized differently in bear vs bull regimes.
