# Wide P/L Dispersion Even with a Positive Expected Edge

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 131-131

## Summary

Figure 7.1 shows 100 simulations of a 1-year option hedged at 30% implied vol with 30% realized vol (zero edge). The hedging portfolio value at expiry ranges from −$200,000 to +$50,000 (for $1,000 initial vega). When a positive edge exists (IV > realized), the distribution shifts positive but the dispersion remains enormous. Table 7.1: daily hedging gives mean P/L ≈ −$97, P10 = $7,612, kurtosis = 4.40 — heavy tails. Practical warning: even correct vol trading bets lose money frequently. A strategy that loses money for 3 consecutive months may still be working as expected. Evaluate performance over many trades and with proper statistical tests.
