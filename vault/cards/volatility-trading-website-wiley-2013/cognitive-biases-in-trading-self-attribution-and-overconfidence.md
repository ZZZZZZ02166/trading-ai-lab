# Cognitive Biases in Trading: Self-Attribution and Overconfidence

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 199-199

## Summary

Self-attribution bias: successes are attributed to skill; failures to bad luck. Leads to gradual overconfidence and position size creep after winning streaks. Counter: keep records of all trades and perform objective attribution analysis. Overconfidence bias: traders overestimate the precision of their volatility forecasts and the reliability of their edge. Real confidence intervals on vol forecasts are much wider than traders typically believe. Counter: formally quantify uncertainty in estimates using historical forecast errors. When uncertain, size smaller — uncertainty compounds with position size.
