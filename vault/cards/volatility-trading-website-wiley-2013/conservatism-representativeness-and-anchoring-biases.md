# Conservatism, Representativeness, and Anchoring Biases

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 208-208

## Summary

Conservatism: underreacting to new information — updating beliefs too slowly. When vol is rising, traders fail to adjust forecast upward quickly enough. Representativeness: judging probability by similarity to a prototype rather than by base rates. 'This stock looks like AAPL in 2012' — ignoring how often such comparisons actually play out. Anchoring: excessive reliance on the first piece of information seen. IV anchors to recent ATM vol; adjustments away from the anchor are insufficient. After a spike to 40%, traders anchor to 40% and are slow to revise down when fundamentals support a return to 20%.
