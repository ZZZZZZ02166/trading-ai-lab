# Sticky Strike vs Sticky Delta: Two Models for Smile Dynamics

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 92-92

## Summary

Two competing conventions for how the IV smile moves when the underlying moves: Sticky Strike: the IV at each absolute strike price is fixed. If S moves from 100 to 95, the IV for the 95-strike stays the same. Sticky Delta: the IV at each delta is fixed. If S moves from 100 to 95, the IV at 50-delta follows S down to the 95-strike. Figure 5.12 shows IV backbone (sticky strike model) vs actual smile: in practice, neither model is exactly right — the smile shifts partially with the underlying. Sticky delta is more accurate in markets with strong skew; sticky strike works better in range-bound conditions.
