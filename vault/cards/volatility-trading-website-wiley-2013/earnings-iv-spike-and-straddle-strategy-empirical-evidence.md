# Earnings IV Spike and Straddle Strategy: Empirical Evidence

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 88-88

## Summary

Figure 5.8 shows AAPL front-month IV rising ~10 days before Q2 2007 earnings, then collapsing sharply on the announcement day. Sinclair tested pre-earnings long straddle (buy 10 days before, sell day of announcement) on 73 stocks: 43% of trades were profitable, win/loss ratio = 1.70, return distribution has skewness = 5.4 and kurtosis = 76.6 (extreme positive outliers). Interpretation: the strategy has a negative win rate but positive expected value due to occasional very large winners (gaps on surprise announcements). Caution: results dominated by a few extreme events; strategy may not be robust out-of-sample. Transaction costs matter significantly.
