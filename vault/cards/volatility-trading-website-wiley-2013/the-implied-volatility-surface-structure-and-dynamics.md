# The Implied Volatility Surface: Structure and Dynamics

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 84-84

## Summary

Figure 5.1 shows the QQQQ IV surface on August 1, 2007: a 3D surface with strikes on x-axis, time-to-expiry on y-axis, IV on z-axis. The surface has two key dimensions: (1) Skew: IV varies across strikes at a given expiry — typically downward-sloping for equity options (OTM puts have higher IV than OTM calls); (2) Term structure: IV varies across expirations at a given strike. Figure 5.3: ATM term structure for QQQQ is slightly upward-sloping (0.219 to 0.228), typical of calm market conditions. Dynamic behavior: level shifts dominate skew slope changes. When VIX spikes, the whole surface moves up more than the smile steepens.
