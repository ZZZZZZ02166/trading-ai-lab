# Six Stylized Facts About Volatility (Sinclair Ch.3 Summary)

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 53-53

## Summary

Universal properties of financial volatility that hold across asset classes: (1) Volatility clusters — high-vol periods follow high-vol periods (autocorrelation in |r|²); (2) Volatility mean-reverts — extremes are not permanent; (3) Return distributions have fat tails (excess kurtosis > 0); (4) Volatility tends to increase as price declines (leverage effect); (5) Volatility and volume are highly correlated; (6) Volatility is approximately log-normally distributed. These facts constrain any valid volatility model and explain why BSM misprices options.
