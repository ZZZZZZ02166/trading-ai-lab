# Leverage Effect: Volatility Increases When Price Falls

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 53-53

## Summary

Figure 3.4 shows autocorrelation for signed absolute returns — negative at lag 1, near zero thereafter. This is the leverage effect: when price falls, vol rises asymmetrically compared to when price rises. Two explanations: (1) Financial leverage — as stock price falls, debt/equity ratio rises, making equity riskier; (2) Volatility feedback — traders expect higher future vol after a decline, driving current vol up (demand for puts increases). Practical consequence: equity skew (OTM puts have higher IV than OTM calls) partly reflects this structural asymmetry.
