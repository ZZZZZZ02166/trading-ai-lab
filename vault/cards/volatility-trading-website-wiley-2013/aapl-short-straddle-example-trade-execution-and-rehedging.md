# AAPL Short Straddle Example: Trade Execution and Rehedging

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 253-253

## Summary

Trade: Short AAPL straddle, June 25, 2007, Vega = $1,000, IV = ~41%. June 28: very quiet day — no hedging required. June 29: slight rally in stock — bought 400 shares to rebalance delta. Position table (Underlying, Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega) updated daily. July 2: volatility declined to 34% — straddle gained from vol drop. July 3: liquidated the position at a profit. Table 14.2: if held to expiry, month-by-month P/L table shows overall positive outcome — actual close-to-close realized vol = 30.1% vs implied at entry = 41.4%. Edge was 11.3 vol points. P/L matched the Vega × (41.4 − 30.1) formula approximately.
