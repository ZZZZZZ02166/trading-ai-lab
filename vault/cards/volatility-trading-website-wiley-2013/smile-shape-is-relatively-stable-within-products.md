# Smile Shape is Relatively Stable Within Products

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 103-103

## Summary

Sinclair's empirical finding: the slope and curvature of the IV smile for a given product tends to be relatively stable over time, even as the level shifts. The most important IV movement is the level (ATM vol) — this dominates PnL for most vanilla options positions. The smile slope adds a second-order effect. Practical consequence: for most vol trading, the primary forecast needed is the ATM vol level. Smile shape can be treated as approximately fixed (update monthly). Exception: around major risk events (elections, crises), the smile steepens dramatically and temporarily.
