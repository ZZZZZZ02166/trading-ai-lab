# Whalley-Wilmott Asymptotic Hedging Bands

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 115-115

## Summary

Whalley and Wilmott (1997) derived an analytic approximation to the HN bands: Band width ≈ [3k × e^(r(T-t)) × |Γ| / (2γ)]^(1/3). Figure 6.5 shows the WW bands as a function of BSM delta: they form approximately straight diagonal lines — wider at high delta, narrower at low delta (roughly proportional to |delta|). Key feature: cost is modeled as proportional to trade size (bid-ask spread), not a fixed charge. The asymptotic approximation is good when costs are small. Weakness: loses the long/short gamma asymmetry of the full HN solution.
