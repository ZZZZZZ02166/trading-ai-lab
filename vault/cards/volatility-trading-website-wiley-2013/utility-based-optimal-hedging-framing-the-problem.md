# Utility-Based Optimal Hedging: Framing the Problem

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 108-108

## Summary

Sinclair frames hedging as a utility maximization problem. The BSM assumption of continuous rehedging is impossible — every hedge trade has costs. The question is: what is the optimal hedging frequency and trigger given risk aversion and costs? Figure 6.1 shows a risk-averse utility function (concave curve) and Figure 6.2 shows risk-seeking (convex). Risk aversion measured by Arrow-Pratt: γ = −U''(W)/U'(W). The exponential utility function U(W) = −exp(−γW) is commonly used for tractability. The optimal hedge is the one that maximizes expected utility, not the one that minimizes tracking error.
