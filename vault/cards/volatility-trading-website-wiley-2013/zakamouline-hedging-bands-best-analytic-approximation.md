# Zakamouline Hedging Bands: Best Analytic Approximation

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 115-115

## Summary

Zakamouline (2006) improved on WW by re-solving the HN problem asymptotically with better accuracy. The Zakamouline bands preserve the asymmetry between long gamma and short gamma positions. Figures 6.8 (Long Call) and 6.8 (Short Call) show the bands as function of delta: curves (not straight lines) that are asymmetric — short gamma requires tighter bands at high delta than long gamma. The Zakamouline solution is: the currently recommended method for practitioners who need analytic hedging bands. More accurate than WW, less computationally intensive than full HN.
