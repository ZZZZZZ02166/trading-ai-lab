# Fractional Kelly: Reduce Drawdowns at Modest Return Cost

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 148-148

## Summary

Betting a fraction f of the Kelly bet (fractional Kelly): Half-Kelly (f/2): reduces expected growth to 75% of full-Kelly but variance to 25%. The trade-off is favorable — drawdowns are dramatically reduced while long-run returns are only modestly lower. Figure 8.6 (Kelly) vs Figure 8.7 (half-Kelly): the PDF of bankroll at half-Kelly stays more concentrated and closer to initial value. Sinclair recommends: half-Kelly is the practical default for most volatility traders. Full Kelly is theoretically optimal but psychologically brutal in practice. Two interpretations of Kelly: (1) maximizes E[log(W)] — utility-based; (2) maximizes expected compound growth rate — frequency-based. Neither interpretation requires Kelly to be exactly the right fraction.
