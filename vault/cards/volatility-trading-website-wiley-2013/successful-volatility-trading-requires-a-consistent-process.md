# Successful Volatility Trading Requires a Consistent Process

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 259-259

## Summary

Sinclair's conclusion: successful trading is not about a single brilliant insight or proprietary formula. It requires: (1) Understanding pricing models (BSM as language); (2) Accurate volatility measurement (multiple estimators); (3) Realistic vol forecasting (GARCH + cones + implied); (4) Disciplined hedging (utility-based bands, not continuous); (5) Correct position sizing (Kelly/Browne framework); (6) Rigorous performance measurement (K ratio, Sharpe, Omega); (7) Psychological discipline (overcome known biases). A trader who is better than average on all these dimensions, even if not exceptional on any one, will outperform a specialist who excels on one dimension but ignores others. Markets are always changing — the trader who keeps learning will prevail.
