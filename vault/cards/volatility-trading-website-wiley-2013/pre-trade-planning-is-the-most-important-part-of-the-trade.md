# Pre-Trade Planning Is the Most Important Part of the Trade

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 250-250

## Summary

The pre-trade planning and analysis is usually the most important part of any trade. Key components: (1) Volatility forecast: what is the best estimate of realized vol using all available methods? (2) Implied vol check: is current IV significantly above or below the forecast? (3) Historical context: where does current IV sit in the vol cone? (4) Position sizing: what size allows profits to matter but losses to be survivable? (5) Exit criteria: at what P/L or vol level will you exit — decided in advance? A trader with a good plan and consistent process will beat a trader with a better gut but no process, over any reasonable time horizon.
