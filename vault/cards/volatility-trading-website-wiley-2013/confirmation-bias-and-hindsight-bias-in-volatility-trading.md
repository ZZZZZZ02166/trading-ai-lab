# Confirmation Bias and Hindsight Bias in Volatility Trading

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 215-215

## Summary

Confirmation bias: seeking information that confirms existing positions. Once short vol, a trader reads news selectively — emphasizing articles supporting continued calm and dismissing warnings of elevated risk. Counter: actively search for evidence that contradicts current positions. Hindsight bias: 'I knew that was going to happen' after the fact. Leads to overestimating forecast accuracy and underestimating model uncertainty. Counter: maintain written forecasts and compare to outcomes. Sinclair emphasizes: read and understand papers that argue against your current view. The most dangerous conviction is an untested one.
