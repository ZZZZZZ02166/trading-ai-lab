# Optimal Position Sizing for Mean-Reverting Assets

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 165-165

## Summary

For a mean-reverting process (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck), the optimal trading rule: buy when price falls below mean, sell when above, scaling position with deviation. The optimal entry point that maximizes P/L distribution is: S_max = 0.75σ away from the mean. Figure 8.18 shows the P/L distribution as a function of entry level — bell-shaped, peaking at 0.75σ. Entering too close to the mean: few opportunities. Entering too far: lower expected P/L because the process may not return. Figure 8.19 shows this for VIX trades: similar shape but fatter. For vol selling: do not enter on every uptick in IV — wait for mean + 0.75σ level.
