# Universal Position Sizing Rule: Big Enough to Matter, Small Enough to Survive

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 170-170

## Summary

Sinclair's practical summary for position sizing: (1) Kelly maximizes long-run growth — use it as a reference, not a mandate; (2) Browne is useful for reaching specific return targets; (3) Adding to losing positions (mean-reversion trading) is theoretically justified but requires rigorous testing and a confirmed edge; (4) Never use arbitrary price-based stops alone — they don't account for vol; (5) The key rule: a trade should be large enough that profits mean something but small enough that losses are not catastrophic. If an optimal position size cannot be found that satisfies this rule, the trade probably does not have enough edge to begin with.
