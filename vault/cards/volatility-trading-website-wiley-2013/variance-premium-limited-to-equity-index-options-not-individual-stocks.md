# Variance Premium Limited to Equity Index Options, Not Individual Stocks

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 228-228

## Summary

Ch.11 Summary: implied volatility is generally higher than subsequent realized vol for equity indices, but this premium is NOT reliably present for individual stocks. The premium is likely due to: negative skewness of index returns (diversification eliminates individual stock skew but not index-level correlation skew); and correlation premium — index options hedge correlation spikes which individual options do not. Variance swaps replicate the premium in a model-free way: the fixed leg consistently exceeds the floating leg for S&P 500 (Carr and Wu, 2009). For individual equities: the premium is smaller, less persistent, and dominated by idiosyncratic risk that is not compensated by a structural premium.
