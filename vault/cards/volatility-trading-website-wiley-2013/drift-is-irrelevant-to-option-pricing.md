# Drift Is Irrelevant to Option Pricing

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 21-21

## Summary

Under BSM, the expected return (drift) of the underlying is completely irrelevant to option value. This is because delta hedging eliminates directional exposure; the only risk that remains is volatility risk. Two traders with opposite views on where a stock is heading will still agree on an option's theoretical value if they agree on volatility. This is one of the most important and counterintuitive results in option theory. Practical implication: do not trade options based on directional views — trade them based on volatility views.
