# Required Knowledge Domains for Professional Volatility Trading

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 259-259

## Summary

Sinclair identifies the complete knowledge base required: (1) Mathematical: stochastic calculus, probability, statistics, optimization; (2) Financial theory: option pricing models, risk-neutral valuation; (3) Practical volatility: five estimators, GARCH, vol cones, IV surface; (4) Hedging theory: utility-based bands (HN, WW, Zakamouline); (5) Money management: Kelly criterion, Browne, mean-reverting sizing; (6) Behavioral finance: 10+ biases and their counters; (7) Market microstructure: transaction costs, market impact, execution. No single textbook covers all of these — this book is one of few that attempts the synthesis. Directly applicable books: Hull (options theory), Haug (pricing formulas), Natenberg (practitioner perspective).
