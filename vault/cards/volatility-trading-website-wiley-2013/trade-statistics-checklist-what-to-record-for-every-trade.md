# Trade Statistics Checklist: What to Record for Every Trade

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 176-176

## Summary

Table 9.1 (IBM trade example) shows the minimum statistics to track: Total P/L, average daily P/L, maximum daily loss, win rate, average profit (winning days), average loss (losing days), available bankroll, average bankroll utilized, Sharpe ratio, and simple return on allocated capital. All instances of a trading signal should be recorded — not just the ones taken. Biased sample selection (only recording good trades) is the most common cause of overstated backtested performance. Figure 9.1 (daily P/L bar chart) and Figure 9.3 (drawdown chart) provide visual diagnostics. Look for excessive drawdown duration, not just depth.
