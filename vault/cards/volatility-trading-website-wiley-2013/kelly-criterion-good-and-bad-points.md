# Kelly Criterion: Good and Bad Points

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 144-144

## Summary

Good points: (1) Maximizes long-run expected logarithmic wealth — Kelly eventually beats any other strategy; (2) Risk of ruin is zero (never bet entire bankroll); (3) Optimal in the sense that no other strategy consistently outperforms Kelly long-term. Bad points: (1) Requires knowing the true win probability and payoff distribution — which we never know precisely; (2) Drawdowns can be severe — Figure 8.8 shows PDF at twice-Kelly collapses badly; (3) A loss followed by a win at Kelly leaves you below where you started (non-ergodic path); (4) Kelly requires betting a fixed fraction of current bankroll — position size changes constantly; (5) The strategy is only asymptotically optimal — short-run performance varies widely.
