# VIX Futures: Contango, Roll Cost, and Term Structure

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 236-236

## Summary

Figure 12.1 shows VIX futures term structure on June 1, 2011: upward sloping (contango) from ~22 (spot VIX) to ~27 (8-month futures). Contango is the typical state: VIX futures price > spot VIX because mean reversion means high spot VIX is expected to decline. Roll cost: VXX holds a rolling long position in 1st and 2nd month VIX futures. In contango, rolling from near to far month requires buying the higher-priced far contract → continuous value decay. VXX has lost ~99% of its value since inception from roll decay alone (periods of backwardation partially offset this). VXX/VXZ basis trade: short VXX, long VXZ (longer-dated) captures the roll differential.
