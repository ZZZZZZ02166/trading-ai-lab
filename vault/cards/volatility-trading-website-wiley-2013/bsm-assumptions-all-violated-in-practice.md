# BSM Assumptions All Violated in Practice

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 25-25

## Summary

BSM assumes: (1) continuous trading — impossible in practice; (2) lognormal returns — real returns have fat tails and jumps; (3) constant volatility — volatility clusters and mean-reverts; (4) constant interest rates; (5) no transaction costs. None of these hold. The practical consequences: discrete hedging introduces tracking error, jumps cause delta gaps, the volatility smile/skew exists because vol varies across strikes, and transaction costs eat into gamma scalp profits. The model remains useful as a quoting convention and risk decomposition tool, not as a price oracle.
