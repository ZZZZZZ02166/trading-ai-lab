# Intraday Volatility is U-Shaped (High at Open and Close)

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 48-48

## Summary

Figure 2.8 shows 30-minute realized vol for SPY and Figure 2.9 for DNA: both exhibit a U-shaped intraday pattern — volatility is highest in the first and last 30 minutes of the trading session and lowest in midday. Mechanism: morning vol reflects overnight news digestion and order imbalances; afternoon spike reflects end-of-day position adjustments and hedging. Practical consequence: estimators using intraday data should account for this seasonality (e.g., normalize by intraday vol pattern) to avoid overstating vol from open/close prices.
