# All Risk Measures Have Weaknesses — Use Multiple

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 193-193

## Summary

No single performance metric captures the full picture of a strategy: Sharpe fails for skewed distributions (penalizes profitable outliers). Sortino requires a minimum acceptable return (MAR) choice — results vary with MAR. Omega varies with threshold L. K ratio depends on time scale. Maximum drawdown depends on sampling frequency (daily vs monthly). Sinclair's guidance: track all four (Sharpe, Sortino, Omega, K) simultaneously. Consistency across metrics is more convincing than a single high number. Also: a good strategy can go through bad periods — don't give up on a strategy based on poor performance over a small sample. Consistency is more important than the absolute performance level.
