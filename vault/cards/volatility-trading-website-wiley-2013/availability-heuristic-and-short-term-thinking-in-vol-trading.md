# Availability Heuristic and Short-Term Thinking in Vol Trading

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Volatility Trading, + Website Wiley (2013) — pp. 199-199

## Summary

Availability heuristic: events that are easy to recall (vivid, recent, dramatic) are judged as more probable than they actually are. After a crash: traders overestimate crash probability and overpay for puts. After calm: traders underestimate tail risk and undersell puts. Short-term thinking: excessive focus on daily/weekly P/L rather than long-term statistical outcomes. Checking P/L too frequently increases perceived pain from losses (Kahneman and Tversky loss aversion: losses hurt 2× more than equivalent gains). Counter: evaluate strategies monthly or quarterly, not daily. Have pre-committed stop criteria rather than making discretionary exit decisions in drawdowns.
