# Delta Hedging Straddles/Strangles: How Hedging Reduces but Doesn't Eliminate Risk

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 117–120

## Summary

Daily delta hedging a short straddle dramatically reduces variance of P/L outcomes compared to unhedged. The hedged position's P/L is determined by: (realized vol − implied vol) × gamma × (stock price)². However, hedging creates a tradeoff: costs money (transaction costs), and the remaining P/L distribution is still negatively skewed with fat tails. Hedging does not eliminate the catastrophic-loss scenario from a sudden large move; it just reduces the frequency of moderate losses.
