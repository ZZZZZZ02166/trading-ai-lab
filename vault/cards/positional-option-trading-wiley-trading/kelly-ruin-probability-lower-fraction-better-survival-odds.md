# Kelly Ruin Probability: Lower Fraction → Better Survival Odds

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 200–202

## Summary

The probability of reaching 200% wealth before being stopped out at 0% (ruin): P(reaching 200% before 0%) decreases from ~90% at very low f to ~45% at full Kelly, then stabilizes around 40–45% for f > 1. This shows that over-betting is not just sub-optimal for growth — it also significantly worsens survival probability relative to trading conservatively. Expected time to reach the goal is minimized near half-Kelly, providing the best balance of speed and survival.
