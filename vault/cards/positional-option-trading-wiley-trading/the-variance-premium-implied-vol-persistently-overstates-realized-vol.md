# The Variance Premium: Implied Vol Persistently Overstates Realized Vol

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 67–69

## Summary

The variance premium is the persistent tendency for implied volatility (IV) to exceed subsequent realized volatility. This is the single most important empirical fact in options trading. It exists across virtually all underlying assets — equities, equity indices, commodities, currencies, and fixed income. The SPX variance premium has been positive and statistically significant for multiple decades. Short volatility strategies (selling options, variance swaps, VIX futures) systematically capture this premium.
