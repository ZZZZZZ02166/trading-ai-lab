# Bitcoin: extreme vol and counterparty risks dwarf any variance premium

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 216–217

## Summary

Figure 10.1: Bitcoin price USD 2018 — peaked near $17,700 in January, fell to $3,000 by year-end. Annualized vol ~68% vs SPX ~15%, EUR/USD ~7%. The variance premium likely exists in crypto options, but counterparty risks are extreme: QuadrigaCX (CEO dies with all passwords, customers lose $190M), exchange hacks, no regulatory oversight. Average currency vol is in single digits; crypto is 10× more volatile. Crypto options cannot be directly compared to regulated market options. Any edge from variance premium is more than offset by counterparty and regulatory risk.
