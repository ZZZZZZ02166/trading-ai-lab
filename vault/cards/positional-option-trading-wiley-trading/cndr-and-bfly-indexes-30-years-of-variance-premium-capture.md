# CNDR and BFLY indexes: 30 years of variance premium capture

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 74–76

## Summary

Figure 4.7: CBOE CNDR (Condor) Index 1988–2018 — sells monthly SPX iron condors. Cumulative value: 100 → ~1,200 over 30 years. Figure 4.8: CBOE BFLY (Butterfly) Index 1988–2018 — similar strategy, more volatile, cumulative 100 → ~800. Both show that systematic short-vol structures on SPX have delivered strong long-run performance across three decades including major crises (2001, 2008). However, both strategies experienced severe drawdowns during crises — confirming the risk premium nature of the variance premium (you earn it by bearing crash risk).
