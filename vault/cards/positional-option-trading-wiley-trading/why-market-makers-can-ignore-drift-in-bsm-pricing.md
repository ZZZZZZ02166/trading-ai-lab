# Why Market Makers Can Ignore Drift in BSM Pricing

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 27–29

## Summary

Market makers can replicate options using the underlying, so they do not need to forecast the underlying's return. If a market maker does incorporate drift into pricing, a competitor who ignores drift can arbitrage them. This is the no-arbitrage foundation of BSM: the replication argument makes the drift irrelevant for pricing. However, directional traders (speculators) who buy options for exposure SHOULD incorporate their drift forecast — they are not replicating, so the no-arbitrage argument doesn't apply to them.
