# Why the variance premium exists and will persist

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 81–83

## Summary

Three structural reasons the variance premium will persist: (1) Insurance demand: investors with long portfolios buy puts for downside protection, paying above fair value just as homeowners overpay for fire insurance. Structural demand. (2) Path-dependency (Figs 4.9–4.10): investors care about the path to terminal wealth, not just the terminal value. They pay extra for options that prevent painful intra-period drawdowns. This is rational, not a bias. (3) Uncertainty premium: options insure against model failure — the possibility that vol will be much higher than anyone estimates. In a world with parameter uncertainty, this is rational.
