# Hedging frequency tradeoff: variance vs transaction costs

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 33–36

## Summary

Figure 1.4: Straddle P/L standard deviation vs number of hedges (0–250 per year). SD declines from ~$650 at zero hedges to ~$300 at 250 hedges — a convex declining curve. The first 20–30 hedges per year eliminate most directional variance; beyond 50 hedges adds little further reduction but increases transaction costs. Figures 1.2–1.3: Unhedged short straddle has strongly right-skewed P/L distribution (fat left tail). Daily-hedged straddle has near-normal, symmetric distribution. Practical implication: most retail/positional traders should hedge infrequently, accepting wider P/L variance in exchange for avoiding transaction cost drag.
