# Cross-asset variance premia: commodities and correlations

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 78–80

## Summary

Table 4.4 — Commodity variance premia: Oats 6.2%, Lumber 3.5%, Cocoa 3%, Rice 3%, OJ 2.3%, Copper 2.4%, Coffee 1.7%, Gold 1%, Silver 0.2%. Table 4.5 — Within-sector correlation of VP: Energy 33.4%, Grains 24.2%, Livestock 31.4%, Metals 30.1%. Table 4.6 — Cross-sector correlation: Energy-S&P 26.5%, Grains-S&P 2%, Metals-S&P 30.7%, T-bonds-S&P 39.3%. Commodities are largely uncorrelated to equity variance premium, making them excellent diversifiers in a short-vol portfolio. T-bonds do NOT hedge equity short-vol risk (positive 39% correlation).
