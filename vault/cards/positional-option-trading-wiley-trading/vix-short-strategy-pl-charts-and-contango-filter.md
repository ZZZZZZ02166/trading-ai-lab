# VIX short strategy: P/L charts and contango filter

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 67–69

## Summary

Figure 4.1–4.3: Selling front-month VIX futures from June 2015 to October 2019. Unfiltered: cumulative P/L rises to ~$80,000 with a sharp ~$30,000 drawdown in February 2018 (XIV collapse). VIX chart (Fig 4.2) shows baseline of 10–20 then spike to ~37 in Feb 2018. Contango-filtered (Fig 4.3): similar overall profit but smoother equity curve — the filter reduces exposure during dangerous regimes. Confirms VIX term structure contango is a useful timing filter, not just a theoretical construct.
