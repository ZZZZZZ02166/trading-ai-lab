# Earnings-Induced Reversals: Short-Term Overreaction

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 106–107

## Summary

Some stocks overshoot their fundamental value on earnings day and then partially reverse over the following week. This creates a strategy opposite to PEAD: sell calls after a large positive earnings gap, sell puts after a large negative earnings gap. The reversal tendency is most pronounced for stocks that gapped up >10% on very high volume. Combining: long straddle going into earnings (pure vol), then fade the move after (reversal) — this layered approach captures the IV premium in both directions.
