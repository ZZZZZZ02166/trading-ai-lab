# Behavioral Biases Are Too Easy to Postulate — Be Skeptical

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 41–42

## Summary

The finance literature lists 50+ behavioral biases that can theoretically explain any market anomaly. Because they're so flexible, they're almost unfalsifiable. Further, behavioral finance largely ignores social aggregation — individual biases don't simply sum; they interact in unknown ways at the market level. Before attributing a pattern to a bias, require that the explanation be specific, predictive, and testable. Bias stories can rationalize anything after the fact without generating actionable forecasts.
