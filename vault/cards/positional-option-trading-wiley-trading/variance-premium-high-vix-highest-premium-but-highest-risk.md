# Variance premium: high VIX = highest premium BUT highest risk

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 85–87

## Summary

The variance premium is NOT always present. In crash events, realized vol massively exceeds implied vol. The premium is largest when VIX is high — precisely when the risk of further vol escalation is also highest. VIX quintile assessment: VIX < 13: Poor risk/reward (premium small, gamma high); VIX 13–20: Best environment (moderate premium, manageable risk); VIX 20–25: Acceptable with sizing; VIX > 25: Reduce size significantly. Do NOT sell volatility after a spike without reducing size. Crowding risk: as more capital chases the premium, it may compress and gap-down risk concentrates.
