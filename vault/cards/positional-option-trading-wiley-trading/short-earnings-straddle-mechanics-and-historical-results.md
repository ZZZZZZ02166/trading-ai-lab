# Short Earnings Straddle: Mechanics and Historical Results

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 90–92

## Summary

Strategy: sell an ATM straddle (1 call + 1 put at same strike) before an earnings announcement, covering both the earnings event and the following several days. Delta-hedge after earnings. Historical results across many underlyings: average P/L positive, median positive, Sharpe ~1.4. However: results are negatively skewed and fat-tailed; occasional large losses exceed many winning trades. Best execution: sell when IV has peaked (day before announcement), close shortly after the move is digested. Do not hold through the next earnings cycle — this is a pure event-volatility trade.
