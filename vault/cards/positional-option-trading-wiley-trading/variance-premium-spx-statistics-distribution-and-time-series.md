# Variance premium SPX: statistics, distribution, and time series

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 69–71

## Summary

Figure 4.4: VIX vs S&P 500 30-day realized vol (1990–2018). VIX (orange) almost always runs above realized vol (blue) — both spiked to 80–90% in 2008. Figure 4.5: Variance premium time series (VIX − realized vol). Almost always positive, with large negative spikes only at major crises (1997, 1998, 2001, 2008, 2011, 2018). Figure 4.6: Premium distribution histogram. Table 4.1 Summary Stats: Mean=4.08, SD=5.96, Skewness=−2.33, Max=31.21, Min=−53.3, Median=4.63, 90th Pct=9.62, 10th Pct=−1.45. The premium is persistently positive with catastrophic left tail events. Average 4 vol points above realized vol — the systematic short-vol edge.
