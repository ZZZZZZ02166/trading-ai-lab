# Peso problem: short vol P/L looks smooth until it isn't

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 83–85

## Summary

Figures 4.11–4.12: A short put's P/L stays flat at ~$1.50–$2.50 for 20 days collecting theta, then spikes to $3.50 when a favorable stock jump occurs on day 21. A long call mirrors this: bleeds slightly negative for 20 days, then spikes to +$3 on jump. The 'Peso Problem': strategies can appear deceptively smooth because catastrophic events (extreme moves, crashes) are rare enough to not appear in any given sample. Historical data with no large crashes overstates the true edge of short-vol strategies. Always size for the event you haven't seen yet.
