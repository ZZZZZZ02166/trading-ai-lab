# 11 tradeable effects: confidence level 3 (highest)

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 88–89

## Summary

Level 3 (highest confidence): (1) Variance premium — sell IV broadly on indices and stocks where IV > realized vol. (2) Earnings IV overpricing — IV of options expiring shortly after earnings overstates the subsequent move by ~30% on average. Sell straddles/strangles before earnings. (3) VIX term structure contango — sell front-month VIX futures when term structure is steep. These effects have: strong statistical evidence across multiple periods and markets, specific predictive mechanisms, and persistent structural causes.
