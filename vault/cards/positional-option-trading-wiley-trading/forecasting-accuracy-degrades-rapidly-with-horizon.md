# Forecasting Accuracy Degrades Rapidly with Horizon

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 62–63

## Summary

Volatility forecasts are most accurate at very short horizons (days to 2 weeks) and degrade quickly. At long horizons (months), the best forecast often reverts toward historical long-run vol. The practical implication: short-dated options give the highest signal-to-noise ratio for vol forecasting. Trading calendar spreads to capture a term structure view requires a reliable forecast of how the vol term structure will evolve — which is harder than forecasting near-term realized vol.
