# Volatility Forecasting Is Necessary Even for Vol Sellers

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 56–57

## Summary

Every option trade involves an implicit volatility forecast. Even selling implied vol 'because it's high' is a forecast that realized vol will be lower. The relevant question is not whether to forecast vol, but how. Pure historical vol (rolling window) is the simplest estimate. GARCH and EWMA methods weight recent data more heavily and adapt faster to vol regime changes. VIX is a market-based 30-day forward IV forecast for SPX.
