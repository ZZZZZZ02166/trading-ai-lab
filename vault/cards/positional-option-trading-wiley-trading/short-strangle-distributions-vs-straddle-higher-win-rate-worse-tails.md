# Short strangle distributions vs straddle: higher win rate, worse tails

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 119–121

## Summary

Figure 6.2 (fairly priced strangle): extremely sharp spike at max profit ($841), even higher win rate than straddle. Table 6.2: Average −$6.12, SD $2,140, Skew −4.8, Kurtosis 24.2, Median $841, 10th Pct −$1,994, Min −$27,683, Profitable 78%. Table 6.4 (mispriced strangle): Average −$3,230, SD $9,190, Skew −4.2, Min −$197,526, Profitable 36%. Strangles hit max profit 78% of the time vs 57% for straddles, but when they lose, they lose much more (min −$197K vs −$15K). The strangle appears safer but has dramatically worse tail risk.
