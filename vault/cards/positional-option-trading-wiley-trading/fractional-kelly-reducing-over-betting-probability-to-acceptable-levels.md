# Fractional Kelly: Reducing Over-Betting Probability to Acceptable Levels

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 194–196

## Summary

To achieve a specified probability of over-betting (say 10% chance), scale Kelly by a 'Kelly Scale Factor': For normal returns: 10% → scale 0.0480 of Kelly; 15% → 0.2301; 20% → 0.3748. Including skewness adjustment (zero-skewness correction): 10% → 0.2054; 15% → 0.3574; 20% → 0.4782. A robust default: trade at 25–50% of the estimated Kelly fraction. This dramatically improves consistency of outcomes without sacrificing much expected growth rate, since growth rate is insensitive to f near its optimum.
