# Kelly Criterion for Continuous Outcomes: Optimal Bet Fraction

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 187–191

## Summary

For strategies with continuously distributed returns, the Kelly criterion optimal bet fraction is: f* ≈ μ/σ² (Sharpe² per unit of vol²), or more precisely f* ≈ μ/(μ²+σ²). This maximizes long-run geometric growth rate. For skewed return distributions: f* ≈ μ/(μ²+σ²) + λ₃μ²/(μ²+σ²)³ — positive skew increases f*, negative skew (typical for short-vol strategies) decreases f*. Short option strategies should be sized below the symmetric Kelly estimate due to their negative skew.
