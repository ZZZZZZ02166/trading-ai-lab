# Growth Rate vs Drawdown: Half-Kelly Maximizes Risk-Adjusted Growth

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 196–198

## Summary

Growth rate as function of Kelly fraction: GR(f) = (f − f²/2)·μ²/σ². GR peaks at f=1 (full Kelly) and falls to zero at f=2. Growth rate to drawdown ratio peaks at approximately f=0.5 (half-Kelly). This means half-Kelly maximizes how much growth you get per unit of drawdown risk. In practice, given estimation uncertainty, trading at ~25–50% of estimated Kelly is both theoretically optimal for risk-adjusted growth AND accounts for parameter uncertainty.
