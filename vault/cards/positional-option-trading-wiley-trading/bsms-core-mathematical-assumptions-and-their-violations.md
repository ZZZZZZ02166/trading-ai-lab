# BSM's Core Mathematical Assumptions and Their Violations

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 26–27

## Summary

BSM assumes: continuous trading, no transaction costs, constant volatility, log-normally distributed returns, a single risk-free rate, and borrowing/lending at that rate. Every single assumption is violated in practice. Returns have fat tails and negative skew; volatility is not constant (smile/skew exists); transaction costs are real. Despite this, BSM remains the industry standard because it provides a consistent, single-parameter language for option markets.
