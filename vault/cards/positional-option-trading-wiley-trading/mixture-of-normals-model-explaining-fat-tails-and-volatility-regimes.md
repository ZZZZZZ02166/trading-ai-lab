# Mixture-of-normals model: explaining fat tails and volatility regimes

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 237–238

## Summary

Figure A1.4 and Equation A1.4: S&P 500 returns modeled as mixture of two normal distributions: low-vol regime (~10–15% annualized) and high-vol regime (~30–80% annualized). Kurtosis formula: 3·(p₁σ₁²+p₂σ₂²)²/(p₁σ₁²+p₂σ₂²)². This two-regime mixture explains the observed leptokurtosis of equity returns without assuming non-normal distributions within regimes. The S&P switches between regimes — normal markets at 10–15% vol, crisis markets at 30–80%. Implication: the fat tails in equity option prices are rational pricing for regime switches, not irrational fear.
