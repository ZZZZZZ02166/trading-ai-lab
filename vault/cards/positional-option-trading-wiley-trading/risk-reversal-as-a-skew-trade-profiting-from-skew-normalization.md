# Risk Reversal as a Skew Trade: Profiting from Skew Normalization

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 169–172

## Summary

A risk reversal (long OTM call + short OTM put) profits when the volatility skew flattens. The skew trade P/L formula: Profit = Vega_call·(σ_call_new − σ_call_old) + Vega_put·(σ_put_old − σ_put_new). If the put-call IV differential narrows (skew flattens), both legs contribute positive P/L. A risk reversal also has positive delta (bullish bias) so it combines a directional bet with a volatility skew normalization bet. Best used when: skew is unusually steep, IV level is reasonable, and there is a directional bullish view.
