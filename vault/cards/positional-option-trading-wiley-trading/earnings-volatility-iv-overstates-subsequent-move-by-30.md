# Earnings Volatility: IV Overstates Subsequent Move by ~30%

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 89–90

## Summary

Empirically, implied volatility of options expiring shortly after earnings announcements overstates the subsequent stock move by roughly 30% on average. This means selling straddles or strangles into earnings (while delta-hedging afterward) has historically positive expected value. The strategy P/L is right-skewed on average but with significant negative skew from occasional large moves. The effect is strongest for smaller, less-followed stocks where information asymmetry is highest.
