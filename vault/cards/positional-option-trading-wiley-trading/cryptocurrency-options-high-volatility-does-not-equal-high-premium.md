# Cryptocurrency Options: High Volatility Does Not Equal High Premium

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 217–219

## Summary

Bitcoin and crypto assets are extremely volatile (annualized vol 60–80%+) but this does not automatically make crypto options worth selling. Issues: (1) exchange counterparty risk is extreme (inadequately regulated, custodial risk); (2) IV may actually underprice realized vol in crypto; (3) regulatory uncertainty. The variance premium exists in crypto but the additional risks (exchange failure, regulatory crackdown) make it not simply comparable to equity options. Treat crypto options as a separate risk category requiring specific counterparty due diligence.
