# Risk premium vs behavioral inefficiency: critical distinction

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 39–41

## Summary

Risk premia are structural payments from parties seeking insurance. The variance premium is the premier example: investors buy options for portfolio protection and rationally pay above fair value, just as homeowners overpay for fire insurance. Risk premia persist because structural demand for insurance persists. Behavioral inefficiencies are temporary mispricings from cognitive biases. These erode once discovered and widely traded. Post-earnings drift is an example. Distinguishing the two matters: strategies built on risk premia have structural persistence; strategies built on inefficiencies have a finite exploitation window.
