# BSM: drift is irrelevant for market makers who hedge

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 27–29

## Summary

Market makers who continuously buy/sell the underlying to replicate an option can guarantee a profit regardless of where the underlying goes. Because the replication cost doesn't depend on drift, drift doesn't appear in BSM. This is correct for hedgers. But it is wrong for speculators who buy options to benefit from directional moves — for them drift matters, and BSM systematically misprices options relative to their speculative value.
