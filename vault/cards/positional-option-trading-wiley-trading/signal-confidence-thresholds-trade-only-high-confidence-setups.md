# Signal Confidence Thresholds: Trade Only High-Confidence Setups

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 88–89

## Summary

Not all potential opportunities meet the same evidential bar. A framework for trade selection: Confidence Level 1 (only trade with significant additional confirmation) vs. Confidence Level 2 (trade with moderate confirmation). Short-selling options around earnings requires: (1) variance premium evidence, (2) specific elevated IV before the event, (3) a clear catalyst with known timing. Trading all opportunities regardless of confidence quality degrades portfolio-level Sharpe.
