# Butterfly and condor P/L distributions: capped risk structures

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 128–131

## Summary

Figures 6.10–6.13: Fairly priced butterfly: bimodal — spike at max profit ($2,756) when stock stays between wings, and spike at partial loss near strikes. Table 6.9: Avg −$25, SD $2,460, Skew 0.4, profitable 46%. Fairly priced condor: Table 6.10: Avg −$9, SD $2,252, Skew −0.4, profitable 58%. Badly priced structures (Tables 6.11–6.12): results flip dramatically — badly priced butterfly: profitable only 20%, mostly at max loss. Condor: profitable 26%. Conclusion: butterflies and condors only work when the short strikes are genuinely overpriced. They limit max loss but offer no edge if the vol premium isn't there.
