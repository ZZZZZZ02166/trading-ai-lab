# Volatility Forecast Comparison: Models Perform Similarly on Average

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 63–65

## Summary

Empirical comparison of vol forecasting models (historical vol, EWMA with λ=0.9 and 0.95, VIX, GARCH(1,1)) on S&P 500 shows similar average errors across methods (differences of ~0.5 vol points). No single model consistently wins. VIX has a slight predictive edge for SPX but is available only for index options. EWMA with λ=0.94 (similar to RiskMetrics) is a reasonable default for daily updates. The modest differences mean model choice matters less than having a process at all.
