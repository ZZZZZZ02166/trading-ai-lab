# Delta-Hedging P/L Decomposition: Gamma vs Theta

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 29–31

## Summary

When an option position is delta-hedged, directional P/L is eliminated. The remaining P/L per time interval is: Profit = ½Γ·S²·(σ_realized² − σ_implied²)·dt. This is the fundamental equation of volatility trading. If realized vol exceeds implied vol, the delta-hedged long option position profits; if realized vol is lower, it loses. The profit is proportional to gamma (highest at ATM) and to the squared vol difference.
