# Hedging short OTM puts: choosing the right spread

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 142–144

## Summary

Table 6.22: For a short 260 put position, hedging with different long put strikes. Strike 180 costs $0.64 but limits max loss to $7,436. Strike 220 costs $2.25, limits to $3,597. ATM option price approximation (Eq 6.2): P ≈ 0.4Sσ√T. This formula gives a quick estimate of option fair value for mental calculations. Key principle: higher-strike hedges cost more but provide better protection. The skew means OTM puts get progressively more expensive per unit of protection, favoring using a single option closer to ATM rather than multiple cheaper far-OTM options.
