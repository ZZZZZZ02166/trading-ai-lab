# Variance Premium Varies with Vol Level — Risk Scales with Opportunity

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 73–75

## Summary

The size and variability of the variance premium both increase with the level of implied volatility. In low-vol regimes, the premium is small and relatively stable. In high-vol regimes, the premium is large but so is the dispersion of outcomes — some periods show realized > implied. This means short-vol trades are most 'attractive' (largest premium) precisely when risk is highest. Selling volatility after a spike requires managing the fat-tail risk of further escalation.
