# VIX as predictor and VIX term structure contango

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 65–66

## Summary

VIX has predictive advantage for SPX volatility over statistical models because it incorporates market information. VIX futures are normally in contango (longer-dated > spot). Cash VIX is a better predictor of future VIX than the futures price. The term structure is in contango 81% of the time (2006 to start of 2019). When in contango, VIX futures converge downward toward spot as expiration approaches, creating persistent roll yield for short VIX positions. Selling front-month VIX futures when term structure is steep captures both roll yield and variance premium.
