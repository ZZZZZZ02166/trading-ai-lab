# Kelly criterion: optimal bet fraction for continuous returns

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 186–187

## Summary

For a strategy with mean return μ and standard deviation σ: f* ≈ μ/σ² (equivalent to Sharpe ratio × σ). For non-normal returns (negative skew, as in short-vol): f* ≈ μ/(μ²+σ²) + λ₃·μ²/(μ²+σ²)³ where λ₃ is the third central moment. Negative skewness (typical for short-vol strategies) reduces optimal Kelly fraction below the naive symmetric estimate. Figure 9.1: Kelly growth rate as function of f — bell-shaped curve, peaks at small fraction (~0.06) when a −3× outcome has 2% probability. Rare catastrophic events drastically reduce the optimal Kelly fraction.
