# Stops fundamentally change return distribution shape

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 204–206

## Summary

Figures 9.10–9.13 show what stops actually do: Figure 9.10: Strategy return distribution — normal, mean 10%, SD 15%. Figure 9.11 (hoped-for): Naive view — stop just truncates left tail at −15%. This is wrong. Figure 9.12 (true fixed stop): Bimodal distribution — mass of stopped trades collapses to a point at −15%, PLUS surviving trades have modified distribution. Average drops to 9.6%. Figure 9.13 (trailing stop): Right tail severely truncated — max return ~20%. Mean drops to 9.4%. Stops eliminate recovering winners, not just large losers. Use stops only when position monitoring is impossible or the drawdown risk is genuinely existential.
