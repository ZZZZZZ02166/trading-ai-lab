# Delta-hedged straddle vs strangle: taming the distribution

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 123–126

## Summary

Figures 6.7–6.8: Delta-hedging daily dramatically narrows the P/L distribution. Hedged straddle range: roughly −$5,000 to +$1,000 (vs −$15,000 unhedged). Table 6.8 — Hedged Straddle vs Strangle: Straddle: Avg $7, Median $12, Skew −0.2, Kurtosis 1.28, Worst −$856, Worst Decile −$242. Strangle: Avg $22, Median $126, Skew −2.71, Kurtosis 13.32, Worst −$2,170, Worst Decile −$450. Figure 6.9: Vega profile — straddle has narrow ATM peak; strangle has wider bimodal profile. Key: after delta-hedging, the straddle has better tail properties than the strangle, but the strangle retains higher average return. Trader preference depends on skew tolerance.
