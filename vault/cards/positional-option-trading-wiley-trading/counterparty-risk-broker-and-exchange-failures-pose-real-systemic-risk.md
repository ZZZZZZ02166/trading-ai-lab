# Counterparty Risk: Broker and Exchange Failures Pose Real Systemic Risk

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 216–217

## Summary

Market risk from option positions is well-understood. Meta risks — counterparty failure, fraud, regulatory changes, liquidity crises — are underappreciated and can cause total loss. Historical examples: MF Global (2011) — customer funds misused; Madoff — Ponzi scheme; QuadrigaCX (2019) — exchange collapse. Risk mitigation: use multiple brokers, prefer regulated exchanges over OTC, maintain excess capital at cash/treasury level, never concentrate all assets with one counterparty.
