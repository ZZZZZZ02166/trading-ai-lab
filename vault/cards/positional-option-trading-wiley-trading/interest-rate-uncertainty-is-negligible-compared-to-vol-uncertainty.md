# Interest Rate Uncertainty Is Negligible Compared to Vol Uncertainty

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 228–229

## Summary

Daily VIX changes have a standard deviation of 1.7 VIX points. Daily 1-year rate changes have a standard deviation of only 0.037%. A 1% rate pricing error on a 1-year option changes delta by ~0.011, which is trivially small vs. the impact of a 1 vol-point IV error. This empirical comparison justifies using deterministic interest rates in option pricing models. Stochastic interest rate models (Hull-White, etc.) add complexity without meaningfully improving option pricing accuracy relative to better volatility modeling.
