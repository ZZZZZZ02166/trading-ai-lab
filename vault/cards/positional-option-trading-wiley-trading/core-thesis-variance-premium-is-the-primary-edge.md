# Core thesis: variance premium is the primary edge

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 21–22

## Summary

Implied volatility is almost always overpriced relative to subsequent realized volatility. This variance premium is the most robust empirical fact in options markets. The correct approach is to find robust edge backed by empirical evidence, choose the option structure to monetize it, size appropriately, and always remember how much you don't know. Variance premium is persistent (a risk premium), not an inefficiency that will erode.
