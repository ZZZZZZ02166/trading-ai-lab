# Implied vol skew: OTM puts systematically overpriced

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 137–139

## Summary

Table 6.18 — SPY put implied vols on July 30, 2019. IV declines monotonically from deep OTM puts (strike 210, IV=0.253) to near ATM (strike 290, IV=0.160). This skew is persistent — far-OTM puts carry a much larger volatility premium than ATM options. The skew reflects: crash risk demand (investors pay for downside insurance), risk-neutral measure overweighting crashes vs physical measure, and systemic leverage. Selling OTM puts collects BOTH the variance premium AND the skew premium. Rule of thumb: SPX 10-delta put IV ≈ 1.45× ATM IV; 25-delta call IV ≈ 0.88× ATM IV.
