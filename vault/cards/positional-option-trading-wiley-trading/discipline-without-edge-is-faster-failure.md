# Discipline without edge is faster failure

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 38–39

## Summary

A trader without a real edge who applies strict discipline will lose money faster and more consistently than a sloppy trader with the same bad strategy. The first task in trading is identifying genuine edge — not managing a strategy without it. Risk management can only scale an edge up or down; it cannot create edge from nothing. Optimism and overconfidence are as dangerous as lack of discipline.
