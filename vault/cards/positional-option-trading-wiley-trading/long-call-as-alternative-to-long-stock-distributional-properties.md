# Long Call as Alternative to Long Stock: Distributional Properties

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 164–167

## Summary

A long ATM call on a $100 stock with 20% drift and 30% vol (1-year horizon, rates=0): Average profit $1,516, SD $3,198, skewness +1.66 (positive skew), median $538. Compared to 100 shares: average $2,640, SD $3,736, skewness +1.12, median $2,214. The call is cheaper (cost = option premium) but has lower median and average profit. The call's advantage is positive skew and defined maximum loss. Useful when convinced of a large move but uncertain about timing — downside limited to premium paid.
