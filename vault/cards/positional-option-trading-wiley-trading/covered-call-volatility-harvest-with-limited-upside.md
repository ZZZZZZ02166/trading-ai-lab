# Covered Call: Volatility Harvest with Limited Upside

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 167–169

## Summary

A covered call (long stock + short call) = long stock with capped upside. The CBOE BuyWrite Index (BXM) tracks selling ATM covered calls on SPX monthly. Historical performance (1988–2019): BXM 8.5% annual return, 12.6% vol vs SPX 7.7% return, 17.3% vol. The covered call gives a higher Sharpe ratio than the underlying because it collects the variance premium. The cost: capped upside during strong bull markets. Preferred when: vol is high, drift forecast is neutral-to-bullish, and variance premium is significant.
