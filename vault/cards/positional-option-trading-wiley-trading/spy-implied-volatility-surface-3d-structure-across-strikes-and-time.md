# SPY implied volatility surface: 3D structure across strikes and time

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 31–33

## Summary

Figure 1.1: SPY IV surface on September 10, 2019 (3D plot). X-axis: Strike 200–330. Y-axis: Time to expiry 31–122 days. Z-axis: IV 0–40%. The surface slopes upward for lower strikes (volatility skew: OTM puts have higher IV than ATM). The term structure is generally flat or slightly humped. The 3D surface shows the full BSM parameterization of the options market — every combination of strike and expiry has its own IV, confirming BSM is not a constant-vol model but a flexible parameterization framework.
