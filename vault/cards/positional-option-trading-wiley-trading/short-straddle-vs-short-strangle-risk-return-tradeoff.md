# Short Straddle vs Short Strangle: Risk-Return Tradeoff

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 116–117

## Summary

Both strategies profit from implied vol exceeding realized vol. Key differences: Straddle (sell ATM call + ATM put): higher initial premium, gamma concentrated at current price, very sensitive to small moves. Strangle (sell OTM call + OTM put): lower premium, wider break-even range, more resilient to moderate moves but tails are more dangerous. With an IV skew, OTM puts are more expensive relative to BSM value — strangle benefits more from the put skew premium.
