# BSM as parameterization tool, not pricing model

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 25–26

## Summary

The market prices options through supply and demand. BSM converts market prices into a single comparable parameter — implied volatility. This is why traders speak in IV terms: BSM lets you compare options across strikes, expirations, and underlyings on a common scale. If BSM were perfect, a single constant IV would describe all options on the same underlying. The volatility smile/skew is simply evidence that BSM is an imperfect model — not a market failure.
