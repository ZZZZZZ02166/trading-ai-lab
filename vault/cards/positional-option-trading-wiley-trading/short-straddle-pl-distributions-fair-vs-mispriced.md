# Short straddle P/L distributions: fair vs mispriced

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 117–119

## Summary

Figure 6.1 (fairly priced): Short straddle P/L — large spike at max profit ($841), long left tail. Average $8, SD $1,882, Skewness −1.83, Kurtosis 6.41, Median $384, 10th Pct −$2,274, Min −$15,321, Profitable 57%. Figure 6.3 (mispriced, realized vol=70%): distribution shifts left, wider, clearly negative EV. Table 6.3 (mispriced straddle): Average −$3,071, SD $5,425, Skew −4.31, Kurtosis 29.01, Min −$94,084, Profitable 25%. Core lesson: the short straddle has positive EV ONLY when you are right about the variance premium. Negative skew and high kurtosis are always present — manage size accordingly.
