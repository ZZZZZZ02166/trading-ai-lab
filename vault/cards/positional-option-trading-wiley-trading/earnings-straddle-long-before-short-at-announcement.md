# Earnings straddle: long before, short at announcement

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 103–105

## Summary

Figure 5.1 (long straddle): Buying ATM straddles 10 days before earnings for 73 large-cap stocks (2005–2010). 43% of trades profitable, win/loss ratio 1.70. Cumulative P/L rose steadily to ~$40,000 over 24 earnings periods on $10,000 notional. Figure 5.2 (short straddle): Selling ATM straddles as close as possible to earnings. 64% profitable, win/loss ratio 0.69. Skewness −5.4, excess kurtosis 12.2 — high win rate but occasional large losses. Key insight: earnings vol is overpriced on average (30% overstatement), but both strategies carry significant tail risk from occasional huge moves.
