# Covered call (BXM index): superior risk-adjusted returns to buy-and-hold

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 170–172

## Summary

Figure 8.6: BXM (CBOE covered call on SPX) vs S&P 500 from 1988–2019. Table 8.5: BXM Annual Return 8.5%, Vol 12.6%, Max Drawdown 40.1%, Skew −0.67. S&P 500: Annual 7.7%, Vol 17.3%, Max Drawdown 56.8%, Skew −0.29. BXM achieves higher return with 27% lower vol and 29% smaller max drawdown. Table 8.6: BXMD (30-delta calls) even better: Annual 10.3%, Vol 14.7%. Figure 8.7 shows vol premium peaks near ATM — selling ATM covered calls maximizes variance premium capture while selling farther OTM gives more delta exposure but less vol premium.
