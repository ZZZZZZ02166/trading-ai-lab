# Rule of Five: Detecting Non-Zero Minimum from Small Samples

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 240–241

## Summary

Heuristic: if a sample of 5 observations all lie above (or below) some threshold, there is a 93.75% probability that the true population median is above (below) that threshold. This non-parametric rule requires no distributional assumption. Applied to trading: if 5 consecutive trades all profit, you have ~94% confidence the strategy median is positive. However, this says nothing about mean or variance — a strategy with positive median can still have negative mean if the loss distribution has a fat left tail.
