# Post-earnings announcement drift (PEAD) and earnings reversals

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 105–107

## Summary

PEAD (Confidence Level 2): stocks continue moving in the direction of the earnings surprise for 60+ days. Strongest for small caps. Trade: buy calls/puts in direction of the surprise after announcement. Win/loss ratio 1.70, but must verify each case. Earnings reversals (Confidence Level 2): some stocks overshoot on earnings day and partially reverse over the next week. Fade very large moves (>10% on high volume). FOMC vol (Confidence Level 2): implied vol around FOMC announcements overstates subsequent moves. Short straddles into FOMC dates have historically positive expected value.
