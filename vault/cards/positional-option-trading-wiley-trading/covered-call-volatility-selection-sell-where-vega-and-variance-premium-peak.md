# Covered Call Volatility Selection: Sell Where Vega and Variance Premium Peak

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 172–174

## Summary

The variance premium (collected from selling) peaks at strikes just above ATM, where vega is highest. Far OTM calls have very little vega so they collect almost no variance premium even though their IV may appear elevated. The covered call should be sold just above the current price (ATM or slightly OTM) to maximize the volatility premium collected. Selling deep ITM calls is equivalent to just holding cash — no variance premium benefit. Selling deep OTM calls captures almost no premium while forfeiting upside participation.
