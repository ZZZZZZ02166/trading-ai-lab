# Interest rate and dividend adjustments to BSM

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 229–231

## Summary

Interest rates: daily rate changes have SD of 0.037% vs VIX daily changes of 1.7 points. Rate uncertainty is negligible relative to vol uncertainty — stochastic rate models add no practical value for option traders. Dividends: adjust S → S − PV(dividends). American calls: early exercise optimal just before ex-dividend date when dividend > remaining time value. The magnitude of required rate corrections vs vol corrections is typically 10:1 in favor of vol corrections — vol forecasting is far more important than rate modeling.
