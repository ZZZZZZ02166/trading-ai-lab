# Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD) as a Directional Catalyst

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 100–102

## Summary

Post-earnings announcement drift: stocks tend to continue moving in the direction of the earnings surprise for 60+ days after announcement. Effect is strongest for small-cap stocks with high transaction costs (limiting arbitrage). Positive PEAD: buy calls after a positive surprise. Negative PEAD: buy puts after a negative surprise. This converts the earnings trade from pure volatility play to a combined vol + directional play. Risk: PEAD is noisy and stocks with the largest expected drift often have the most IV premium priced in.
