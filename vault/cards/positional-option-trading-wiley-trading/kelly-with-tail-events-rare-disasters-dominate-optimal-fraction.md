# Kelly with tail events: rare disasters dominate optimal fraction

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 187–190

## Summary

Figure 9.2: Adding a rare tail event (P3=1%, W3=−3×) to a three-outcome model. Growth rate curve peaks at much lower f (~0.03) vs the base case, and turns sharply negative at f>0.10. Equation 9.3: B_n = B_{n-1} + f·B_{n-1}·g(X_n). Key insight: a 1% probability of losing 3× stake reduces the optimal Kelly fraction to very small values. This is the mathematical reason why short-vol strategies require much smaller position sizes than naive Kelly estimates suggest — the fat-tail events that appear rarely in historical data may dominate the optimal fraction.
