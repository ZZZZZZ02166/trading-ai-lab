# Manipulation and Fraud: Options Markets Are Not Immune

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 219–220

## Summary

Historical cases of manipulation affecting options traders: Sumitomo copper manipulation (Hamanaka, 1995), Madoff options strategy fraud, various pump-and-dump schemes. Red flags: unusually consistent returns with low drawdown (Madoff), strategies that only work with privileged information, counterparties unwilling to explain their edge. Due diligence checklist: verified track record, audited accounts, regulatory registration, clear explanation of strategy, no unusual custody arrangements.
