# VIX Term Structure: Contango Explains Most of the Variance Premium

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 69–73

## Summary

The VIX term structure is normally in contango (longer-dated VIX futures > spot VIX) because investors pay a premium for protection against future vol spikes. This creates a persistent roll yield for short VIX positions. VIX futures converge toward the cash VIX at expiration, not toward the futures price — cash VIX is a better predictor of future VIX than the futures price. Selling front-month VIX futures when term structure is steep captures both roll yield and variance premium.
