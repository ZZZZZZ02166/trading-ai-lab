# Effective bid-ask spread: far wider than posted for large orders

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 245–245

## Summary

Table A3.1: UVXY order book (August 10, 2016). Best bid 20.64, best ask 20.65 — posted spread $0.01. But buying 5,000 shares requires walking up the book to 20.69, giving an effective spread of $0.0612 — 6× the posted spread. Key principle: value trades against the decision price (mid-price), not the bid or ask. This prevents being psychologically intimidated into worse fills. For options: the indicated spread might be bid 9.0 for 100 options, but the actual effective spread for larger sizes is always wider. Always estimate true execution cost before trading, especially for large positions in less liquid instruments.
