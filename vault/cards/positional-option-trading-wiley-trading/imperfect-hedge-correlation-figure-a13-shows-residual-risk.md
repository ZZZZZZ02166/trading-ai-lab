# Imperfect hedge correlation: Figure A1.3 shows residual risk

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 234–237

## Summary

Figure A1.3: SD of P/L for an option hedged with imperfectly correlated underlying. X-axis: Correlation 0–1. Y-axis: SD($) 0–$40,000. At correlation=0: SD ~$35,000 (essentially unhedged). At correlation=0.9: SD ~$10,000 (still substantial residual risk). At correlation=1.0: SD=0 (perfect hedge). Practical implication: using SPY to hedge an individual stock with 70–80% correlation leaves ~25% of the P/L variance unhedged. Proxy hedging is much better than no hedging but not close to perfect hedging.
