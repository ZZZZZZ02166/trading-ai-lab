# Kelly growth rate formula and half-Kelly as risk-efficiency optimum

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 200–201

## Summary

Growth rate: GR(f) = (f − f²/2)·μ²/σ². Table 9.2: Scale factors to keep over-betting probability below target levels. Equation 9.38: Prob(f < f*) = Z((f − f*)·√(n−1) / sd(f̂)). Figure 9.5: Fractional Kelly scaling factors by confidence level. GR is maximized at f=1 (full Kelly), falls to zero at f=2, negative for f>2. The growth-rate-to-drawdown ratio peaks at f=0.5 (half-Kelly). Half-Kelly maximizes risk-adjusted returns — gives the most growth per unit of drawdown. Equations 9.39–9.40: Ruin probability P(A,B) = (1−A^(1−2/f))/(B^(1−2/f)−A^(1−2/f)).
