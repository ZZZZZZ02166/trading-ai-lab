# Variance and skew premia dominate over directional accuracy

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 183–185

## Summary

General principle: variance and skew premia are more important to position profitability than getting the directional call right. A moderately wrong directional view with a short-vol structure can still be profitable if the vol premium is captured. Crash hedge comparison (Tables 8.13–8.14): one-by-two put spread provides better downside protection (higher P/L at −30% and −20% moves) than single OTM put, because the ratio structure collects skew premium from the overpriced short-strike put leg. The skew makes ratio spreads structurally attractive for crash hedges.
