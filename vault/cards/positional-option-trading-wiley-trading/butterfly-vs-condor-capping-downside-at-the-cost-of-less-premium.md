# Butterfly vs Condor: Capping Downside at the Cost of Less Premium

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 120–123

## Summary

A butterfly (long low strike, short 2×middle, long high strike) limits maximum loss compared to a short straddle, at the cost of collecting less net premium. A condor (long far OTM options + short near OTM options) limits maximum loss compared to a strangle, similarly reducing premium collected. With positive volatility skew (typical for equities), wings are relatively expensive — buying them as protection in a butterfly/condor is more costly. When skew is flat, wing protection is cheap and butterflies/condors are preferable.
