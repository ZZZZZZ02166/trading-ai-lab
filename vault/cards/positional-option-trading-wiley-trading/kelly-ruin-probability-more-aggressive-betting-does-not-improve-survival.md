# Kelly ruin probability: more aggressive betting does NOT improve survival

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 201–204

## Summary

Figures 9.6–9.7: Growth rate and growth-rate-to-drawdown ratio as functions of Kelly fraction. Growth peaks at full Kelly; growth-to-drawdown ratio peaks at half-Kelly. Equation 9.40: Ruin probability formula — probability of doubling before ruin. Counterintuitively: at very low f (0.1× Kelly), ruin probability ~90% before doubling. At full Kelly: ruin probability ~45%. At 2× Kelly: worse than full Kelly. Figure 9.8: Ruin probability curve. Figure 9.9: Expected time to reach target. Equation 9.41: E[T] = expected time to reach goal. Trading at 25–50% Kelly gives best combination of growth efficiency AND survival probability.
