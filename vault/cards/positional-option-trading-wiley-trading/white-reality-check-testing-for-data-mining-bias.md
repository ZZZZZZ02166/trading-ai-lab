# White Reality Check: testing for data mining bias

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 46–48

## Summary

The White Reality Check and Hansen's SPA test detect whether apparent outperformance in backtests results from overfitting to historical data. Without such tests, most 'edges' discovered through large-scale parameter search are artifacts. Requirements for a genuine edge: (1) strong statistical evidence across multiple markets and time periods, (2) a specific, predictive mechanism — not just 'behavioral bias,' (3) survival after controlling for known risk factors, (4) is it a risk premium (persistent) or inefficiency (temporary)?
