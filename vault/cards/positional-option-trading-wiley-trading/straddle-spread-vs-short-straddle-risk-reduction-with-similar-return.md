# Straddle spread vs short straddle: risk reduction with similar return

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 133–135

## Summary

Table 6.15 — Short Straddle vs Straddle Spread (short front-month, long back-month): Straddle: Avg $261, SD $597, Skew −1.4, Max $899, Min −$3,753, Profitable 72%. Spread: Avg $265, SD $210, Skew 0.0, Max $578, Min −$66, Profitable 87%. Figure 6.14: Straddle spread P/L at expiry — tent shape peaking at ~$6 ATM, flat at edges. The spread reduces worst-case loss by 50× (from −$3,753 to −$66) and increases win rate (87% vs 72%) with essentially the same average return. Cost: slightly capped upside. Best use: when vol is elevated and you want downside protection.
