# Delta-hedged P/L formula: the foundation of vol trading

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 27–29

## Summary

For a delta-hedged option position held over a small interval dt, the P/L is: Profit = ½Γ·S²·(σ_realized² − σ_implied²)·dt. This means: if realized vol exceeds implied vol, you profit; if it falls short, you lose. Magnitude depends on gamma (highest ATM) and the squared difference between realized and implied vol. This is the core equation of volatility trading — everything else follows from it.
