# Risk Management Cannot Turn a Negative-Edge Strategy Positive

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 186–187

## Summary

Good risk management can improve the performance of a positive-edge strategy by reducing variance and avoiding ruin. But it cannot transform a strategy with negative expected value into a profitable one. The primary purpose of risk management is loss mitigation and longevity, not edge creation. Traders who over-rely on risk management to 'save' bad strategies are misunderstanding its function. Edge must come first; only then does risk management add value.
