# The Boness Subjective Pricing Model: Incorporating Drift

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 149–151

## Summary

The Boness (1962) model prices call options using the real-world (physical) drift μ instead of BSM's risk-neutral rate r. Formula: C = S·exp(μT)·N(d₃) − X·N(d₄), where d₃,d₄ use μ instead of r. This gives a 'subjective value' for an option conditional on a specific drift forecast. If a trader believes μ = 10% and the market prices at μ = 0% (risk-neutral), the subjective value will be higher for calls and lower for puts than the market price — identifying directional opportunities.
