# Short put vs covered call: P/L distribution comparison

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 168–170

## Summary

Short ATM put (Fig 8.4, Table 8.4): Avg $706, SD $986, Skew −2.22, Kurtosis 4.53, Median $1,192, Profitable 78%. Heavily right-skewed — spike at max profit. Covered call payoff (Fig 8.5): hockey-stick shape with upside capped at strike + premium. Key insight: short put is synthetically equivalent to covered call (put-call parity). Both collect the variance premium while capping upside. Short put is simpler to execute and has similar risk/reward. Choose based on transaction costs, margin, and directional conviction.
