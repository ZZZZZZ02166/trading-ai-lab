# Subaccount method: quarter-Kelly vs fixed stop comparison

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 211–213

## Summary

Figure 9.14: Quarter-Kelly final account distribution — bell-shaped, range $78–$130. Figure 9.15: Equivalent subaccount method (full Kelly on $43.70 subaccount) — same distribution. Figure 9.16: 43.7% subaccount — broader distribution. Table 9.4 — Quarter Kelly vs Fixed Stop vs Trailing Stop: Quarter-Kelly: Avg $101.70, Min $78.30, Avg DD 9%, Max DD 26%. Fixed Stop: Avg $102.20, Min $68.75, Avg DD 12%, Max DD 38%. Trailing: Avg $117.13, Min $69.36, Avg DD 12%, Max DD 30%. Quarter-Kelly has smallest drawdowns of all three. Trailing stop has highest average return but also highest volatility and a long-shot of extreme gains/losses.
