# Rule of Five and Rule of Three: quick statistical heuristics

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 241–243

## Summary

Rule of Five: if 5 observations all lie above a threshold, 93.75% confidence the population median is above that threshold (probability = 1 − 2×(0.5)⁵ = 0.9375). Rule of Three (Equation A2.3): upper 95% bound on probability of a rare event = 3/n, where n is the number of observations without seeing the event. Derivation (Eq A2.4): (1−p)^n = 0.05 → p = 1−0.05^(1/n) ≈ 3/n for large n. Example: never lost >15% in 100 trades → 95% upper bound on that loss probability = 3/100 = 3%. These quick heuristics allow traders to make statistical inferences without full distribution fitting.
