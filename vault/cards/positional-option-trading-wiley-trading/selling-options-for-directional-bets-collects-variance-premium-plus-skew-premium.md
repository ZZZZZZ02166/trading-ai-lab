# Selling Options for Directional Bets Collects Variance Premium Plus Skew Premium

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 152–154

## Summary

OTM calls (covered calls) and OTM puts collect the variance premium AND the vol skew premium. The variance premium (IV > realized vol) is the primary source of profitability. The skew premium provides extra compensation for OTM puts (more expensive than fair value) and slightly less for OTM calls. When using options to express directional views, selling OTM options is generally superior to buying because you receive rather than pay these premia. The exception: buying deep OTM calls captures convex upside while defining maximum loss.
