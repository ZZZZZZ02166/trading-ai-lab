# Meta risks: counterparty failure kills portfolios independent of strategy

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 218–220

## Summary

Market risk is well-understood. Meta risks — counterparty failure, fraud, regulatory changes, liquidity crises — are underappreciated and can cause total loss regardless of strategy quality. Historical examples: MF Global (2011, customer funds misused), Madoff (Ponzi scheme), QuadrigaCX (crypto exchange, CEO holds all keys), Sumitomo copper (price manipulation). Risk mitigation: multiple brokers, prefer regulated exchanges, maintain excess capital outside trading accounts, never concentrate with single counterparty, verify track records with audited returns. Never invest in strategies you don't understand.
