# Variance Premium Exists Across Asset Classes, Not Just Equities

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 75–77

## Summary

The variance premium is not unique to equity markets. It has been documented in commodity options (oil, natural gas, grains), currency options (major pairs), interest rate options, and VIX options. In most cases the premium is statistically significant and economically meaningful. This suggests the premium reflects a structural risk-transfer dynamic (investors paying for insurance) rather than an equity-specific behavioral anomaly. Cross-asset diversification of short-vol positions captures the premium while diversifying idiosyncratic risks.
