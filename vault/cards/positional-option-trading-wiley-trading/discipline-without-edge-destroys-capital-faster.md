# Discipline Without Edge Destroys Capital Faster

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 38–39

## Summary

A trader without a genuine edge who trades in a disciplined, consistent manner will lose money faster and more reliably than an undisciplined trader. Discipline amplifies whatever underlying process you are running — if the process has negative expected value, more discipline means more consistent losses. The first requirement for successful trading is a genuine, positive-expectation edge.
