# Short put spread and risk reversal: directional + vol premium trades

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 175–178

## Summary

Short ATM/20-delta put spread (Fig 8.8, Tables 8.7–8.8): With flat vol: Avg $422, SD $680, Skew −1.42, Profitable 78%. With vol skew (OTM put at 40.8% IV): Avg $216, lower but still positive EV. Risk reversal (short 20-delta put, long 20-delta call, Fig 8.9, Table 8.9): Avg $948, SD $2,140, Skew 2.69, Kurtosis 9.00, Profitable 85%. Risk reversal profits greatly from upside moves and is long positive skew — best when bullish AND skew premium is steep (OTM put overpriced vs call). One-by-two call spread (Fig 8.10): bimodal distribution, riskier — use only with very high directional conviction.
