# Hedging Frequency vs Transaction Costs Tradeoff

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Positional Option Trading (Wiley Trading) — pp. 31–35

## Summary

Continuous delta hedging eliminates directional risk entirely but incurs continuous transaction costs. In practice, less frequent hedging widens the distribution of P/L outcomes but reduces costs. The right hedging frequency depends on: position gamma (high gamma → hedge more often), transaction costs (high costs → hedge less often), and acceptable P/L variance. Ignoring hedging costs systematically overstates the attractiveness of long-vol strategies.
