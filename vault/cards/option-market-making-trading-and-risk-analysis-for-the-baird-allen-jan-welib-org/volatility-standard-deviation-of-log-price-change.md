# Volatility: Standard Deviation of Log Price Change

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 35–36

## Summary

In options pricing, volatility (S) is defined as the annualized standard deviation of log percentage price changes. It measures the typical magnitude of price fluctuation. A 10% volatility for an asset at price 100 means there is approximately a 68% chance the asset will be between 90 and 110 after one year (±1 standard deviation). Volatility is expressed as either a percentage or as an annualized standard deviation in points (SDP).
