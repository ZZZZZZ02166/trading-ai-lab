# Position Risk Appendix: 22 Core Single-Month Risk Profiles

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 67–180

## Summary

The book's appendix contains detailed payoff and Greek risk profile charts (delta, gamma, theta, kappa) for all 22 core single-month option positions at both 30 days and at expiration. These profiles are the fundamental reference for understanding how each strategy behaves across the full price range. Every market maker should internalize these profiles to immediately recognize the risk character of any position encountered in practice.
