# Contango vs Backwardation in Futures Markets

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 44–44

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.9] Futures markets are in contango when distant contract months trade at a premium to near months — typical for storable commodities with positive cost of carry (storage + financing). Markets are in backwardation when near months trade above distant months, usually due to supply shortage or negative convenience yield. Understanding term structure (contango/backwardation) is important for options on futures markets.
