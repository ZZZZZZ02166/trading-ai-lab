# Market Making Costs Reduce Gross Spread Income

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 15–16

## Summary

Market makers pay exchange membership fees, office overhead, data services, and clearing costs that reduce gross bid/offer profits. Additionally, order-flow competition means edge is sometimes sacrificed to attract business. Studies of securities dealers and futures scalpers show that net profit per trade is well below the full bid/offer spread — successful market making requires high volume to make these thin margins work.
