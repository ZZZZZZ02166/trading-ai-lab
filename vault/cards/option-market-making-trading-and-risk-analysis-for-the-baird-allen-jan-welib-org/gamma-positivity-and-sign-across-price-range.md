# Gamma Positivity and Sign Across Price Range

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 64–65

## Summary

For long options (long calls or puts), gamma is always positive at every futures price. For short options, gamma is always negative. For spreads, gamma can be positive over some price ranges and negative over others (bimodal or trimodal). A long straddle has unimodal positive gamma — always benefits from large moves. A long butterfly has trimodal gamma — benefits from small moves ATM but loses from large moves. Understanding gamma sign by price range is essential.
