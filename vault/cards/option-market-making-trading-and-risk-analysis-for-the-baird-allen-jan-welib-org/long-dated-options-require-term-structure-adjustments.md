# Long-Dated Options Require Term Structure Adjustments

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 35–35

## Summary

For options lasting longer than one year, the BSM assumption of a flat interest rate term structure becomes increasingly inaccurate. Long-dated bond options require estimates of multiple future interest rates. Additionally, log-normal models become less accurate for very long-dated options because they cannot account for mean reversion and other long-term structural properties of price series. Alternative binomial and lattice models handle these cases better.
