# Overtrading Destroys Edge by Spreading Attention Too Thin

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 188–188

## Summary

Overtrading — trading too many contracts or too many different strikes simultaneously — is a common market maker mistake. When transaction volume exceeds the trader's ability to monitor and hedge all positions, errors accumulate. A market maker should trade only at their prices and only in quantities where the edge is clear. It is better to trade fewer contracts at strong edge prices than many contracts at thin or no edge. Patience is a core market-making virtue.
