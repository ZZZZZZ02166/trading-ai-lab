# Wheat Futures Show Consistent Fat-Tail Price Changes

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 32–33

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.5] Analysis of September wheat futures price changes across multiple years shows that the empirical frequency distribution consistently deviates from the normal curve: more observations cluster near zero change AND more observations appear in the extreme tails than the normal distribution predicts. This pattern is robust across different contract years (1970, 1974, 1980, 1986), confirming fat-tail behavior in commodity price changes.
