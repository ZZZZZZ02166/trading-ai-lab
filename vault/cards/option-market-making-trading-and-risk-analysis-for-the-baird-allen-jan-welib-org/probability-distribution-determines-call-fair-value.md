# Probability Distribution Determines Call Fair Value

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 25–26

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.3] If outcomes from 95 to 105 each have known probabilities, the fair value of a 100 call is the sum of (outcome − 100) × probability for each outcome above 100. For a symmetric bell-shaped distribution centered at 100, the fair value calculated this way is approximately $0.88. This demonstrates how option pricing reduces to expected value calculation once the price distribution is specified.
