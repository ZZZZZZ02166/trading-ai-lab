# Options Trading is a Zero-Sum Game

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 14–15

## Summary

For every buyer of an option there is a seller; what one trader makes in profit, the other must lose. Futures and options exchange trading neither adds to nor subtracts from total wealth but shifts it from one group to another. This zero-sum characteristic means sustainable market-making profit must come from the bid/offer edge, not from directional speculation.
