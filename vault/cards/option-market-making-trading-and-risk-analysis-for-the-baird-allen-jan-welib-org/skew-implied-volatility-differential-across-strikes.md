# Skew: Implied Volatility Differential Across Strikes

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 59–59

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 3.3] Implied volatility skew refers to the fact that options at different strikes in the same expiration cycle often trade at different implied volatility levels. A positive skew means OTM options (both puts and calls) trade at higher IV than ATM options — a 'volatility smile.' The center of the skew is defined by the ATM straddle's implied vol. Skew risk arises when the shape or slope of this curve changes.
