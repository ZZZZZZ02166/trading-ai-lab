# Every Carryover Position Resembles a Core Option Type

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 67–67

## Summary

No matter how large or complex a market maker's accumulated carryover position becomes, it will always resemble one of the 22 basic single-month option position types on a risk basis. Any combination of options will show either unimodal, bimodal, or trimodal risk characteristics. Understanding the 22 core risk profiles gives the market maker a mental model to instantly categorize and manage any complex position.
