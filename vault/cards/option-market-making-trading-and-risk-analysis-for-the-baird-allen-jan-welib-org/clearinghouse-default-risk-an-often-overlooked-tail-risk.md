# Clearinghouse Default Risk: An Often-Overlooked Tail Risk

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 61–62

## Summary

Extramarket (non-quantifiable) risks are those that cannot be measured with standard Greek risk models. The most serious is clearinghouse or broker default — a market maker can lose their entire capital if a counterparty defaults. While clearinghouses reduce this risk through margin requirements, a cascade of failures (as seen with Volume Investors in 1985 and Fossett in 1989) can still propagate to innocent participants. This risk requires adequate capitalization as a buffer.
