# Gamma Peaks ATM and Spikes Near Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 53–54

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 3.1 bottom panel] Gamma is highest for ATM options and approaches zero for deep ITM or OTM options. As expiration approaches, the ATM gamma spike grows dramatically — an option one day from expiration has gamma concentrated in a very narrow range around the strike. This creates the 'pin risk' near expiration: a small price move can cause enormous delta changes for short option positions held through expiration.
