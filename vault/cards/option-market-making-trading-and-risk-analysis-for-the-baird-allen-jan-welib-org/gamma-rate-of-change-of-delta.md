# Gamma: Rate of Change of Delta

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 52–53

## Summary

Gamma (Γ) is the change in delta for each dollar change in the underlying price. It measures the curvature of the option's price curve. Long options always have positive gamma (delta increases when ITM, decreases when OTM). Short options have negative gamma. ATM options have the highest gamma; deep ITM or OTM options have near-zero gamma. Near expiration, ATM gamma spikes dramatically.
