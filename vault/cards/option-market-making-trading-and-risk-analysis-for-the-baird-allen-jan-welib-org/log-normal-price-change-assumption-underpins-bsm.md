# Log-Normal Price Change Assumption Underpins BSM

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 27–28

## Summary

The BSM model assumes futures price changes follow a log-normal distribution — i.e., the logarithm of price change is normally distributed. This means price changes can be described by a mean and standard deviation, and the probability of any outcome can be derived from the normal distribution curve. While this assumption simplifies pricing, empirical evidence shows actual price changes have fatter tails and higher peaks than the log-normal predicts.
