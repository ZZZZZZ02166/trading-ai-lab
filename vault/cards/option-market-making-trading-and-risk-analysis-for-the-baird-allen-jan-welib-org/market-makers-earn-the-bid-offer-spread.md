# Market Makers Earn the Bid-Offer Spread

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 10–13

## Summary

A market maker's primary income comes from repeatedly buying at the bid and selling at the offer, capturing the price spread as gross profit. This is known as 'getting the edge.' The market maker does not take directional views; the goal is risk-neutral profit from providing liquidity, not from predicting price movement.
