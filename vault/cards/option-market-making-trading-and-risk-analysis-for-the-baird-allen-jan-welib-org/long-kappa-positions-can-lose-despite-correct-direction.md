# Long Kappa Positions Can Lose Despite Correct Direction

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 58–58

## Summary

If a trader buys a call expecting a price rise, but implied volatility falls when the price rises, the position can lose money despite being directionally correct. This 'long kappa, price rises, IV falls' scenario is common — sharp price moves often cause market participants to lower their volatility expectations. Traders who do not understand kappa frequently blame the market maker for 'cheating' them when their profitable move still results in a loss.
