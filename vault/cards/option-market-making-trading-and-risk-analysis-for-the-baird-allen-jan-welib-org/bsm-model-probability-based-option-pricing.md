# BSM Model: Probability-Based Option Pricing

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 23–24

## Summary

The Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) model, developed by Black and Scholes (1973) and modified by Merton, derives option fair value from statistical models of probability. It uses five inputs: current futures price, strike price, days to expiration, risk-free interest rate, and volatility (standard deviation of log price change). The model links the probability distribution of future price outcomes to the expected payoff schedule, providing a theoretical fair value.
