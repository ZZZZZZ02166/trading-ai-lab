# Financial Returns Are Leptokurtic, Not Normally Distributed

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 30–31

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.4] Empirical evidence consistently shows financial asset returns have higher central peaks and fatter tails than the normal distribution predicts — a property called leptokurtosis. Long-term stock and commodity records show observations 5-6 standard deviations from the mean that should be virtually impossible under normality. This fat-tail behavior means the BSM model systematically underprices deep out-of-the-money options and understates tail risk.
