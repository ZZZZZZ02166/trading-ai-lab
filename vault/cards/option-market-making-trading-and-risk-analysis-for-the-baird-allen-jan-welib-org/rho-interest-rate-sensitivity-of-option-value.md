# Rho: Interest Rate Sensitivity of Option Value

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 58–59

## Summary

Rho (ρ) is the dollar change in option price per one-percentage-point change in the risk-free interest rate. For futures options (using the Fischer Black model), rho is generally small because the futures price already incorporates the cost of carry. For stock/bond options, rho is more significant — higher interest rates reduce the present value of the exercise price, increasing call values and decreasing put values.
