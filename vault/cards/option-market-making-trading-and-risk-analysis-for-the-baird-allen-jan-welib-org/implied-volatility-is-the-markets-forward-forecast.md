# Implied Volatility is the Market's Forward Forecast

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 41–42

## Summary

Implied volatility (IV) is derived by solving the BSM formula for the volatility that makes the theoretical price equal to the observed market price. IV is the market's current consensus estimate of future volatility for that expiration. When IV is high relative to recent historical volatility, the market is pricing in an expectation of larger future moves. When IV is low, the market expects calm. Neither extreme is reliably predictive.
