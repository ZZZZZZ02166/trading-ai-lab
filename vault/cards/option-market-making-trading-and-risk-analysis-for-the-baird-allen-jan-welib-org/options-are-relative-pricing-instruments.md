# Options Are Relative Pricing Instruments

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 26–26

## Summary

Market makers need not know the 'true' fair value of an option in absolute terms — they use the BSM model primarily for relative pricing (i.e., comparing one option to another). Even if the BSM model's normality assumption is imperfect, it provides a consistent framework to identify when one option is mispriced relative to others. Profitable market making depends on relative pricing accuracy, not absolute fair value.
