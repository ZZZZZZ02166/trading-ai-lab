# Time Spread Risk: Calendar Spread Exposure

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 60–61

## Summary

Time spreads (calendar spreads) hold simultaneous positions in options of different expiration months. They are subject to two additional time-based risks not present in single-month positions: (1) Time Delta — the risk from differential sensitivity to underlying futures price changes across contract months; (2) Time Kappa/Vega — the risk from divergent implied volatility changes between months. Both can cause large P&L swings independent of single-month risks.
