# Risk Sign Modality Determines Position Risk Character

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 63–64

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 4.2] The number of times a position's gamma, kappa, or theta changes sign across the futures price range determines its 'risk sign modality.' Unimodal positions (single sign across all prices) include straddles and single options. Bimodal positions change sign once — characteristic of vertical spreads. Trimodal positions change sign twice — butterflies and condors. Irregular positions from complex carryovers can have many modes. Modality predicts how risk behaves as price moves.
