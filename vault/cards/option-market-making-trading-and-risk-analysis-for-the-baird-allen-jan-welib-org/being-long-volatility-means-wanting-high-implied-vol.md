# Being Long Volatility Means Wanting High Implied Vol

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 58–58

## Summary

A position that benefits from rising implied volatility is 'long volatility.' Long options (calls or puts) are long volatility — they benefit from IV increases. Short options are short volatility — they benefit from IV decreases. Market makers who accumulate net long option positions are net long volatility and benefit when market uncertainty rises. The goal is to be long volatility at low IV and short at high IV.
