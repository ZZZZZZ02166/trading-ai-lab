# Cost of Carry Affects Option Pricing for Non-Futures Assets

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 43–44

## Summary

For income-earning assets (stocks, bonds, currency), the cost of carry — dividends, interest, or yield — must be incorporated into option fair value. A dividend-paying stock's call option must discount the forward price by expected dividends. For negative-carry assets (commodities with storage costs), futures may trade above spot. The BSM futures model avoids these adjustments since futures prices already incorporate carry.
