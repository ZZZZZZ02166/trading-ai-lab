# Option Risk Dollar Size Must Include Full Contract Value

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 48–49

## Summary

A 10-cent gamma risk on one futures option must be multiplied by the dollar value of the contract (e.g., $500/point for S&P500) to get the actual dollar risk ($50). For a 10-option position in a $500/point contract, gamma risk is $500. Market makers who think only in cents per option will dramatically underestimate actual risk exposure. Always translate Greek risk values to dollar amounts using full contract multipliers.
