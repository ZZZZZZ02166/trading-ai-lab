# Pure Market Making: Profit from Liquidity, Not Direction

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 18–19

## Summary

The perspective of pure market making is that income comes solely from providing liquidity (capturing the bid/offer spread), not from speculative gain or directional bets. Market-making goals are risk-neutral: make a profit regardless of whether the market goes up or down. This requires understanding catastrophic option risk and establishing practical measures to avoid it — otherwise, one risks bankruptcy over the long run.
