# Standard Deviation in Points (SDP): Volatility to Price

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 37–38

## Summary

Standard Deviation in Points (SDP) converts annualized percentage volatility to an expected price range. Formula: SDP_t = P × (V / √PY), where P = current price, V = volatility percentage, PY = number of periods in a year. Example: for P=100, V=15%, over one day (PY=250), SDP = 100 × (0.15/√250) = 0.95 points/day. This translates abstract volatility percentages into concrete expected daily price moves.
