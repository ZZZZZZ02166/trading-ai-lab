# Empirical Returns Show Fat Tails and High Central Peak

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 31–32

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.4] When real financial asset returns are overlaid against a theoretical normal curve, the empirical distribution shows: (1) a taller, sharper central peak (more observations near the mean), (2) heavier tails (more extreme observations than predicted). This pattern — called platykurtosis in the tails, leptokurtosis overall — appears consistently across stocks, bonds, and commodities, and is a fundamental challenge for log-normal options pricing models.
