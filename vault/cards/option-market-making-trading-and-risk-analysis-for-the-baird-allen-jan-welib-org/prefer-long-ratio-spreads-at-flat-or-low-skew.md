# Prefer Long Ratio Spreads at Flat or Low Skew

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 181–182

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 8.4] When implied volatility skew is flat or negative (wings cheap relative to ATM), long ratio spreads are attractive. When skew shifts to high positive (wings expensive), long ratio spreads lose because the short wing positions are marked against the holder. The strategic rule: buy ratio spreads at flat/low skew, sell them at high skew. Skew wobble — random fluctuations around the trend — can cause daily P&L noise without a directional move.
