# Volatility is the Only Unknown in the BSM Model

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 29–30

## Summary

Of the five BSM inputs, four are directly observable: current futures price, strike price, days to expiration, and risk-free interest rate. Only volatility (the standard deviation of future price changes) must be estimated. This makes volatility the central challenge of options pricing — different traders with different volatility estimates will arrive at different fair values. This uncertainty creates both trading opportunity and risk.
