# Implied and Historical Volatility Often Diverge

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 41–42

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.8] October cotton futures data shows that implied volatility and 20-day lagged historical volatility frequently diverge — sometimes by 10+ percentage points. Implied vol leads historical vol and often overshoots actual future realized vol. Using only historical vol to set prices would frequently result in mispricing relative to where the market is trading. Market makers must track both and understand the divergence.
