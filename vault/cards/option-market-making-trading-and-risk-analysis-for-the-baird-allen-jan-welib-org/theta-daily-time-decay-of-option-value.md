# Theta: Daily Time Decay of Option Value

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 54–56

## Summary

Theta (Θ) is the daily change in option price due to the passage of time alone, holding all other variables constant. Long options have negative theta — they lose value each day as time passes. Short options have positive theta — they gain value each day. ATM options have the largest absolute theta; deep ITM and far OTM options have smaller theta. Theta and gamma are closely related and have opposite signs for long positions.
