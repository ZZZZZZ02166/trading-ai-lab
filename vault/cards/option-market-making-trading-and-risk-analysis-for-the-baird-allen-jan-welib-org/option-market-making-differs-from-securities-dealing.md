# Option Market Making Differs from Securities Dealing

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 16–17

## Summary

Unlike stock or bond dealers who can go flat at end of day, option market makers accumulate carryover positions across multiple strikes and expirations that cannot be fully liquidated quickly. This carryover risk is unique to options — the position's value depends on five variables (price, strike, time, volatility, interest rate) rather than just price. Managing this complex ongoing exposure is the central challenge of option market making.
