# Delta Neutrality Requires Constant Rehedging Due to Gamma

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 51–52

## Summary

A position's delta changes continuously as the underlying price moves — this is gamma. A delta-neutral position struck at price 100 will have nonzero delta if price moves to 105 or 95. Market makers must rehedge by buying or selling futures to restore neutrality. The more active the underlying, the more frequent the rehedging required. High gamma positions near expiration require very frequent adjustments.
