# Net Kappa/Vega Imbalance Must Be Corrected Promptly

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 189–190

## Summary

As a market maker accumulates trades, net kappa/vega can drift significantly long or short if one side of the market consistently buys or sells options. If net kappa becomes too large on one side, the position is exposed to large P&L swings from implied volatility changes — unrelated to market-making activity. The rule: stay net kappa-neutral, or at most slightly long. Correct large kappa imbalances by adjusting spread ratios or trading the opposite side even at a small concession.
