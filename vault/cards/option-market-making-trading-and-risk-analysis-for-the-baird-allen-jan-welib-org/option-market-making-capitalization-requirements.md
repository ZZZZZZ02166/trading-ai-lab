# Option Market Making Capitalization Requirements

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 192–193

## Summary

Exchange memberships (seats) range from $1,000 to $500,000 depending on the exchange. Capital requirements for option market making should be well above the theoretical maximum loss of all positions — exchange minimums are typically $25,000-$50,000 but active market makers in large contracts need substantially more. Leasing memberships reduces upfront capital requirements. The 1990s growth of electronic and OTC markets has made market making accessible without physical floor presence.
