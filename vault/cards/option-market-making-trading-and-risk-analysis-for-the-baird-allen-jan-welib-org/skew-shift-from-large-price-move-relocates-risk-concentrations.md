# Skew Shift from Large Price Move Relocates Risk Concentrations

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 182–182

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 8.4] When the underlying makes a large upward move, the entire implied volatility skew shifts upward — what was an OTM call strike becomes near ATM, and the put wing now carries higher IV. For vertical spread traders, this shift can turn a profitable position into a losing one even with no change in the underlying's position relative to the original strikes. Skew shift risk cannot be hedged by standard Greek measures alone.
