# Delta Neutral Position Eliminates Short-Term Price Risk

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 51–52

## Summary

A delta-neutral position has net delta of zero — the position neither gains nor loses value from small, immediate moves in the underlying. This is achieved by combining options (positive or negative delta) with futures (delta ±1.0). Market makers seek delta neutrality to isolate and capture the bid/offer spread without directional exposure. However, delta neutrality is not static — it requires continuous rebalancing as the underlying moves (gamma).
