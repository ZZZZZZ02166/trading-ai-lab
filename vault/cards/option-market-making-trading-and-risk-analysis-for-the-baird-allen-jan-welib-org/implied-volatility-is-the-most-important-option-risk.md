# Implied Volatility is the Most Important Option Risk

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 42–43

## Summary

Kappa/vega risk — the sensitivity of option price to changes in implied volatility — is one of the most important risks for option market makers. Options can show profits from directional moves only to have those profits evaporate when implied volatility falls. Conversely, options can increase in value even when the underlying moves against the position if implied volatility rises sufficiently. Managing vega exposure is central to sustained profitability.
