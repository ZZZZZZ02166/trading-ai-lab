# Hedge Delta When You Should, Not When It is Most Convenient

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 186–186

## Summary

The cardinal rule of market making is 'hedge when you should, not when it is best.' Waiting for the ideal hedging moment — when futures are moving favorably — is speculation. Any time a market maker waits to hedge, they are taking a directional position. Small losses from disciplined immediate hedging are far preferable to large losses from delayed hedges that moved against the position. Discipline in delta hedging is the single most important market-making habit.
