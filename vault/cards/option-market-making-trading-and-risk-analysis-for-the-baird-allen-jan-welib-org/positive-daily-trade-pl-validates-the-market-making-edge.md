# Positive Daily Trade P&L Validates the Market-Making Edge

**Type:** strategy_idea
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 185–186

## Summary

If a market maker's total profits are mostly from carryover P&L (mark-to-market gains on existing positions) and daily trading P&L is near zero, the trader is primarily speculating rather than market making. A genuine market maker shows consistently positive daily trading P&L across many cycles, regardless of carryover. The daily trading account is the truest measure of whether a trader is actually capturing the bid/offer spread as intended.
