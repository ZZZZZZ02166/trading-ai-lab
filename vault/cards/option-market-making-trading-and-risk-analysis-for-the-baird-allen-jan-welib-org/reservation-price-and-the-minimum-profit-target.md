# Reservation Price and the Minimum Profit Target

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 15–16

## Summary

The reservation price is the reduced expected gross profit after accounting for exchange fees, overhead, and the reality that the market maker cannot always capture the full bid/offer spread on every trade. Because brokers sometimes fill orders at better than equilibrium and edge is given away on some trades, the net profit per round-turn is well below the full spread. High volume with low containable risk is essential for profitability.
