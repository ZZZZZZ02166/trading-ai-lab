# Floor Trading Requires Numbers Sense, Stamina, and Discipline

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 190–191

## Summary

Successful option floor trading requires: strong mental arithmetic (quick calculation of delta, fair value, and edge in a noisy pit); physical stamina and reflexes for a physically demanding environment; self-esteem to withstand the aggressive social dynamics of the floor; and a cooperative style — building good relationships with brokers and other market makers sustains long-term order flow. Poor pit manners or bad reputation reduces business over time.
