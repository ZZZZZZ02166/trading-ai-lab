# Fair Value = Sum of Probability-Weighted Payoffs

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 24–25

## Summary

The fair value of any bet or option is the sum of each possible outcome multiplied by its probability: V = Σ(P × $). For a call option, fair value is the probability-weighted average of all possible in-the-money payoffs at expiration. This framework shows that once the probability distribution of future prices is known, fair value can be computed exactly — the only challenge is estimating those probabilities accurately.
