# Quirkiness: Complex Positions Develop Unpredictable Risk Near Expiration

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 65–66

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 4.1] A complex market maker carryover position spanning many strikes can develop 'quirkiness' near expiration — the position takes on the risk characteristics of the nearest single-option position as expiration approaches and standard deviations shrink. This means a position that looked benign a month earlier can suddenly develop large localized risk near specific strikes at expiration. Regular position reviews against all strikes are essential.
