# 100-105 Call Spread Value Changes Significantly with Skew Level

**Type:** volatility_insight
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 181–182

## Summary

The fair value of a 100-105 call spread varies substantially depending on the implied volatility skew level: with flat skew at IV=15, the 1×1 spread is worth $1.66 and the 1×2 ratio spread is $0.92. With high positive skew, they drop to $1.42/$0.44. Skew directly affects the relative pricing of OTM options vs ATM. Market makers must know current skew levels to avoid systematically mispricing vertical and ratio spreads.
