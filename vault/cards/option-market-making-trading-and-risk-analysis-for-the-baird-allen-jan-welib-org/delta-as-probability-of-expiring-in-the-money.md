# Delta as Probability of Expiring In-the-Money

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 50–51

## Summary

An option's delta is also approximately equal to the probability that the option will expire in-the-money under the risk-neutral probability measure. An ATM call with delta of +0.50 has roughly a 50% chance of expiring ITM. A deep ITM call with delta near 1.0 has near-100% probability. This probabilistic interpretation helps market makers intuitively assess how option fair values relate to expiration probabilities.
