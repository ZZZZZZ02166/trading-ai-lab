# Historical vs Implied Volatility: Past vs Future

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 39–40

## Summary

Historical (statistical) volatility (HV) is calculated from past price changes and measures what volatility has been. Implied volatility (IV) is derived by inverting the BSM formula with the market option price — it represents the market's forward-looking estimate of future volatility. HV is always known with certainty; IV is the market's consensus forecast. Neither is a reliable predictor of future realized volatility.
