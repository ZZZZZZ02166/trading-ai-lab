# 22 Core Option Positions Cover All Market Maker Strategies

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 62–62

## Summary

All option trading strategies for a single-month cycle can be classified as combinations of 22 core positions: long/short call, long/short put, straddle, strangle, bull/bear spread, fence, ratio spreads (call and put), long/short cartwheels, long/short wrangles, long/short butterflies, condors, conversions/reversals. Any complex carryover position the market maker accumulates will resemble one of these 22 basic positions from a risk standpoint.
