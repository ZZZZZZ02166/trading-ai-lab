# Position Risk Modality Shifts Between Risk Types

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 66–67

## Summary

The risk sign modality (unimodal, bimodal, trimodal) is characteristic of a position type but can shift as the position evolves. The key insight: most spreads have bimodal gamma risk — one sign on the upside, the opposite on the downside. The modal peaks are always nearest to the short option strikes. Knowing where the peaks of each risk type fall helps the market maker predict how their total position risk changes as markets move.
