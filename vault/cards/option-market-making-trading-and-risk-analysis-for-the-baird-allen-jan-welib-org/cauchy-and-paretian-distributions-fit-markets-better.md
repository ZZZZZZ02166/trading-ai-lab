# Cauchy and Paretian Distributions Fit Markets Better

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 34–34

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.7] Mandlebrot (1964) proposed that financial asset returns resemble Paretian (fat-tailed) rather than normal distributions. The Cauchy distribution — which has infinite variance and very heavy tails — provides a better statistical fit to wheat futures price changes than the normal curve. These fat-tailed distributions imply that extreme price events occur far more frequently than BSM-based models suggest, requiring option market makers to price tail risk more carefully.
