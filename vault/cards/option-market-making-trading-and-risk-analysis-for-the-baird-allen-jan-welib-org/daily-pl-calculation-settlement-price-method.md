# Daily P&L Calculation: Settlement Price Method

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 184–184

## Summary

To calculate daily carryover P&L: value the entire position at Day 1 settlement prices, then at Day 2 settlement prices without new trades — the difference is carryover P&L. To calculate daily trade P&L: for each new trade, use trade price versus settlement price. Total net P&L = carryover P&L + daily trade P&L. This method reconciles to clearing statements and allows precise attribution of profits between speculation and market making.
