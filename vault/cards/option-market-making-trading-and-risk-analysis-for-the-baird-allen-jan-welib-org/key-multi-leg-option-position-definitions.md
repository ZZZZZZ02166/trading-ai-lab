# Key Multi-Leg Option Position Definitions

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 62–62

## Summary

Straddle: same strike call + put (long or short). Strangle: different strike call + put (long or short). Vertical spread: long and short options at different strikes, same expiration. Fence: long put and short call (or vice versa) at different strikes — a collar. Ratio spread: long one, short multiple at different strikes. Butterfly: long one center strike, short two wings. Condor: long two outer strikes, short two inner. All are single-expiration strategies.
