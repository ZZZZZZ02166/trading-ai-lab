# Catastrophic Option Risk vs Limited-Risk Strategies

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 18–19

## Summary

Not all option trading positions are catastrophically exposed; some are inherently limited-risk. A market maker who understands the distinction can earn high rates of return with low or containable risk. Those who do not understand this distinction risk financial catastrophe — the 1980s witnessed multiple multi-million-dollar bankruptcies of option trading firms that failed to manage carryover risk.
