# Option Values Can Explode or Collapse Rapidly

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 17–17

## Summary

Options are highly leveraged instruments that do not move dollar-for-dollar with the underlying asset. Option values can shrink or explode at rapidly changing rates — sometimes much faster than the underlying. Time decay continuously erodes option value, but price moves can cause sudden large changes. Option market makers must understand all these risk dimensions deeply or face catastrophic loss.
