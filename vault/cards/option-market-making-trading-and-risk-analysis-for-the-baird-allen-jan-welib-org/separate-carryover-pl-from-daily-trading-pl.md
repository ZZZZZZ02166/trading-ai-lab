# Separate Carryover P&L from Daily Trading P&L

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 183–184

## Summary

A market maker should track two separate P&L accounts: the carryover account (daily mark-to-market of existing positions) and the daily trading account (profits from new trades that day). This separation reveals whether profit comes from the market-making edge on new trades or from carryover position moves. A consistently positive daily trading P&L with neutral carryover confirms genuine market-making success rather than speculation.
