# Kappa (Vega): Sensitivity to Implied Volatility Change

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 57–58

## Summary

Kappa (K), also called vega, is the dollar change in option price per one-point (1%) change in implied volatility. Formula: K/vega = Dollar change in option price / Positive one-point implied volatility change. A long call always has positive kappa — it benefits from rising implied vol. A short call has negative kappa. ATM long-dated options have the highest kappa; short-dated OTM options have near-zero kappa.
