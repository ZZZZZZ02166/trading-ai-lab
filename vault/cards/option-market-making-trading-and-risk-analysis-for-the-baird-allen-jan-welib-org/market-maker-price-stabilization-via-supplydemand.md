# Market Maker Price Stabilization via Supply/Demand

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 13–13

## Summary

Market makers stabilize prices around a long-term equilibrium (P*) by entering the market whenever prices deviate. When demand pushes prices above equilibrium (P1), they sell; when prices fall below equilibrium (P2), they buy. The bid/offer spread (P1 above, P2 below equilibrium) represents the market maker's compensation for providing this stabilization service.
