# Six Core Option Risks: Delta, Gamma, Theta, Kappa, Rho, Skew

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 47–48

## Summary

Option market makers face six quantifiable market-based risks: (1) Delta — sensitivity to underlying price change; (2) Gamma — sensitivity to delta change (curvature); (3) Theta — time decay; (4) Kappa/Vega — sensitivity to implied volatility change; (5) Rho — sensitivity to interest rate change; (6) Skew — differential IV changes across strikes. Additionally, time spreads expose to time delta and time kappa. Each risk must be understood and managed independently.
