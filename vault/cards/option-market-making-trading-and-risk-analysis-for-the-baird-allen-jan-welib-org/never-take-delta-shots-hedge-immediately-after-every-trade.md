# Never Take Delta 'Shots' — Hedge Immediately After Every Trade

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 186–186

## Summary

The most common and costly mistake is deciding not to hedge delta immediately after completing a non-spread option trade, hoping the market will move favorably first. This is speculation disguised as market making. If the trader was willing to accept a loss immediately (by hedging), they should hedge immediately. Options are often priced at high-volatility estimates — waiting for a favorable delta move risks losing both the directional bet and the original edge.
