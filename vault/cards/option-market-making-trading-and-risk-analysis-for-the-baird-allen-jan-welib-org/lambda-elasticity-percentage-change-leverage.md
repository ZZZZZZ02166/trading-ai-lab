# Lambda (Elasticity): Percentage Change Leverage

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 52–53

## Summary

Lambda (Λ) is the percentage change in option value for a 1% change in the underlying asset price — it measures the leveraged return of an option relative to the underlying. Lambda is always greater than one since options are leveraged instruments. A deep ITM option approaching delta=1 will have lambda approaching 1.0 (near-linear). Far OTM options with small deltas have very high lambda — high percentage leverage for large directional moves.
