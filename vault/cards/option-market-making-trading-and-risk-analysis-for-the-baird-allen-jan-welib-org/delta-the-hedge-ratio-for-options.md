# Delta: The Hedge Ratio for Options

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 49–50

## Summary

Delta (Δ) is the dollar change in option price for each dollar change in the underlying asset price — also called the hedge ratio. Delta ranges from 0 to +1.00 for calls (and 0 to -1.00 for puts). A delta of +0.50 means the option moves $0.50 for each $1.00 move in the underlying. Deep ITM options have delta near ±1.0; far OTM options have delta near 0; ATM options have delta near ±0.50.
