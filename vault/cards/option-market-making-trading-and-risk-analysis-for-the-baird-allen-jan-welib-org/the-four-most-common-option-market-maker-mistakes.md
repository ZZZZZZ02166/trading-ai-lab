# The Four Most Common Option Market Maker Mistakes

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 186–186

## Summary

The four most frequent mistakes by option market makers are: (1) Failure to hedge delta — taking directional 'shots' instead of immediately hedging; (2) Incorrect delta hedging — hedging at stale or posted board prices rather than actual traded prices; (3) Overtrading — trading too many contracts to reduce per-trade edge; (4) Poor kappa/vega risk adjustment — allowing net kappa to become too large in one direction. Each of these can turn edge into loss over time.
