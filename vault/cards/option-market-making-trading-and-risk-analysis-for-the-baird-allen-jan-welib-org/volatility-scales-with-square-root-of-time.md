# Volatility Scales with Square Root of Time

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 38–39

## Summary

The T^(1/2) rule: volatility scales with the square root of the number of time periods. To convert annual volatility to a shorter period, divide by √(periods per year): daily vol = annual vol / √250, weekly vol = annual vol / √52, monthly vol = annual vol / √12. This square-root-of-time relationship means volatility over longer horizons grows slower than linearly — a 1-month vol is not 2× a 2-week vol, but √2 times.
