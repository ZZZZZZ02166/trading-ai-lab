# What is an Option Market Maker

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 10–13

## Summary

An option market maker (also called a dealer or scalper) is a trader who stands ready at all times to buy at a bid price or sell at an offer price whatever is being traded. Market makers provide the service of immediacy — ensuring a ready and liquid market for publicly brokered orders. Without them, prices would be more volatile and erratic because buy-and-sell orders are rarely evenly matched at any given time.
