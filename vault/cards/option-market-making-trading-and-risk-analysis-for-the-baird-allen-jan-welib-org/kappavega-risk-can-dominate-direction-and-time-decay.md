# Kappa/Vega Risk Can Dominate Direction and Time Decay

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 57–58

## Summary

A 10-point rise in implied volatility for a 60-day $500/point futures option position can create $800+ swings in position value — often larger than delta or theta effects over the same period. Kappa risk must be treated separately from delta risk because implied vol can move independent of futures price. During market events (earnings, economic data, crises), IV can jump 10-20 points within hours, creating massive kappa gains or losses.
