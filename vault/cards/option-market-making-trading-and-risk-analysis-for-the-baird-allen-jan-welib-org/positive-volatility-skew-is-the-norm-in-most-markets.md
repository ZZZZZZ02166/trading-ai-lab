# Positive Volatility Skew is the Norm in Most Markets

**Type:** market_structure_insight
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 59–59

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 3.3] Most futures and financial option markets exhibit positive skew — OTM wing options trade at higher implied volatility than ATM options. This reflects market participants' preference for long out-of-the-money options (lottery tickets), demand for protective puts, and empirical fat-tail probability. Negative skew (wings below ATM IV) is rare and usually temporary. Understanding skew direction helps market makers price wings correctly.
