# Time Delta and Time Kappa Are Calendar Spread-Specific Risks

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 60–61

## Summary

Time delta risk arises because futures prices for different contract months can move by different amounts (the basis can change). A long front-month / short back-month call spread has time delta risk if the front-month futures rallies more than the back-month. Time kappa risk arises when front-month and back-month implied volatilities move differently. Calendar spreads can be virtuously risk-neutral in single-month terms while having substantial time-based risks.
