# Options Markets as Risk-Shifting Insurance

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 14–15

## Summary

Futures and options markets do not create or destroy total wealth — they redistribute risk between parties who want to shed it and those willing to accept it. Commercial firms use futures to hedge price change risk, freeing capital for productive activity. Options extend this risk-shifting to futures markets themselves, allowing speculators and market makers to absorb unwanted investment risk from commercial hedgers.
