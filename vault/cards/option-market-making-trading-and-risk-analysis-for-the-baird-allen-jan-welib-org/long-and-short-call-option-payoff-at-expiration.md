# Long and Short Call Option Payoff at Expiration

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 19–20

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.1] A long 100 strike call purchased for $1.70 premium has a hockey-stick payoff at expiration: maximum loss of $1.70 below $100, breakeven at $101.70, and unlimited upside above. The short call is the mirror image: maximum profit of $1.70 below $100, breakeven at $101.70, and unlimited downside above. The premium paid/received is always the pivot point of the payoff.
