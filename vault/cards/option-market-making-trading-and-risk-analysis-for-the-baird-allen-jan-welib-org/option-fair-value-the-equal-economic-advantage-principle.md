# Option Fair Value: The Equal Economic Advantage Principle

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 23–24

## Summary

An option is fairly valued when trading at that price produces neither gain nor loss over the long run for either the buyer or seller. Fair value represents equal economic advantage for both sides of the trade — the price at which the option correctly compensates for the statistical probability of payoffs. Market makers use fair value as the center of their bid/offer spread rather than trading directionally.
