# Interest Rate Risk Can Be Significant for Non-Futures Options

**Type:** caution
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 59–59

## Summary

For stock options, a 10-percentage-point rise in interest rates can change a call's fair value by 20%+ for long-dated options. Futures option traders face this less directly, but spread traders holding positions across cash and futures markets must account for rho. During periods of extreme interest rate volatility (like the early 1980s), ignoring rho risk can produce large unexpected losses for options portfolios with long time to expiration.
