# Delta Changes with Price and Time to Expiration

**Type:** mechanism
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 50–51

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 3.1] Delta is not static — it changes as the underlying price moves and as time passes. As a call moves deeper ITM, its delta drifts toward +1.0; as it moves further OTM, delta drifts toward 0. Near expiration, delta becomes a step function (0 or 1 at the strike). At 60 days, the delta curve is a smooth S-shape; at expiration it is nearly binary. This delta drift (gamma) creates the need for continuous rehedging.
