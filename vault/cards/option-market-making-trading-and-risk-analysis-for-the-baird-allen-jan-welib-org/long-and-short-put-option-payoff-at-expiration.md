# Long and Short Put Option Payoff at Expiration

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 20–21

## Summary

[VISUAL: Figure 2.2] A long 100 strike put purchased for $1.70 has breakeven at $98.30 at expiration: maximum loss of $1.70 above $100 and increasing profit below $98.30. The short put mirrors this: maximum profit of $1.70 above $100, increasing loss below $98.30. Put/call payoff symmetry means long put + long call = long straddle, while short put + short call = short straddle.
