# Theta-Gamma Relationship: Time Decay Compensates for Gamma Risk

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 56–57

## Summary

For a long option position, gamma and theta have equal magnitude but opposite signs. A long call gains value from favorable price moves (positive gamma benefit) but loses value each day from time decay (negative theta cost). The 'break-even' relationship: the gamma benefit from a price move must exceed the daily theta cost for the position to profit. Short premium strategies earn theta at the cost of negative gamma exposure.
