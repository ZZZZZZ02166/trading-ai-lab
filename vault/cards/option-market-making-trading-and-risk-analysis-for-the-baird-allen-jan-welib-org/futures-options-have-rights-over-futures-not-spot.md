# Futures Options Have Rights Over Futures, Not Spot

**Type:** concept
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 45–46

## Summary

Options on futures give the holder the right to enter a futures contract — not the underlying spot commodity. This means futures options have unique arbitrage relationships with the futures term structure. Time spread options (options with different expirations) expose the market maker to both single-month risks AND the risk that implied volatility differences between months change (time kappa/vega risk).
