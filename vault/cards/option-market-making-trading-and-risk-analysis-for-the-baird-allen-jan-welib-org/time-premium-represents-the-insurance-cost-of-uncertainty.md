# Time Premium Represents the Insurance Cost of Uncertainty

**Type:** principle
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 22–22

## Summary

An option's time premium is essentially the market's price for the uncertainty about future price moves. The longer the time remaining, the higher the time premium, since there is more opportunity for an out-of-the-money option to move in-the-money. Option pricing models attempt to determine the fair value of this time premium using statistical models of price distributions and volatility estimates.
