# Cash vs Futures Options: Key Structural Difference

**Type:** definition
**Source:** Option Market Making — pp. 20–20

## Summary

A cash option gives the holder the right over the spot (cash) instrument itself. A futures option gives the holder rights over a futures contract. Both may trade simultaneously in the same market, creating cash/futures option arbitrage opportunities. When exercised, a futures call delivers a long futures position (not the underlying asset), which is an important distinction for market makers and traders managing expiration risk.
